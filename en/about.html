About

Holidays, festivals and travel seasons for 66 countries on one calendar.

What this site does

World Festival Calendar is a free travel calendar that puts the public holidays, festivals, sports events and best travel seasons of 66 countries on one calendar, so you can see at a glance where there is a festival this month and which days will be crowded because of local holidays.

Each country also lists top sights, nature, World Heritage sites and local experiences you can do all year round, with Japan broken down into its 47 prefectures.

Where the data comes from

  • Public holidays: official government calendars, compiled from the open-source holidays dataset and checked by hand.
  • Festivals and events: national tourism boards, organisers and official event sites; about 1533 entries so far.
  • World Heritage: the UNESCO World Heritage List, 370 sites.
  • Monthly picks (Chinese edition): Taiwanese tour operators' departures and travel platforms, each with a source link.

How it is kept up to date

Dates that follow the lunar or Islamic calendar, or that organisers announce each year, are first estimated from past years and marked "approx.". The site is checked every month and updated once official dates are out; events that are not held every year, such as the Olympics, are tracked separately.

Who makes it

The site is built and maintained by an independent developer who loves travelling. It takes no paid placements from tour operators or tourism boards and is supported by ads and reader donations on Ko-fi. The English edition is translated from the original Chinese edition.
