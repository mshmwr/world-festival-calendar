Brazil events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; big crowds after New Year's Eve on Copacabana.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–10 (approx.) Festival Salvador Carnival: Bahia's street carnival with music trucks, the largest by participants in the world.
    • Feb 5–10 Festival Rio Carnival: Samba school parades in the Sambadrome; tickets go on sale months ahead.

  3. March

    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 21 Public holiday Tiradentes Day: 21 April honours the independence hero Tiradentes.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 30 (approx.) Festival São Paulo Pride Parade: One of the largest Pride parades in the world.

  6. June

    • Jun 1–30 (approx.) Festival Festa Junina: Country-style festivals all through June; biggest in Campina Grande in the northeast.
    • Jun 24–Jul 25 Sports FIFA Women's World Cup: The first Women's World Cup in South America, hosted by eight cities including Rio and São Paulo.
    • Jun 26–28 (approx.) Festival Parintins Folklore Festival: A giant ox-puppet contest in a town on the Amazon.

  7. July

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Pantanal dry season: In the dry season animals gather at the water; the best chance to see jaguars.
    • Jul 28–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Paraty International Literary Festival (FLIP): International literary festival in the colonial town of Paraty.

  8. September

    • Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026) (approx.) Festival São Paulo Biennial: International contemporary art biennial in even years (next in 2028).
    • Sep 7 Public holiday Independence Day: Military parade in Brasília on 7 September.

  9. October

    • Oct 12 Public holiday Our Lady of Aparecida: 12 October honours Brazil's patron saint; it's also Children's Day.
    • Oct 24–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Natal Luz, Gramado: A three-month Christmas festival in a German-heritage town in the south.

  10. November

    • Nov 2 Public holiday All Souls' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 5–7 Sports F1 São Paulo Grand Prix: Formula 1 at Interlagos, South America's only F1 round.
    • Nov 15 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 20 Public holiday Black Consciousness Day: A national holiday since 2024.

  11. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
    • Dec 31 Festival New Year's Eve on Copacabana: Two million people dressed in white watch fireworks on the beach.

Coming up in future years
