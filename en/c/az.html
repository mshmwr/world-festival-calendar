Azerbaijan events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1–4 Public holiday New Year: New Year holidays.
    • Jan 20 Public holiday Black January (Martyrs' Day): Commemorates the 1990 crackdown by Soviet troops; people lay flowers at the Alley of Martyrs.

  2. March

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 20–26 Public holiday Novruz: Azerbaijan's most important holiday, about a week off, with bonfires, fire-jumping and sweet stalls in Baku's old city.

  3. April

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.

  4. May

    • May 9–10 Public holiday Victory in Europe Day: Public holiday.
    • May 16–18 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • May 28 Public holiday Independence Day: Public holiday on 28 May.

  5. June

    • Jun 15 Public holiday National Salvation Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 26–28 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 20–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Gabala International Music Festival: Classical music festival in the Gabala mountains in the north.

  7. September

    • Sep 24–26 Sports F1 Azerbaijan Grand Prix (Baku): Formula 1 on a street circuit beside Baku's old city, racing on Saturday; it skirts the city walls and has a very long straight.

  8. October

    • Oct 15–25 (approx.) Festival Baku Jazz Festival: Baku's international jazz festival in a city with a jazz pedigree.
    • Oct 25–27 (approx.) Festival Pomegranate Festival, Goychay: Pomegranate harvest festival in Goychay, with markets and tastings.

  9. November

    • Nov 8 Public holiday Victory Day: Commemorates the 2020 Karabakh war; public holiday.
    • Nov 9 Public holiday Flag Day: Celebrations at National Flag Square in Baku.

  10. December

    • Dec 15–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Baku New Year lights: New Year lights and markets along Baku Boulevard and Fountain Square.
    • Dec 31 Public holiday Solidarity Day of World Azerbaijanis: 31 December runs into the New Year holidays for a long break.
