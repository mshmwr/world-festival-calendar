Greece events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Crosses are thrown into the sea at harbours across the country.
    • Jan 17–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Patras Carnival: Greece's biggest carnival, climaxing on the weekend before Lent.

  2. March

    • Mar 15 Public holiday Clean Monday: First day of Orthodox Lent: kite-flying and picnics nationwide.
    • Mar 25 Public holiday Independence Day: Military parade in Athens on 25 March.

  3. April

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: Greece's most important holiday, with a candlelit midnight service.
    • Apr 30–May 3 (approx.) Festival Orthodox Easter in Corfu: On Holy Saturday morning, clay pots are thrown from balconies into the streets: Greece's most distinctive Easter.

  4. May

    • May 1–4 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.

  5. June

    • Jun 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Athens Epidaurus Festival: Summer performances at the Odeon of Herodes Atticus and the ancient theatre of Epidaurus.
    • Jun 21 Public holiday Whit Monday: Public holiday.

  6. August

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Dormition of the Mother of God: 15 August is the peak of Greek holidays; island ferries and rooms are hardest to book.
    • Aug 15 Festival Feast of the Virgin, Tinos: Big pilgrimages and festivities on Tinos and Paros.

  7. October

    • Oct 28 Public holiday Ohi Day: National Day on 28 October, with student parades.

  8. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.
    • Nov 4–14 (approx.) Festival Thessaloniki International Film Festival: Greece's most important film festival.
    • Nov 14 (approx.) Festival Athens Marathon (the Authentic): The original route from the town of Marathon to Athens' Panathenaic Stadium.

  9. December

    • Dec 1–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas & New Year (Syntagma Square, Athens): Christmas tree on Syntagma Square and city lights; on 6 January (Epiphany) crosses are thrown into the harbours.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.
