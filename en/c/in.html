India events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 8–14 (approx.) Festival International Kite Festival, Gujarat: International kite festival on the riverfront in Ahmedabad; the whole city flies kites on 14 January.
    • Jan 14–15 (approx.) Festival Harvest festivals (Pongal, Makar Sankranti): South India's harvest festival; Gujarat holds its International Kite Festival at the same time.
    • Jan 21–25 (approx.) Festival Jaipur Literature Festival: The world's largest free literary festival, held in Jaipur, often during Golden Triangle tours.
    • Jan 26 Public holiday Republic Day: Military parade along Kartavya Path, New Delhi.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Goa Carnival: Goa's street carnival before Lent, with floats and music.
    • Feb 18–20 (approx.) Festival Desert Festival, Jaisalmer: Camel races, pageants and folk performances in a Rajasthan desert city.

  3. March

    • Mar 6 Public holiday Maha Shivaratri: Hindu festival with all-night pilgrimages to holy sites such as Varanasi.
    • Mar 10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: National holiday; the area around Delhi's Jama Masjid is lively.
    • Mar 22 Festival Holi: Coloured-powder revelry; most traditional in Mathura and Vrindavan.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: National holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 13–14 (approx.) Festival Baisakhi: Punjab's New Year harvest festival; liveliest at the Golden Temple in Amritsar.
    • Apr 14 Public holiday Ambedkar Jayanti: National holiday.
    • Apr 15 Public holiday Ram Navami: Hindu festival.
    • Apr 18 Public holiday Mahavir Jayanti: Jain festival.

  5. May

    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: National holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Buddha Purnima: Pilgrimages and ceremonies at Bodh Gaya and Sarnath.

  6. June

    • Jun 16 (approx.) Public holiday Ashura: Islamic holiday.

  7. July

    • Jul 5 (approx.) Festival Rath Yatra, Puri: Huge chariot procession in Puri, Odisha, pulled by hundreds of thousands of devotees.

  8. August

    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Nehru Trophy Snake Boat Race, Kerala: Long snake-boat race on the Alappuzha backwaters, on the second Saturday of August.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Independence Day: The Prime Minister speaks at Delhi's Red Fort; roads nearby are closed.
    • Aug 15 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Islamic holiday.
    • Aug 25 Public holiday Janmashtami: Liveliest in Mathura and Vrindavan; Mumbai has human-pyramid pot-breaking (Dahi Handi).

  9. September

    • Sep 4–14 (approx.) Festival Ganesh Chaturthi: Biggest in Mumbai, where Ganesh idols are immersed in the sea on the last day.
    • Sep 12 (approx.) Festival Onam, Kerala: Kerala's biggest harvest festival, with flower carpets, feasts and snake-boat races.

  10. October

    • Oct 2 Public holiday Gandhi Jayanti: National holiday and a nationwide dry day.
    • Oct 4–8 (approx.) Festival Durga Puja, Kolkata: Kolkata's biggest festival: the city fills with artistic pandals, and idols are immersed in the river on the last day.
    • Oct 9 Public holiday Dussehra: Good triumphs over evil as demon effigies are burned; Mysuru's celebrations are the grandest.
    • Oct 15–19 (approx.) Festival Rajasthan International Folk Festival, Jodhpur: Folk music festival at Mehrangarh Fort in Jodhpur, around the full moon.
    • Oct 29 Public holiday Diwali: India's biggest festival, with lamps and fireworks nationwide.

  11. November

    • Nov 7–14 (approx.) Festival Pushkar Camel Fair: A desert fair in Rajasthan where tens of thousands of camels are traded.
    • Nov 14 Public holiday Guru Nanak Jayanti: The Golden Temple in Amritsar is lit up for the occasion.

  12. December

    • Dec 20–31 (approx.) Festival Christmas & New Year in Goa: Christmas Mass in Goa's churches and New Year beach parties; the priciest time to stay.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; liveliest in Goa.
