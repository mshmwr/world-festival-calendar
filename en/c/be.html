Belgium events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 7–9 Festival Carnival of Binche: UNESCO-listed: the Gilles clowns throw oranges into the crowd.
    • Feb 19–21 (approx.) Festival Salon du Chocolat Brussels: Chocolatier showcase with tastings.

  3. March

    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 (approx.) Festival Procession of the Holy Blood, Bruges: A religious procession on Ascension Day, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • May 16–17 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.
    • May 21–23 (approx.) Festival Brussels Jazz Weekend: Free jazz concerts across Brussels.
    • May 23 (approx.) Festival Doudou festival, Mons: A dragon-slaying ritual on Trinity Sunday, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  5. July

    • Jul 16–25 (approx.) Festival Ghent Festival (Gentse Feesten): Ten days of music and street festivities in Ghent's old town, one of Europe's biggest city festivals.
    • Jul 16–25 (approx.) Festival Tomorrowland: World-famous electronic music festival in Boom; tickets sell out globally in seconds.
    • Jul 21 Public holiday National Day: Military parade and fireworks in Brussels on 21 July.
    • Jul 23–25 Sports F1 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa): Formula 1 at Spa-Francorchamps in the Ardennes forest, where the weather is unpredictable.

  6. August

    • Aug 14–16 (2026) Festival Brussels Flower Carpet: Held every two years (even years): nearly a million begonias form a carpet.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  7. September

    • Sep 3–5 (approx.) Festival Belgian Beer Weekend, Brussels: Belgian beer festival on the Grand-Place.

  8. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Armistice Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 27–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Brussels Christmas Market: Winter Wonders Christmas market and ice rink.

  9. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

Coming up in future years

  • 2028: Aug 14–Aug 16 (provisional) Festival Brussels Flower Carpet: Held every two years: about half a million begonias carpet the Grand-Place in mid-August of even years.
