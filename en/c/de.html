Germany events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; shops close.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–9 Festival Cologne Carnival: The big Rose Monday parade; the whole city dresses up.
    • Feb 11–21 (approx.) Festival Berlinale: One of the world's top three film festivals; some screenings are open to the public.

  3. March

    • Mar 13–28 (approx.) Festival Easter markets: Easter egg markets in Nuremberg, Munich and elsewhere.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: Good Friday to Easter Monday; shops are closed on Sundays and holidays.

  4. April

    • Apr 30 Festival Walpurgis Night (Harz): Witches' night in the Harz mountains, with bonfires and costume parties.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday; shops close.
    • May 1 (approx.) Festival Rhein in Flammen: Fireworks and illuminations along the Rhine valley, several nights each summer.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday, also Germany's Father's Day.
    • May 17 Public holiday Whit Monday: Public holiday.

  6. June

    • Jun 5 (approx.) Festival Heidelberg Castle illuminations: The castle is lit up with fireworks three times each summer (June, July, September).
    • Jun 19–27 (approx.) Festival Kiel Week: The world's biggest sailing event, with parades and music in Kiel harbour.

  7. July

    • Jul 24 (approx.) Festival Berlin Pride (CSD): One of Europe's largest Pride parades.

  8. August

    • Aug 26–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Stuttgart Wine Village: Wine festival on Stuttgart's market square.

  9. September

    • Sep 18–Oct 3 Festival Oktoberfest, Munich: The world's biggest beer festival; book tent seats early.
    • Sep 26 (approx.) Sports Berlin Marathon: A flat course where the men's world record has been broken many times; finishes through the Brandenburg Gate.

  10. October

    • Oct 1–10 (approx.) Festival Festival of Lights, Berlin: Light projections on Berlin landmarks.
    • Oct 3 Public holiday German Unity Day: National Day on 3 October; shops close. Often near the last day of Oktoberfest.

  11. November

    • Nov 23–Dec 24 Festival Christmas markets: Nuremberg, Dresden and Cologne have the most famous markets.

  12. December

    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December; most Christmas markets end by the 23rd.
