China events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year's Day: New Year's holiday; popular sights and train tickets get tight.
    • Jan 5–Feb 28 Festival Harbin Ice and Snow Festival: Giant ice sculptures lit up at night at Ice and Snow World; bring full gear for -20°C.

  2. February

    • Feb 5–11 Public holiday Spring Festival (Lunar New Year): The Spring Festival travel rush; sights are crowded and some shops close.

  3. March

    • Mar 15–Apr 10 (approx.) Festival Shanghai Peach Blossom Festival: Peach orchards bloom around Nanhui in Pudong; a favourite spring outing.

  4. April

    • Apr 1–May 5 (approx.) Festival Luoyang Peony Festival: Peonies bloom at Wangcheng Park and the National Peony Garden, best in mid-April; pair it with the Longmen Grottoes.
    • Apr 5 Public holiday Qingming Festival: Short Qingming holiday; crowds heading home to sweep tombs and out on spring outings.
    • Apr 9–11 (approx.) Festival Guangxi Sanyuesan Song Festival: Zhuang song festival with antiphonal singing and firecracker-catching in Nanning and Wuming; a two-day holiday in Guangxi.
    • Apr 13–15 Festival Xishuangbanna Water-Splashing Festival: Dai New Year in Jinghong, with dragon-boat races, rocket launches and city-wide water fights.
    • Apr 16–18 Sports F1 Chinese Grand Prix (Shanghai): Formula 1 at the Shanghai International Circuit, reached by Metro Line 11.

  5. May

    • May 1–4 Public holiday May Day holiday: May Day Golden Week, one of the busiest domestic travel periods; sights cap visitors and prices rise.
    • May 15 (approx.) Sports Great Wall Marathon: A marathon on the Huangyaguan section of the Great Wall in Tianjin, famous for its steps.

  6. June

    • Jun 9 Public holiday Dragon Boat Festival: Short Dragon Boat holiday with boat races in many places.
    • Jun 11–20 (approx.) Festival Shanghai International Film Festival: China's only "A-category" international film festival, with many public screenings.

  7. July

    • Jul 17–Aug 10 (approx.) Festival Qingdao International Beer Festival: Asia's biggest beer festival, running for weeks at the Golden Beach beer city.

  8. September

    • Sep 11–19 Sports World Athletics Championships, Beijing: Held at Beijing's National Stadium (Bird's Nest); Beijing's second time hosting.
    • Sep 15 Public holiday Mid-Autumn Festival: Mid-Autumn holiday, often merged with National Day.
    • Sep 16–20 (approx.) Festival Qiantang River Tidal Bore Festival: The tidal bore peaks around the 18th of the 8th lunar month; watch the "single-line tide" at Yanguan, Haining.

  9. October

    • Oct 1–7 Public holiday National Day Golden Week: Peak national travel week; popular sights cap visitor numbers, so avoid it if you can.
    • Oct 1–Nov 30 Season Yangcheng Lake hairy crab season: Hairy crabs are at their best; restaurants in Shanghai and Suzhou serve crab banquets.
    • Oct 15–Nov 10 (approx.) Festival Fragrant Hills Red Leaves Festival, Beijing: The smoke trees of the Fragrant Hills turn red, best late October to early November; crowded at weekends.

  10. December

    • Dec 18–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Harbin Ice and Snow World: A giant ice-sculpture park by the Songhua River, opening around late December; most spectacular lit up at night.
