Iceland events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday after Reykjavík's city-wide New Year's Eve fireworks.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–7 (approx.) Festival Winter Lights Festival, Reykjavík: Reykjavík's winter light festival and Pool Night.

  3. March

    • Mar 1 Festival Beer Day: Marks the end of beer prohibition in 1989, celebrated in bars.
    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday.

  4. April

    • Apr 22 Public holiday First Day of Summer: The third Thursday of April, a traditional Icelandic holiday.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 1–Aug 15 Season Puffin season: Puffins can be seen in the Westfjords and the Westman Islands.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 16–17 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.
    • May 25–Jul 20 Season Midnight sun: Almost no darkness in summer; perfect for a Ring Road trip.

  6. June

    • Jun 6 (approx.) Festival Seamen's Day: Harbour festivities on the first Sunday of June.
    • Jun 17 Public holiday National Day: Parades and concerts in Reykjavík.

  7. July

    • Jul 30–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Þjóðhátíð, Westman Islands: A huge bonfire and music festival in the Westman Islands.

  8. August

    • Aug 2 Public holiday Commerce Day: First Monday in August; music festivals around the country over the long weekend.
    • Aug 21 (approx.) Festival Reykjavík Culture Night: A culture festival on the day of the Reykjavík Marathon, with fireworks at night.

  9. September

    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.

  10. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.
    • Nov 4–6 (approx.) Festival Iceland Airwaves: Reykjavík's music festival, a winter travel highlight.

  11. December

    • Dec 12–24 Festival Icelandic Christmas: the 13 Yule Lads: A Yule Lad arrives each of the 13 nights before Christmas, with events around the country.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December.
    • Dec 31 Festival Reykjavík New Year's Eve fireworks: Residents set off their own fireworks: the whole city lights up on New Year's Eve.
