Poland events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday with Three Kings parades.

  2. March

    • Mar 13–29 (approx.) Festival Kraków Easter market: Easter market and decorated eggs on the Main Market Square.
    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday; shops close.

  3. May

    • May 1–3 Public holiday May holidays (Labour Day & Constitution Day): 1–3 May long weekend, peak travel time for Poles.
    • May 15–Sep 30 Festival Chopin concerts, Warsaw: Free open-air Chopin recitals by the Chopin Monument in Łazienki Park every summer Sunday.
    • May 16 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.
    • May 27 Public holiday Corpus Christi: Public holiday with religious processions.

  4. June

    • Jun 25–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Jewish Culture Festival, Kraków: Jewish culture festival in Kazimierz, one of the largest in Europe.

  5. July

    • Jul 24–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival St Dominic's Fair, Gdańsk: A summer fair dating back to the 13th century; the old town fills with stalls.

  6. August

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  7. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Families return home to visit graves, and cemeteries glow with candles.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 11 November, with a parade in Warsaw.
    • Nov 27–Dec 26 (approx.) Festival Kraków Christmas market: Market on the Main Square and a nativity-crib (szopka) contest.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Warsaw Christmas market & lights: Lights along the Royal Route and the Old Town Square in Warsaw.

  8. December

    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Since 2025, 24 December is also a holiday; shops close.
