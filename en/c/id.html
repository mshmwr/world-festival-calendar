Indonesia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 5 Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Islamic holiday; public holiday.
    • Jan 13–23 Festival Galungan, Bali: A major Balinese festival every 210 days; every house raises a bamboo penjor, followed ten days later by Kuningan.

  2. February

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday, celebrated in Chinese communities.

  3. March

    • Mar 7 (approx.) Festival Ogoh-ogoh parade: On the eve of Nyepi, villages parade giant ogoh-ogoh effigies and then burn them; the next day the island falls silent.
    • Mar 8 (approx.) Public holiday Nyepi (Bali Day of Silence): The whole island goes silent for 24 hours: the airport closes and visitors must stay in; giant ogoh-ogoh effigies parade the night before.
    • Mar 10–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Nationwide travel home; transport is tight for a week either side.
    • Mar 26–28 Public holiday Easter holidays: Good Friday and Easter.

  4. April

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday, often with extra collective leave days around it; domestic transport is crowded.
    • May 20 (approx.) Festival Vesak sky lanterns at Borobudur: Monks walk from Mendut Temple to Borobudur, and sky lanterns are released at night.

  6. June

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Pancasila Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jun 13–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Bali Arts Festival: A month of dance and music at the Denpasar Arts Centre.

  7. July

    • Jul 1–Sep 30 (approx.) Season Toraja funeral season: The Toraja of Sulawesi hold their great funerals mostly in the dry season; visits can be arranged through local guides.
    • Jul 15–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival Bali Kite Festival: Giant traditional kite contests on Sanur beach.

  8. August

    • Aug 8–15 (approx.) Festival Sekaten, Yogyakarta: A traditional festival at Yogyakarta's palace for the Prophet's Birthday, with gamelan and a night market.
    • Aug 11–21 Festival Galungan, Bali: The second Galungan of 2027, followed ten days later by Kuningan.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 17 Public holiday Independence Day: Flag-raising ceremonies and games around the country.

  9. October

    • Oct 27–31 (approx.) Festival Ubud Writers & Readers Festival: International literary festival in Ubud, Bali, with talks, music and markets.

  10. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday, often with extra collective leave days around it.
