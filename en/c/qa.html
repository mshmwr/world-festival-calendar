Qatar events by month (2027)

  1. February

    • Feb 1–10 (approx.) Festival Qatar International Food Festival: International food festival in a Doha park.
    • Feb 8–Mar 12 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid events at Katara: Ramadan night market and Eid fireworks at Katara Cultural Village.
    • Feb 9 Public holiday National Sport Day: Second Tuesday of February, with runs and sports events nationwide.

  2. March

    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday; government offices usually close for longer.

  3. May

    • May 8–17 (approx.) Festival Doha International Book Fair: International book fair at the Doha Exhibition Center.
    • May 16–18 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.

  4. July

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Summer in Qatar: Summer is mostly indoors, with family activities and deals at malls and hotels.

  5. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.

  6. December

    • Dec 3–5 Sports F1 Qatar Grand Prix (Lusail): Formula 1's night race at Lusail International Circuit.
    • Dec 10–18 (approx.) Festival Qatar National Day week & Darb Al Saai: In the week before National Day, the heritage village at Umm Salal opens with camels and falcons.
    • Dec 18 Public holiday National Day: Parade and fireworks on the Corniche on 18 December, with flags all over the city.

Coming up in future years

  • 2030: Nov 4–Nov 19 (provisional) Sports Doha Asian Games: Doha's second Asian Games; the OCA is considering moving the Games to the year before the Olympics, which could push them to 2031.
