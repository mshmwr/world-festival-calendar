Italy events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday with parades of the witch Befana.
    • Jan 23–Feb 9 Festival Venice Carnival: Elaborate masks and costumes; St Mark's Square is the heart of it.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–22 (2026) Sports Milano Cortina Winter Olympics: The 25th Winter Olympics, spread across Milan, Cortina d'Ampezzo and other venues; the next will be in the French Alps in 2030.

  3. March

    • Mar 6–15 (2026) Sports Milano Cortina Winter Paralympics: The Winter Paralympics, held right after the Olympics.
    • Mar 28 Public holiday Easter (Vatican): The Pope celebrates Mass in St Peter's Square.
    • Mar 28 (approx.) Festival Scoppio del Carro, Florence: An antique cart packed with fireworks explodes outside the cathedral on Easter Sunday.

  4. April

    • Apr 13–18 (approx.) Festival Milan Design Week: The international furniture fair plus design shows across the city; hotel prices soar.
    • Apr 25 Public holiday Liberation Day: Public holiday on 25 April.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday with a big concert in Rome.
    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.

  6. June

    • Jun 2 Public holiday Republic Day: Military parade and air display in Rome on 2 June.
    • Jun 12–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Verona Opera Festival: Opera under the stars in Verona's Roman arena.

  7. July

    • Jul 2 Festival Palio di Siena: Bareback horse race on the Piazza del Campo, run again on 16 August.
    • Jul 17–18 (approx.) Festival Festa del Redentore, Venice: Fireworks over St Mark's Basin on the third weekend of July, with locals picnicking in boats.

  8. August

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Ferragosto (Assumption Day): Peak Italian holiday time: city shops often close and the beaches are packed.
    • Aug 16 Festival Palio di Siena: Riders from 17 city districts race bareback in just 90 seconds.

  9. September

    • Sep 1–11 (approx.) Festival Venice Film Festival: One of the world's top three film festivals, held on the Lido.
    • Sep 3–5 Sports F1 Italian Grand Prix (Monza): Formula 1 at Monza near Milan, Ferrari's home crowd; fans flood the track after the race.
    • Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Regata Storica, Venice: Historic regatta and parade on the Grand Canal on the first Sunday of September.

  10. October

    • Oct 4 Public holiday St Francis Day: A public holiday restored in 2026.
    • Oct 9–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Alba White Truffle Fair: White truffle fair in Alba, Piedmont, a highlight of autumn food travel.

  11. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 7 Public holiday National Unity Day: A public holiday in November.

  12. December

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday that officially opens the Christmas season.
    • Dec 8–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in Rome (Vatican midnight Mass): Christmas market on Piazza Navona, with the tree and nativity in St Peter's Square.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.

Coming up in future years
