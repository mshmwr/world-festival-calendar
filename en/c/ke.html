Kenya events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 1–Mar 15 Season Dry-season safari: The dry season in January–February concentrates animals near water; it's also calving season.

  2. March

    • Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  3. April

    • Apr 1–May 31 Season Long rains low season: The long rains: some camps close, roads are muddy and prices are at their lowest.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.

  5. June

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Madaraka Day: 1 June marks self-rule in 1963.

  6. July

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Great Migration: Wildebeest cross the Mara River, most spectacularly in August.

  7. October

    • Oct 10–11 Public holiday Mazingira Day: Public holiday on 10 October.
    • Oct 20 Public holiday Mashujaa Day: Public holiday on 20 October.

  8. November

    • Nov 1–Dec 15 Season Short rains season: Brief showers; safaris still run and prices are lower than in peak season.
    • Nov 19–22 (approx.) Festival Lamu Cultural Festival: Traditional Swahili dhow races and donkey races.

  9. December

    • Dec 12–13 Public holiday Jamhuri Day: National Day on 12 December.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December; safari lodges enter their year-end peak.
