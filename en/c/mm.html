Myanmar events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 4 Public holiday Independence Day: Marks independence from Britain in 1948.
    • Jan 8 Public holiday Karen New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jan 14–16 (approx.) Festival Naga New Year: Naga New Year in northern Sagaing, with warrior-costume dances; travel permits required.

  2. February

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday; Yangon's Chinatown is lively.
    • Feb 12 Public holiday Union Day: Public holiday.

  3. March

    • Mar 2 Public holiday Peasants' Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 22 Public holiday Full Moon of Tabaung: The pagoda festival, celebrated at Shwedagon.
    • Mar 27 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 13–21 Public holiday Thingyan water festival: Myanmar's New Year, with water-throwing nationwide.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Full Moon of Kason (Buddha Day): Devotees water the bodhi trees at the pagodas.

  6. July

    • Jul 18 Public holiday Full Moon of Waso (start of Buddhist Lent): Buddhist Lent begins.
    • Jul 19 Public holiday Martyrs' Day: Remembers the assassination of General Aung San.

  7. October

    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.
    • Oct 14–16 (approx.) Festival Thadingyut festival of lights: Celebrates the Buddha's return from heaven; temples and homes are lit up.
    • Oct 30 Public holiday Diwali: Public holiday.

  8. November

    • Nov 7–13 (approx.) Festival Taunggyi Balloon Festival: Hot-air balloons loaded with fireworks are launched at night.
    • Nov 13 Public holiday Tazaungdaing festival of lights: Coincides with the Taunggyi Balloon Festival; lights everywhere.
    • Nov 23 Public holiday National Day: Public holiday.

  9. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
