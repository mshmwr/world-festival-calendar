Mongolia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 7–9 (approx.) Public holiday Tsagaan Sar (Mongolian New Year): Mongolian lunar new year; families gather and some shops close.

  3. March

    • Mar 2–3 (approx.) Festival Khövsgöl Ice Festival: Ice sculptures, sledding and reindeer-herder traditions on frozen Lake Khövsgöl.
    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.

  4. May

    • May 20 Public holiday Buddha's Birthday: Ceremonies at monasteries; public holiday.

  5. June

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Children's Day: Public holiday with family events in Ulaanbaatar.

  6. July

    • Jul 10–15 Public holiday National Day (Naadam holidays): Holidays during Naadam; Ulaanbaatar hotels are at their priciest and hardest to book.
    • Jul 11–15 Festival Naadam Festival: Wrestling, horse racing and archery: Mongolia's biggest festival.

  7. October

    • Oct 3–4 (approx.) Festival Golden Eagle Festival: Kazakh eagle-hunting contest in Bayan-Ölgii in western Mongolia; popular with photo tours.

  8. November

    • Nov 26 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 29 Public holiday Genghis Khan's Birthday: A holiday set by the Mongolian calendar; the date changes every year.

  9. December

    • Dec 29 Public holiday Independence Day: Marks the 1911 declaration of independence.
