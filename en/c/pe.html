Peru events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 1–14 (approx.) Festival Virgen de la Candelaria, Puno: A huge religious dance festival in Puno on Lake Titicaca, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  3. March

    • Mar 5–14 (approx.) Festival Grape Harvest Festival, Ica: Grape harvest festival in Ica, the home of pisco.
    • Mar 25–28 Public holiday Holy Week: Religious processions in Cusco from Maundy Thursday to Easter; peak domestic travel.

  4. May

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season Machu Picchu dry season: The Inca Trail closes for maintenance every February.
    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.
    • May 23–26 (approx.) Festival Qoyllur Rit'i pilgrimage: A mass pilgrimage beneath an Andean glacier, held before Corpus Christi.
    • May 27 (approx.) Festival Corpus Christi, Cusco: Procession of saints' statues on Cusco's main square.

  5. June

    • Jun 7 Public holiday Flag Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 24 Festival Inti Raymi: A re-enactment of the Inca sun ceremony at Sacsayhuamán, Cusco.
    • Jun 29 Public holiday Saints Peter and Paul Day: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 23–Aug 8 Sports Pan American Games, Lima: The Americas' biggest multi-sport games, Lima's second time hosting.
    • Jul 28–29 Public holiday Fiestas Patrias (national holidays): National Day on 28–29 July, peak travel time for Peruvians; book Machu Picchu tickets even earlier.

  7. August

    • Aug 6 Public holiday Battle of Junín Day: Public holiday.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday Santa Rosa de Lima Day: Public holiday.

  8. October

    • Oct 8 Public holiday Battle of Angamos Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 18–28 (approx.) Festival Señor de los Milagros procession, Lima: A huge purple-clad religious procession in Lima in October.

  9. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.

  10. December

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 9 Public holiday Battle of Ayacucho Day: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
