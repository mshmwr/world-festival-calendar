Australia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; big crowds after Sydney's fireworks.
    • Jan 8–26 (approx.) Festival Sydney Festival: Sydney's summer arts festival with outdoor music and theatre.
    • Jan 17–31 (approx.) Sports Australian Open: Melbourne's Grand Slam, the first of the year.
    • Jan 26 Public holiday Australia Day: A regatta and fireworks on Sydney Harbour.

  2. February

    • Feb 19–Mar 6 (approx.) Festival Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras: The big parade along Oxford Street in Sydney.
    • Feb 19–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Adelaide Fringe: The world's second-largest fringe festival.

  3. March

    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A four-day weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; most shops close on Good Friday.
    • Mar 31–Apr 25 (approx.) Festival Melbourne International Comedy Festival: One of the world's three biggest comedy festivals.

  4. April

    • Apr 2–4 Sports F1 Australian Grand Prix (Melbourne): Formula 1 on the Albert Park street circuit in Melbourne; in 2027 it moves from early March to early April, and accommodation prices jump on race weekend.
    • Apr 25 Public holiday Anzac Day: Dawn services everywhere on 25 April; most shops are closed in the morning.

  5. May

    • May 21–Jun 12 (approx.) Festival Vivid Sydney: Light projections on the Opera House and light art around the harbour.

  6. June

    • Jun 11–22 (approx.) Festival Dark Mofo, Hobart: An alternative arts festival around the winter solstice in Tasmania.

  7. July

    • Jul 15–Oct 31 (approx.) Season Hervey Bay whale watching season: Humpback whales rest in Hervey Bay, Queensland, on their southern migration. July to October is the main season, with most whales in the bay from August to October. Sources: qld.gov.au, statements.qld.gov.au

  8. August

    • Aug 29 (approx.) Sports Sydney Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors, crossing the Harbour Bridge to finish at the Opera House.

  9. September

    • Sep 3–24 (approx.) Festival Brisbane Festival: Arts festival on the Brisbane River, opening with fireworks over the water.
    • Sep 11–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Floriade, Canberra: Canberra's spring flower show, one of the largest in the southern hemisphere.

  10. October

    • Oct 1–Nov 13 Sports Rugby World Cup: The first 24-team men's Rugby World Cup, opening in Perth with the final in Sydney.
    • Oct 22–Nov 8 (approx.) Festival Sculpture by the Sea, Bondi: A huge sculpture exhibition on the coastal walk from Bondi to Tamarama.

  11. November

    • Nov 2 Sports Melbourne Cup: "The race that stops a nation"; a holiday in Victoria.

  12. December

    • Dec 15–Jan 31 (approx.) Festival Tasmanian lavender season: Peak bloom at Bridestowe Lavender Estate.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: Midsummer; sales begin on the 26th, and the Sydney to Hobart race sets off that day.
    • Dec 26 Sports Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race: The classic yacht race that leaves Sydney Harbour on Boxing Day.
    • Dec 31 Festival Sydney New Year's Eve fireworks: One of the world's first big New Year's Eve fireworks shows.

Coming up in future years

  • 2032: Jul 23–Aug 8 Sports Brisbane Summer Olympics: Hosted by Brisbane and South East Queensland, Australia's third Summer Olympics.
