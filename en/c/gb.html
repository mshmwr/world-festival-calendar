United Kingdom events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year bank holiday; most shops keep shorter hours.
    • Jan 25 Festival Burns Night: Celebrates the Scottish poet Robert Burns, with haggis and poetry.

  2. February

    • Feb 7 (approx.) Festival Chinese New Year in London: A big Lunar New Year parade in Chinatown and Trafalgar Square, one of the largest outside Asia.

  3. March

    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A four-day weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; peak time for domestic trips.
    • Mar 27 (approx.) Sports The Boat Race (Oxford v Cambridge): The traditional rowing race on the Thames, watched by crowds along the banks.

  4. April

    • Apr 24–25 (approx.) Sports London Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors; from 2027 it runs over Saturday and Sunday.

  5. May

    • May 3 Public holiday Early May Bank Holiday: First Monday in May.
    • May 18–22 (approx.) Festival RHS Chelsea Flower Show: The Royal Horticultural Society's show, Britain's most important gardening event.
    • May 31 Public holiday Spring Bank Holiday: Last Monday in May.

  6. June

    • Jun 15–19 (approx.) Festival Royal Ascot: A royal race meeting where the hats steal the show.
    • Jun 23–27 (approx.) Festival Glastonbury Festival: Britain's biggest outdoor music festival; tickets sell out fast, and it takes a fallow year every few years.
    • Jun 28–Jul 11 Sports Wimbledon: The grass-court Grand Slam; queuing for same-day tickets is a tradition.

  7. July

    • Jul 2–4 Sports F1 British Grand Prix (Silverstone): Formula 1 at Silverstone, the birthplace of F1; often the same time as Wimbledon, so London hotels are harder to book.
    • Jul 23–Aug 2 (2026) Sports Commonwealth Games, Glasgow: Multi-sport games for 74 Commonwealth nations and territories, held in Glasgow, Scotland.

  8. August

    • Aug 6–30 Festival Edinburgh Festival Fringe: The world's largest arts festival, running alongside the Military Tattoo.
    • Aug 6–28 (approx.) Festival Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo: Night-time military music on the esplanade of Edinburgh Castle, during the Fringe.
    • Aug 29–30 Festival Notting Hill Carnival: Europe's biggest street carnival, with Caribbean-style parades.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday Summer Bank Holiday: Last Monday in August, the same day as Notting Hill Carnival.

  9. September

    • Sep 18–26 (approx.) Festival London Design Festival: Design shows and installations across the city.

  10. October

    • Oct 13–17 (approx.) Festival Frieze London: International contemporary art fair in Regent's Park, with gallery shows across the city.

  11. November

    • Nov 5 Festival Guy Fawkes Night: Fireworks and bonfires across Britain.
    • Nov 20–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Hyde Park Winter Wonderland: A Christmas fair in Hyde Park with a Ferris wheel, ice rink and markets.

  12. December

    • Dec 25–28 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: On 25 December public transport almost stops and shops close; the winter sales start on the 26th.
    • Dec 31 Festival London New Year's Eve fireworks: Fireworks around the London Eye on the Thames; tickets required.
