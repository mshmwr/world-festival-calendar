Morocco events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 11 Public holiday Proclamation of Independence Day: Public holiday.
    • Jan 14 Public holiday Amazigh New Year: A public holiday since 2024.
    • Jan 25–Feb 28 Season Almond blossom season (Tafraoute): Almond trees blossom in the Anti-Atlas.

  2. February

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: Most restaurants close by day and sights keep shorter hours.
    • Feb 8–Mar 10 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid (Fes, Marrakech): Many restaurants close by day during Ramadan; markets come alive after the evening iftar.

  3. March

    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 8–10 (approx.) Festival Kelaat M'Gouna Rose Festival: Harvest festival in the Valley of Roses.
    • May 16–17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Morocco's most important holiday; transport is crowded and many shops close for several days.

  5. June

    • Jun 4–12 (approx.) Festival Fes Festival of World Sacred Music: World sacred music festival in the old city of Fes.
    • Jun 4–6 (approx.) Festival Cherry Festival, Sefrou: Cherry festival in Sefrou in the Middle Atlas, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jun 24–26 (approx.) Festival Gnaoua World Music Festival, Essaouira: Gnaoua and world music festival in the Atlantic port of Essaouira.

  6. July

    • Jul 30 Public holiday Throne Day: 30 July marks the King's accession.

  7. August

    • Aug 14 Public holiday Oued Ed-Dahab Day: Public holiday.
    • Aug 14–15 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 20–21 Public holiday Revolution of the King and the People & Youth Day: Two holidays in a row on 20 and 21 August.

  8. October

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.
    • Oct 28–31 (approx.) Festival Date Festival, Erfoud: Date harvest festival in Erfoud, gateway to the Sahara.
    • Oct 31 Public holiday Unity Day: A public holiday added in 2025.

  9. November

    • Nov 6 Public holiday Green March Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 18 November.
    • Nov 26–Dec 4 (approx.) Festival Marrakech International Film Festival: North Africa's most important film festival.

Coming up in future years

  • 2030 (dates TBA) Sports FIFA World Cup (Spain, Portugal & Morocco): Co-hosted with Spain and Portugal, with Casablanca, Marrakech and other host cities; around June–July.
