Malaysia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 22 Festival Thaipusam at Batu Caves: The world's largest Thaipusam, with a million devotees climbing 272 steps.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–8 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Two days of Chinese New Year; Chinese-run shops close longer, with celebrations in Penang and Kuala Lumpur.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan bazaars: Ramadan bazaars open every evening; Kampung Baru in Kuala Lumpur is the liveliest.
    • Feb 14–20 (approx.) Festival Chinese New Year in George Town, Penang: New Year temple fairs and lion dances in George Town's heritage zone.
    • Feb 20 (approx.) Festival Chap Goh Mei orange throwing, Penang: At Penang's Esplanade, single women toss oranges into the sea hoping for a good match.

  3. March

    • Mar 10–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: End of Ramadan; Muslim families hold open houses and many travel home.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: National holiday.
    • May 17 Public holiday Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha): National holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Vesak Day: Buddhist holiday with float processions at temples.
    • May 30–31 Festival Kaamatan harvest festival, Sabah: The Kadazandusun thank the rice spirit and crown a harvest queen.

  5. June

    • Jun 1–2 Festival Gawai Dayak, Sarawak: The Dayak harvest festival, with longhouses open to visitors.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Islamic New Year: National holiday.
    • Jun 7 Public holiday Agong's Birthday: National holiday.
    • Jun 26–28 (approx.) Festival Rainforest World Music Festival, Kuching: World music festival at the Sarawak Cultural Village.

  6. July

    • Jul 1–10 (approx.) Festival Royal Floria Putrajaya: International flower festival in Putrajaya with floats and floral displays.

  7. August

    • Aug 1–31 (approx.) Festival George Town Festival: A whole August of arts, music and street performances in George Town, Penang.
    • Aug 15–16 Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): National holiday with parades.
    • Aug 31 Public holiday National Day: Parade at Merdeka Square, Kuala Lumpur.

  8. September

    • Sep 16 Public holiday Malaysia Day: Marks the formation of Malaysia in 1963.
    • Sep 30–Oct 8 (approx.) Festival Nine Emperor Gods Festival: From the 1st to the 9th of the 9th lunar month, Nine Emperor Gods temples in Penang and Kuala Lumpur hold vegetarian fasts, processions and fire-walking.

  9. October

    • Oct 28 Public holiday Deepavali: The Indian community's festival of lights, with decorations in Kuala Lumpur's Little India.

  10. November

    • Nov 1–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Malaysia Year-End Sale: Year-end sales season, with lights around Bukit Bintang in Kuala Lumpur.

  11. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Kuala Lumpur's malls go big on Christmas decorations.
