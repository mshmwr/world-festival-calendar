UAE events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday after the Burj Khalifa fireworks.

  2. February

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: No eating or drinking in public by day; restaurants keep shorter hours and iftar markets open at night.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan nights & iftars: Hotels host iftar and suhoor during Ramadan; avoid eating in public by day.

  3. March

    • Mar 1–31 (approx.) Festival Dubai Art Season: Dubai's art season (Art Dubai and more) is concentrated in March.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Holiday at the end of Ramadan, with mall promotions and fireworks.
    • Mar 27 (approx.) Sports Dubai World Cup (horse racing): One of the world's richest race days, at Meydan Racecourse.

  4. April

    • Apr 24–May 10 (approx.) Festival Dubai Food Festival: Restaurant deals and beach food events across Dubai.

  5. May

    • May 15–18 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday lasting several days.

  6. June

    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  7. July

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Dubai Summer Surprises: Summer sales season with family activities in the malls.

  8. August

    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  9. October

    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.

  10. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Nov 18–23 (approx.) Festival Abu Dhabi Art: Art fair on Saadiyat Island.

  11. December

    • Dec 2 Public holiday National Day: Fireworks and parades in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
    • Dec 5–Jan 11 (approx.) Festival Dubai Shopping Festival: City-wide sales, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Dec 10–12 Sports F1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix: The Formula 1 season finale at Yas Marina, starting at dusk; combine it with the Yas Island theme parks.
