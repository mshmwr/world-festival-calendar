France events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; most shops and some museums close.
    • Jan 13–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Winter sales (Soldes): France's official winter sales, with big discounts in department stores and boutiques.

  2. February

    • Feb 13–28 (approx.) Festival Nice Carnival & Menton Lemon Festival: Flower battles on floats and giant citrus sculptures.
    • Feb 23–Mar 3 (approx.) Festival Paris Fashion Week (autumn/winter): Autumn/winter womenswear shows; lots of fashion events and pricier hotels.

  3. March

    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 4 (approx.) Festival Paris Marathon: A city marathon down the Champs-Élysées and past the Arc de Triomphe.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: The only day by law when almost everything shuts: the Louvre and other museums and most shops close.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: A Thursday holiday, often bridged to make a four-day weekend.
    • May 8 Public holiday Victory in Europe Day: Public holiday.
    • May 11–22 (approx.) Festival Cannes Film Festival: International film festival with red-carpet glamour.
    • May 17 Public holiday Whit Monday: Public holiday.
    • May 23–Jun 6 (approx.) Sports French Open (Roland-Garros): The clay-court Grand Slam at Roland-Garros by the Bois de Boulogne.

  6. June

    • Jun 20–Jul 25 Season Provence lavender season: The fields of the Valensole plateau peak in early July and vanish after harvest.
    • Jun 21 Festival Fête de la Musique: Free street music on the summer solstice all over France; every corner of Paris has a gig.

  7. July

    • Jul 4–26 (approx.) Sports Tour de France: Finishes on the Champs-Élysées in Paris.
    • Jul 5–26 (approx.) Festival Festival d'Avignon: One of the world's biggest theatre festivals, in the Palais des Papes and theatres across Avignon.
    • Jul 8–Aug 20 (approx.) Festival Paris Plages: The banks of the Seine become artificial beaches and leisure areas.
    • Jul 14 Public holiday Bastille Day: Military parade on the Champs-Élysées and fireworks at the Eiffel Tower.

  8. August

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: 15 August, peak French holiday time.

  9. September

    • Sep 10–Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Bordeaux grape harvest: Harvest time at the châteaux; some offer hands-on visits.

  10. October

    • Oct 2 (approx.) Festival Nuit Blanche, Paris: An all-night contemporary art event in Paris, with museums and public spaces open free.

  11. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday during the school autumn break.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Armistice Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 (approx.) Festival Beaujolais Nouveau Day: The new wine is released on the third Thursday of November, celebrated in bars everywhere.
    • Nov 27–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Strasbourg Christmas Market: France's oldest Christmas market and a must on Alsace Christmas tours.

  12. December

    • Dec 5–8 Festival Fête des Lumières, Lyon: Light projections across the city's buildings, free over four nights.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Most shops and restaurants close.

Coming up in future years
