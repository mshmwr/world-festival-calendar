South Korea events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: A one-day New Year holiday; most sights stay open.
    • Jan 9–Feb 1 (approx.) Festival Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival: Ice fishing and catching trout by hand, Gangwon's signature winter event.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–9 Public holiday Seollal (Lunar New Year): Lunar New Year holidays; most shops close and transport is crowded.

  3. March

    • Mar 1 Public holiday Independence Movement Day: Commemorates the 1919 March First independence movement, with events nationwide.
    • Mar 27–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival: Korea's biggest cherry blossom festival; Yeojwacheon's Romance Bridge is the classic photo spot.

  4. April

    • Apr 3–9 (approx.) Festival Yeouido Cherry Blossom Festival: 1,800 cherry trees line Gukhoe-daero in Seoul.
    • Apr 4–12 (approx.) Festival Jeju Canola Flower Festival: Canola and cherry blossoms bloom together along Noksan-ro in Gasiri.

  5. May

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Labour Day: A public holiday from 2026; businesses close.
    • May 5 Public holiday Children's Day: The busiest day at theme parks, zoos and family attractions.
    • May 13 Festival Lotus Lantern Festival (Buddha's Birthday): Lantern parade along Jongno in Seoul; Jogyesa Temple is hung with lotus lanterns.

  6. June

    • Jun 3 (2026) Public holiday Local election day: A holiday for the national election; some shops stay open.
    • Jun 5–12 (approx.) Festival Gangneung Danoje Festival: On UNESCO's intangible heritage list: shamanic rites, mask plays, swinging and wrestling by the Namdaecheon in Gangneung.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Memorial Day: Honours those who died for the nation; sirens sound for a minute's silence at 10 a.m.

  7. July

    • Jul 1–5 (approx.) Festival Daegu Chimac Festival: Fried chicken and beer festival at Duryu Park in Daegu, Korea's summer favourite.
    • Jul 17–26 (approx.) Festival Boryeong Mud Festival: Mud fights on Daecheon Beach; the liveliest outdoor event of summer.
    • Jul 17–19 Public holiday Constitution Day: Restored as a public holiday from 2026.

  8. August

    • Aug 6–8 (approx.) Festival Incheon Pentaport Rock Festival: Korea's largest outdoor rock festival, at Songdo Moonlight Festival Park in Incheon.
    • Aug 15–16 Public holiday Liberation Day: Marks liberation from Japanese rule in 1945; flags fly everywhere.

  9. September

    • Sep 14–16 Public holiday Chuseok: One of Korea's biggest holidays; Gyeongbokgung and other palaces often open free.
    • Sep 25–Oct 4 (approx.) Festival Andong International Mask Dance Festival: Traditional Hahoe mask dances plus mask performances from around the world.

  10. October

    • Oct 1–15 (approx.) Festival Jinju Namgang Lantern Festival: Tens of thousands of lanterns float on the Namgang River beneath Jinju Fortress.
    • Oct 1–10 (approx.) Festival Busan International Film Festival: One of Asia's most important film festivals, centred on the Busan Cinema Center in Haeundae.
    • Oct 3 (approx.) Festival Seoul International Fireworks Festival: A million people watch from Yeouido Hangang Park.
    • Oct 3–4 Public holiday National Foundation Day: Celebrates the founding of Korea by Dangun.
    • Oct 9–11 Public holiday Hangul Day: Celebrates King Sejong's creation of Hangul, with events at Gwanghwamun.
    • Oct 10–Nov 15 Season Autumn leaves season: Seoraksan turns first in mid-October; Naejangsan peaks in early November.

  11. November

    • Nov 1–17 (approx.) Festival Seoul Lantern Festival: The whole length of Cheonggyecheon is hung with themed lanterns.
    • Nov 1–Jan 31 (approx.) Season Jeju tangerine picking season: Tangerine picking on Jeju farms mostly runs from November to January (some farms October to February), with the ripest fruit from mid-November. Most need booking and close in rain. Sources: wikitree.co.kr, jeju.com
    • Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Busan Fireworks Festival: Fireworks over the sea in front of Gwangan Bridge; book viewing seats early.

  12. December

    • Dec 12–31 (approx.) Festival Seoul Christmas Market, Gwanghwamun: A winter market and lights on Gwanghwamun Square, best after dark.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: Christmas lights are best in Myeongdong and the department stores; book restaurants ahead.
