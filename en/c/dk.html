Denmark events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 26–29 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Fashion Week: Nordic fashion week.

  2. February

    • Feb 5–28 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Light Festival: Winter light art festival.

  3. March

    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday; most shops close.

  4. May

    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 16–17 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.

  5. June

    • Jun 23 Festival Midsummer bonfires (Sankthans): Midsummer bonfires by the sea where a witch effigy is burned.
    • Jun 27–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Roskilde Festival: The biggest music festival in the Nordics.

  6. July

    • Jul 2–11 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Jazz Festival: Over a hundred jazz gigs across the city.

  7. August

    • Aug 20–29 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Cooking festival: A Nordic food festival.
    • Aug 27–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Aarhus Festival: Denmark's biggest city culture festival.

  8. October

    • Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Culture Night: Hundreds of museums and churches open late into the night.

  9. November

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Tivoli Christmas season: Christmas lights at the century-old amusement park.

  10. December

    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December; 25 and 26 December are holidays.
