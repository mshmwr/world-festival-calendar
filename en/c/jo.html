Jordan events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Jan 5 (approx.) Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Public holiday.

  2. March

    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Best time to visit: Mild spring weather, good for hiking in Petra.
    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Spring in Jordan: The most comfortable season, ideal for Wadi Rum and hiking in Petra.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.

  3. April

    • Apr 9 (approx.) Festival Dead Sea Ultra Marathon: Runs from Amman down to the Dead Sea, finishing at the lowest point on Earth.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 15–18 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • May 25 Public holiday Independence Day: Parades and fireworks in Amman.

  5. June

    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 22–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Jerash Festival of Culture and Arts: Music and folk performances in Jerash's Roman city; Jordan's biggest cultural festival.

  7. August

    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  8. September

    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Autumn in Jordan: Cool autumn weather; the Red Sea at Aqaba is still warm enough to swim.

  9. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
