Canada events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: National holiday.
    • Jan 29–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Québec Winter Carnival: Ice palace, snow sculptures and an ice canoe race.
    • Jan 29–Feb 15 (approx.) Festival Winterlude, Ottawa: The Rideau Canal becomes the world's largest naturally frozen skating rink, with ice sculptures.

  2. March

    • Mar 1–Apr 15 Festival Québec sugar shack season: Maple brunches and maple taffy on snow at a cabane à sucre.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: National holiday; Easter Monday is not a holiday in most provinces.

  3. May

    • May 7–17 (approx.) Festival Canadian Tulip Festival, Ottawa: Over a million tulips in Ottawa, a gift that began with the Dutch royal family.
    • May 21–23 Sports F1 Canadian Grand Prix (Montréal): Formula 1 at Circuit Gilles Villeneuve on Île Notre-Dame, with big events in the city.

  4. June

    • Jun 12–Jul 7 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (Toronto & Vancouver): Canada hosts 13 matches in Toronto and Vancouver.
    • Jun 25–Jul 3 (approx.) Festival Festival International de Jazz de Montréal: One of the world's biggest jazz festivals, with lots of free outdoor shows.

  5. July

    • Jul 1 Public holiday National Day: Celebrations on Parliament Hill in Ottawa.
    • Jul 9–18 Festival Calgary Stampede: The world's biggest rodeo and parade.

  6. August

    • Aug 5–8 (approx.) Festival Fêtes de la Nouvelle-France, Québec: Old Québec recreates colonial life of the 17th and 18th centuries.

  7. September

    • Sep 6 Public holiday Labour Day: First Monday of September.
    • Sep 9–19 (approx.) Festival Toronto International Film Festival: One of North America's most important film festivals.
    • Sep 20–Oct 20 Season Autumn leaves season: The Maple Leaf Route from Québec to Niagara is at its reddest.

  8. October

    • Oct 15–Nov 20 Season Churchill polar bear season: Polar bears gather on the shore at Churchill, Manitoba, waiting for the sea to freeze.
    • Oct 30–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Banff Mountain Film Festival: International mountain and adventure film festival in Banff.

  9. November

    • Nov 14–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Winter Festival of Lights, Niagara Falls: The falls are lit up at night with light displays.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.
    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Vancouver Christmas Market: A German-style Christmas market next to the Vancouver Convention Centre.

  10. December

    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; Boxing Day on the 26th is a big sales day.
