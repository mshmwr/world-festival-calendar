Russia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1–8 Public holiday New Year holidays: Russia's longest holiday, usually running to 8 January.
    • Jan 7 Public holiday Orthodox Christmas: Midnight services in the churches.

  2. February

    • Feb 23 Public holiday Defender of the Fatherland Day: Public holiday.

  3. March

    • Mar 1–15 (approx.) Festival Lake Baikal ice festival: Lake Baikal's frozen season, with on-ice activities and ice caves.
    • Mar 8–14 Festival Maslenitsa: A week of pancakes to see off winter, ending with the burning of a straw effigy.
    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 25–May 9 (approx.) Festival Moscow Easter festival: Easter egg installations and fairs around Orthodox Easter (2 May in 2027).

  5. May

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Spring and Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 9–10 Public holiday Victory Day: Victory Day parade on Red Square on 9 May; central Moscow roads are closed.

  6. June

    • Jun 12–14 Public holiday Russia Day: National Day.
    • Jun 19 (approx.) Festival Sabantuy, Kazan: Tatar summer farming festival with wrestling, pole-climbing and other traditional contests.
    • Jun 27 (approx.) Festival White Nights & Scarlet Sails, St Petersburg: Scarlet-sailed ship and fireworks on the Neva during the White Nights.

  7. September

    • Sep 4–5 (approx.) Festival Moscow City Day: Concerts and fireworks across the city on the first weekend of September.

  8. November

    • Nov 4 Public holiday Unity Day: Public holiday.

  9. December

    • Dec 1–Jan 15 (approx.) Festival Moscow New Year lights: Big light displays and New Year fairs around Red Square and Tverskaya Street.
