New Zealand events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1–4 Public holiday New Year: Holidays on 1 and 2 January.

  2. February

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Waitangi Day: New Zealand's national day, with Māori cultural events at Waitangi.
    • Feb 25–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Aotearoa New Zealand Festival of the Arts, Wellington: New Zealand's largest international arts festival, with theatre, dance and music in the capital.
    • Feb 26–28 (approx.) Festival Auckland Lantern Festival: Auckland's Lunar New Year lantern festival.

  3. March

    • Mar 13 (approx.) Festival Hokitika Wildfoods Festival: A quirky food festival of "wild foods" like insects.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; most shops close on Good Friday.

  4. April

    • Apr 25–26 Public holiday Anzac Day: Dawn services everywhere on the morning of 25 April.

  5. June

    • Jun 7 Public holiday King's Birthday: First Monday of June, around the start of the ski season.
    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.
    • Jun 25–28 (approx.) Festival Queenstown Winter Festival: Celebrations to open the ski season.
    • Jun 25 Public holiday Matariki (Māori New Year): The rising of the Matariki star cluster, a public holiday since 2022.

  6. September

    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Spring in New Zealand (lambs & blossoms): Lambs are born on the farms and Christchurch's gardens bloom.
    • Sep 23–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival World of WearableArt, Wellington: An international wearable-art design competition and stage show.

  7. October

    • Oct 25 Public holiday Labour Day: Fourth Monday of October.

  8. December

    • Dec 15–Feb 28 Season Peak summer season: Peak season for South Island hikes and lakes; book accommodation early.
    • Dec 25–28 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: Midsummer, with sales from 26 December.
    • Dec 31 Festival Auckland New Year's Eve (Sky Tower fireworks): New Year's Eve fireworks from the Sky Tower.
