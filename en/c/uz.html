Uzbekistan events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year holidays.

  2. March

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr (Ramazon Hayit): Public holiday.
    • Mar 18–24 (approx.) Festival Navruz fairs: Around Navruz, Samarkand and Bukhara cook the traditional sweet sumalak, with fairs.
    • Mar 21–22 Public holiday Navruz: Spring-equinox new year, with traditional fairs and shows in Samarkand and Bukhara.

  3. May

    • May 9–10 Public holiday Day of Remembrance and Honour: WWII memorial day on 9 May.
    • May 16–17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha (Qurbon Hayit): Public holiday.
    • May 27–30 (approx.) Festival Silk and Spices Festival, Bukhara: Crafts and traditional performances in old Bukhara.

  4. August

    • Aug 15–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Melon season (Kovun Sayli): Late-summer melons and grapes; markets are at their most abundant.
    • Aug 25–30 (approx.) Festival Sharq Taronalari music festival: International music festival on Samarkand's Registan, held every two years (odd years).

  5. September

    • Sep 1 Public holiday Independence Day: Celebrations and fireworks in Tashkent.

  6. October

    • Oct 1 Public holiday Teachers' Day: Public holiday.

  7. December

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.
