Maldives events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 8 (approx.) Public holiday First day of Ramadan: Resorts operate as normal during Ramadan; most restaurants in Malé close during the day.

  3. March

    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • May 15–19 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday lasting several days.

  5. June

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 26 Public holiday Independence Day: Parades and fireworks in Malé.

  7. August

    • Aug 3 (approx.) Public holiday National Day: Commemorates the expulsion of the Portuguese; set by the Islamic calendar.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  8. September

    • Sep 2 (approx.) Public holiday Day the Maldives Embraced Islam: A public holiday set by the Islamic calendar.

  9. November

    • Nov 3 Public holiday Victory Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.

  10. December

    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.
