Spain events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Three Kings' Day: Three Kings parades on the evening of 5 January; 6 January is a holiday.
    • Jan 20 Festival Tamborrada, San Sebastián: The city drums for 24 hours to honour its patron saint.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–14 (approx.) Festival Cádiz Carnival: Spain's liveliest carnival, famous for its satirical songs.

  3. March

    • Mar 15–19 Festival Las Fallas, Valencia: Giant figures are all burned on the final night.
    • Mar 21–28 Festival Semana Santa (Holy Week): The religious processions in Seville and Málaga are the most spectacular.

  4. April

    • Apr 11–17 (approx.) Festival Seville April Fair: Flamenco dresses, casetas and horse-drawn parades.
    • Apr 23 Festival Sant Jordi (books & roses), Barcelona: Barcelona's day of love: La Rambla fills with book stalls and roses.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 3–16 (approx.) Festival Córdoba Patios Festival: Residents open their flower-filled courtyards to visitors; on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  6. June

    • Jun 12–14 (2026) Sports F1 Spanish Grand Prix (Barcelona): Formula 1 at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya; in 2027 the Spanish round moves to Madrid.
    • Jun 23 Festival Night of San Juan: Solstice bonfires with all-night fireworks on the beaches of Barcelona and Alicante.
    • Jun 29 Festival Haro Wine Battle: A red-wine battle in the Rioja town of Haro.

  7. July

    • Jul 6–14 Festival San Fermín (Running of the Bulls), Pamplona: San Fermín, with bull runs at 8 a.m. each morning.

  8. August

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday; when it falls on a Sunday some regions move it to Monday.
    • Aug 25 Festival La Tomatina, Buñol: Last Wednesday of August; tickets required.

  9. September

    • Sep 10–12 Sports F1 Madrid Grand Prix: Formula 1 on Madrid's new IFEMA street circuit, from 2026.
    • Sep 20–24 (approx.) Festival La Mercè, Barcelona: Barcelona's biggest festival, with human towers, giants and fire runs.

  10. October

    • Oct 8–16 (approx.) Festival Fiestas del Pilar, Zaragoza: A big festival around National Day, with flower offerings to the Virgin of the Pillar.
    • Oct 12 Public holiday National Day (Fiesta Nacional): Military parade in Madrid on 12 October.

  11. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Madrid Christmas markets & lights: Christmas market on Plaza Mayor and a parade on the eve of Three Kings.

  12. December

    • Dec 6–8 Public holiday December long weekend (Constitution Day & Immaculate Conception): Holidays on 6 and 8 December, often bridged into a long weekend (puente).
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

Coming up in future years

  • 2030 (dates TBA) Sports FIFA World Cup (Spain, Portugal & Morocco): Co-hosted by Spain, Portugal and Morocco, with three centenary matches first in Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay; around June–July.
