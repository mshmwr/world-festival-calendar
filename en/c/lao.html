Laos events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 19–21 (approx.) Festival Wat Phou Festival: A Buddhist festival at Wat Phou in Champasak (World Heritage), with full-moon lights and a traditional fair.

  3. March

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 14–16 Public holiday Lao New Year (Pi Mai): Luang Prabang's water festival is the most traditional, with a beauty pageant and sand stupas.

  5. May

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 8–10 (approx.) Festival Boun Bang Fai rocket festival: A pre-monsoon rain-making festival where villages launch home-made bamboo rockets.

  6. July

    • Jul 18 (approx.) Festival Khao Phansa: Buddhist Lent begins, with candles and offerings to monks at the temples.

  7. October

    • Oct 15–16 (approx.) Festival Boun Ok Phansa & boat racing: The end of Buddhist Lent, with floating lanterns on the Mekong and boat races in Vientiane.

  8. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.
    • Nov 11–13 (approx.) Festival That Luang Festival: The most important Buddhist festival, at That Luang in Vientiane.

  9. December

    • Dec 2 Public holiday National Day: Public holiday on 2 December.
