Argentina events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires Carnival (murgas): Murga music and dance shows in neighbourhood streets.
    • Feb 8–9 Public holiday Carnival holidays: Carnival parades everywhere on the Monday and Tuesday before Lent.

  3. March

    • Mar 1–8 (approx.) Festival Vendimia (grape harvest festival), Mendoza: Parades and a harvest queen in the wine region.
    • Mar 1–May 31 Season Best time for Iguazú Falls: Plenty of water in autumn, cooler weather and fewer crowds.
    • Mar 24 Public holiday Day of Remembrance for Truth and Justice: 24 March remembers the victims of the military dictatorship, often bridged into a long weekend.
    • Mar 25–26 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter.

  4. April

    • Apr 2 Public holiday Malvinas Veterans' Day: Public holiday on 2 April.
    • Apr 22–May 10 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires International Book Fair: One of the largest book fairs in the Spanish-speaking world.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 19–23 (approx.) Festival arteBA art week, Buenos Aires: The arteBA art fair and gallery week.
    • May 25 Public holiday May Revolution Day: Public holiday on 25 May.

  6. June

    • Jun 20–21 Public holiday Flag Day & General Güemes Day: Two memorial holidays in June, often making a long weekend.

  7. July

    • Jul 9 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 9 July.

  8. August

    • Aug 13–26 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires Tango Festival: The Tango World Championship, with lots of free shows.
    • Aug 16 Public holiday General San Martín Day: A public holiday in August.

  9. October

    • Oct 1–11 (approx.) Festival Oktoberfest, Villa General Belgrano: Oktoberfest in a German-heritage town.
    • Oct 11 Public holiday Day of Respect for Cultural Diversity: A public holiday in October.

  10. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.
    • Nov 6–14 (approx.) Festival Fiesta de la Tradición (gaucho festival), San Antonio de Areco: A celebration of gaucho traditions, with Argentine cowboy shows.
    • Nov 9 (2026) Public holiday Papal visit holiday: A special holiday declared for the visit of Pope Leo XIV.
    • Nov 11–20 (approx.) Festival Mar del Plata International Film Festival: Latin America's only "A-category" international film festival.
    • Nov 20 Public holiday National Sovereignty Day: A public holiday in November.

  11. December

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
