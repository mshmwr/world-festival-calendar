Czechia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year & Restoration Day of the Czech State: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Masopust carnival: Traditional costumed processions before Lent, most authentic in the countryside.

  3. March

    • Mar 13–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival Prague Easter markets: Easter markets on Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: Public holiday, with an Easter market on Prague's Old Town Square.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 8 Public holiday Liberation Day: WWII Victory Day.
    • May 12–Jun 3 Festival Prague Spring International Music Festival: Opens on 12 May, the anniversary of Smetana's death, with Má vlast.
    • May 28–30 (approx.) Festival Prague Food Festival: Food festival in the Royal Garden of Prague Castle.

  5. June

    • Jun 19–21 (approx.) Festival Festival of the Five-Petalled Rose, Český Krumlov: The town dresses in medieval costume for jousting and markets.

  6. July

    • Jul 2–10 (approx.) Festival Karlovy Vary International Film Festival: International film festival in the Czech spa town.
    • Jul 5–6 Public holiday Saints Cyril & Methodius Day & Jan Hus Day: Two public holidays in a row.

  7. September

    • Sep 28 Public holiday Czech Statehood Day (St Wenceslas): Public holiday.

  8. October

    • Oct 2–3 (approx.) Festival Pilsner Fest: Beer festival in the birthplace of pilsner.
    • Oct 14–17 (approx.) Festival Signal Festival, Prague: Light art festival across Prague.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 28 October.

  9. November

    • Nov 17 Public holiday Struggle for Freedom and Democracy Day: Marks the 1989 Velvet Revolution.
    • Nov 28–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Prague Christmas markets: A giant Christmas tree and market on Old Town Square.

  10. December

    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of the 24th; most Christmas markets stay open into early January.
