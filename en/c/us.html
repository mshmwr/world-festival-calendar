United States events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Festival Rose Parade: A century-old tradition in Pasadena, California.
    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: Federal holiday; many sights close or shorten hours on New Year's Day.
    • Jan 18 Public holiday Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Third Monday of January; federal holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 9 Festival Mardi Gras, New Orleans: Parades and bead-throwing in the French Quarter.
    • Feb 14 Sports Super Bowl: The NFL championship game, in the San Francisco Bay Area in 2026 and at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles in 2027; the halftime show is America's event of the year.
    • Feb 15 Public holiday Presidents' Day: Third Monday of February, with mall sales and busy ski resorts.

  3. March

    • Mar 13 (approx.) Festival St Patrick's Day (Chicago River dyed green): Chicago dyes its river green and parades on the Saturday before 17 March; New York's Fifth Avenue parade is on the 17th.
    • Mar 20–Apr 12 (approx.) Festival National Cherry Blossom Festival, Washington DC: Cherry trees bloom around the Tidal Basin on the National Mall, with a Japanese culture parade.

  4. April

    • Apr 9–18 (approx.) Festival Coachella: A huge music and arts festival in the California desert over two weekends.
    • Apr 19 Sports Boston Marathon: The world's oldest city marathon, on the third Monday of April (Patriots' Day); qualifying times required.
    • Apr 23–May 2 (approx.) Festival New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival: Jazz and heritage music festival at the New Orleans Fair Grounds.
    • Apr 30–May 2 Sports F1 Miami Grand Prix: Formula 1 on a circuit around Hard Rock Stadium, with a big party atmosphere.

  5. May

    • May 1 (approx.) Sports Kentucky Derby: America's most famous horse race, on the first Saturday of May.
    • May 31 Public holiday Memorial Day: Last Monday of May; summer travel season begins and national parks get busier.

  6. June

    • Jun 11–Jul 19 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (USA, Canada & Mexico): The first 48-team World Cup, hosted by 16 cities: 11 in the US plus Canada and Mexico; the final is in New York/New Jersey.
    • Jun 18–19 Public holiday Juneteenth: Federal holiday marking the end of slavery.
    • Jun 27 (approx.) Festival NYC Pride March: The big march on the last Sunday of June, with rainbow flags all over the city.

  7. July

    • Jul 4 Public holiday Independence Day: Fireworks nationwide; Macy's show in New York is the biggest.
    • Jul 22–25 (approx.) Festival San Diego Comic-Con: The world's biggest comics and pop culture convention.

  8. August

    • Aug 29–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Burning Man: A large arts community gathering in Nevada's Black Rock Desert; tickets are limited.
    • Aug 30–Sep 12 (approx.) Sports US Open (tennis): The final Grand Slam of the year at Flushing Meadows, New York; the night sessions are electric.

  9. September

    • Sep 1–30 (approx.) Festival Aloha Festivals, Honolulu: Hawaiian culture festival with floral parades and block parties.
    • Sep 6 Public holiday Labour Day: First Monday of September, the last long weekend of summer.

  10. October

    • Oct 2–10 Festival Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta: The world's largest balloon festival, with hundreds launching at once.
    • Oct 10 (approx.) Sports Chicago Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors, on a flat, fast course.
    • Oct 11 Public holiday Columbus Day (Indigenous Peoples' Day): Second Monday of October; not observed in some states.
    • Oct 22–24 Sports F1 United States Grand Prix (Austin): Formula 1 at the Circuit of the Americas in Texas, with big concerts over the weekend.
    • Oct 31 Festival Halloween: New York's Village Halloween Parade in Greenwich Village.

  11. November

    • Nov 7 (approx.) Sports New York City Marathon: The world's largest marathon, through all five boroughs with huge crowds.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Veterans Day: Federal holiday; shops stay open.
    • Nov 18–20 Sports F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix: Formula 1 late on Saturday night on the Las Vegas Strip; hotels along the track are at their priciest.
    • Nov 25 Public holiday Thanksgiving & Macy's Parade: Macy's giant-balloon parade in New York; Black Friday follows the next day.
    • Nov 30–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in New York (Rockefeller Center tree): The Rockefeller Center tree lighting, Fifth Avenue windows and skating rinks.

  12. December

    • Dec 24–25 Public holiday Christmas: Most shops and sights close on 25 December; the Rockefeller Center tree in New York draws the biggest crowds.
    • Dec 31 Festival Times Square New Year's Eve: The ball-drop countdown; stake out a spot hours in advance.

Coming up in future years

  • 2028: Jul 14–Jul 30 Sports Los Angeles Summer Olympics: Los Angeles hosts the Summer Olympics for the third time, with some events elsewhere in California and in Oklahoma City.
  • 2028: Aug 15–Aug 27 (provisional) Sports Los Angeles Paralympics: The Summer Paralympics, held after the Olympics.
  • 2031 (dates TBA) Sports Rugby World Cup (USA): The first men's Rugby World Cup held in the United States.
  • 2031 (dates TBA) Sports FIFA Women's World Cup (USA, Mexico & co-hosts): Co-hosted by the US, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica.
  • 2034 (dates TBA) Sports Salt Lake City Winter Olympics: Salt Lake City's second Winter Olympics, around February.
