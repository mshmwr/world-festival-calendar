Romania events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1–2 Public holiday New Year: Holidays on 1 and 2 January.
    • Jan 6–7 Public holiday Epiphany & St John's Day: Two public holidays in a row.
    • Jan 24 Public holiday Union of the Romanian Principalities Day: Public holiday.

  2. March

    • Mar 1 Festival Mărțișor: The tradition of giving red-and-white charms to welcome spring, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  3. April

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: Romania's most important holiday, with a long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday, often merging with Easter or a weekend into a long break.
    • May 9 (approx.) Festival Junii Brașovului parade: A horseback parade on the first Sunday after Orthodox Easter.

  5. June

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Children's Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 4–13 (approx.) Festival Transilvania International Film Festival: International film festival in Cluj.
    • Jun 18–27 (approx.) Festival Sibiu International Theatre Festival: One of the world's three biggest performing arts festivals, with lots of street shows in Sibiu's old town.
    • Jun 20–21 Public holiday Orthodox Pentecost: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 24–26 (approx.) Festival Sighișoara Medieval Festival: Medieval carnival in Dracula's hometown citadel.

  7. August

    • Aug 5–8 (approx.) Festival Untold Festival, Cluj: Romania's biggest music festival.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Dormition of the Mother of God: Public holiday.

  8. October

    • Oct 31 Festival Halloween at Bran Castle: Halloween party at Dracula's castle.

  9. November

    • Nov 27–Dec 27 (approx.) Festival Bucharest Christmas Market: Christmas market on Constitution Square.
    • Nov 30–Dec 1 Public holiday St Andrew's Day & National Day: Holidays on 30 November and 1 December, with a military parade in Bucharest.

  10. December

    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.
