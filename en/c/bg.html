Bulgaria events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 29–31 (approx.) Festival Kukeri festival: Mask festival in Pernik, where huge fur-covered masks drive away evil spirits.

  2. February

    • Feb 14 Festival Trifon Zarezan (vine-pruning day): A traditional day of pruning the vines and praying for a good harvest.

  3. March

    • Mar 3 Public holiday Liberation Day: National Day on 3 March.

  4. April

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  5. May

    • May 1–4 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 Public holiday St George's Day (Army Day): Public holiday.
    • May 15–Jun 10 Festival Rose Valley harvest: Roses are picked at dawn in the Kazanlak valley; the Rose Festival is in early June.
    • May 24 Public holiday Day of Slavonic Literacy and Culture: Public holiday with student parades.
    • May 30–Jun 7 (approx.) Festival Rose Festival: Rose picking and parades in the Kazanlak Rose Valley on the first weekend of June.

  6. June

    • Jun 1–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season: Summer evening performances in the Roman theatre.

  7. July

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Varna Summer International Music Festival: Summer classical music festival in the Black Sea city of Varna.

  8. September

    • Sep 6 Public holiday Unification Day: Public holiday.
    • Sep 22 Public holiday Independence Day: Public holiday.

  9. December

    • Dec 1–31 (approx.) Festival Sofia Christmas Market: Christmas market in central Sofia.
    • Dec 24–28 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays from 24 to 26 December.
