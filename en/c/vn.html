Vietnam events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: A one-day New Year holiday.
    • Jan 30–Feb 8 (approx.) Festival Nguyen Hue Tết flower street, Ho Chi Minh City: Before Tết, Nguyen Hue walking street becomes a huge flower street.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–10 Public holiday Tết (Lunar New Year): Vietnam's biggest holiday; many shops close for a week or more.
    • Feb 11–Apr 20 (approx.) Festival Perfume Pagoda Festival: The pilgrimage season at the Perfume Pagoda outside Hanoi, reached by small boats.
    • Feb 20 (approx.) Festival Hoi An Lantern Festival (first full moon): On the 14th of each lunar month Hoi An turns off its lights for lanterns and river candles; biggest at the first full moon.

  3. April

    • Apr 16 (approx.) Public holiday Hung Kings' Commemoration: On the 10th of the 3rd lunar month, honouring the nation's founding kings; a public holiday.
    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Reunification Day & Labour Day holidays: A 30 April–1 May long weekend, one of the busiest domestic travel periods; beach towns raise prices.

  4. June

    • Jun 1–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Da Nang International Fireworks Festival: International fireworks teams compete over the Han River on Saturday nights.
    • Jun 1–6 (approx.) Festival Hue Festival: A big festival in even years; odd years host the smaller Hue Traditional Craft Festival.

  5. August

    • Aug 16 (approx.) Festival Ghost Festival (Vu Lan): On the 15th of the 7th lunar month, with temple ceremonies and floating lanterns.

  6. September

    • Sep 1–Oct 10 Season Golden rice season, Sapa & Mu Cang Chai: The terraces turn golden before the harvest.
    • Sep 2 Public holiday National Day: Celebrations at Ba Dinh Square in Hanoi; busy holiday travel.
    • Sep 15 Festival Hoi An Mid-Autumn Lantern Festival: Lanterns fill the old town and floating lanterns drift on the Thu Bon River.

  7. October

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.

  8. November

    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.
    • Nov 24 Public holiday Vietnam Culture Day: A public holiday added in 2025.

  9. December

    • Dec 20–25 (approx.) Festival Da Lat Flower Festival: Da Lat's flower festival is held about every two years in December, with flower floats and displays.
