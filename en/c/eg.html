Egypt events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 7 Public holiday Coptic Christmas: 7 January, Christmas for Egypt's Coptic Christians.
    • Jan 27–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Cairo International Book Fair: One of the largest book fairs in the Middle East.
    • Jan 28 Public holiday January 25 Revolution & Police Day: Public holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan lanterns & night markets, Cairo: During Ramadan Cairo's streets are hung with fanous lanterns and the old-town night markets run late.
    • Feb 22 Festival Abu Simbel Sun Festival: At dawn the sun lights the statue of Ramesses II deep inside the temple; it happens again on 22 October.

  3. March

    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • Mar 15–Jun 15 Season Red Sea diving season: Water temperatures rise and visibility is good at Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh.

  4. April

    • Apr 29 Public holiday Sinai Liberation Day: Public holiday.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 3 Public holiday Sham el-Nessim: The day after Coptic Easter, when everyone goes out for picnics; sights are crowded.
    • May 15–18 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: A public holiday lasting several days; peak domestic travel.

  6. June

    • Jun 10 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  7. July

    • Jul 1 Public holiday June 30 Revolution Day: Public holiday.
    • Jul 23 Public holiday July 23 Revolution Day: National Day.

  8. August

    • Aug 7–22 Festival Coptic Feast of the Virgin: The Coptic fast of the Virgin, with pilgrimages to monasteries.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Mawlid at Al-Hussein Mosque, Cairo: Festivities around Cairo's Al-Hussein Mosque for the Prophet's Birthday, with traditional sweets.

  9. October

    • Oct 7 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: 6 October marks the 1973 war.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.
    • Oct 22 Festival Abu Simbel Sun Festival: A solar phenomenon that happens twice a year.

  10. November

    • Nov 12–21 (approx.) Festival Cairo International Film Festival: The oldest international film festival in the Middle East.
