Hong Kong events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day public holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 6–7 Festival Lunar New Year Night Parade & fireworks: Night parade of floats in Tsim Sha Tsui, and fireworks over the harbour on the second day.
    • Feb 6–9 Public holiday Lunar New Year holidays: Days 1–3 of the new year are holidays; many small shops reopen only after day 4, but big malls stay open.

  3. March

    • Mar 25–27 (approx.) Festival Art Basel Hong Kong: Asia's biggest art fair at the Wan Chai convention centre, with gallery events around town.
    • Mar 26–28 (approx.) Sports Hong Kong Sevens: Held at the Kai Tak main stadium, famous for its fancy-dress party in the stands.
    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: The Easter long weekend is peak time for Hongkongers travelling abroad.

  4. April

    • Apr 5 Public holiday Qingming Festival: Public holiday; traffic controls near cemeteries.
    • Apr 29 (approx.) Festival Tin Hau Festival: On the 23rd of the 3rd lunar month, with flower-cannon processions in Yuen Long and at Joss House Bay.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 13 Festival Cheung Chau Bun Festival: Piu Sik parade on Cheung Chau and the midnight bun-scrambling race.
    • May 13 Public holiday Buddha's Birthday: Same day as the Cheung Chau Bun Festival; expect long queues for the Cheung Chau ferry.

  6. June

    • Jun 9 Public holiday Dragon Boat Festival: Dragon-boat races in many districts; Stanley and Tai O are the liveliest.
    • Jun 9 (approx.) Sports Stanley International Dragon Boat Championships: Dragon-boat racing at Stanley Main Beach on Dragon Boat Festival, with a lively atmosphere.

  7. July

    • Jul 1 Public holiday HKSAR Establishment Day: Fireworks or celebrations over Victoria Harbour on 1 July.
    • Jul 14–20 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Book Fair: A huge book fair at the Wan Chai convention centre; crowded during school holidays.

  8. August

    • Aug 2–30 (approx.) Festival Yu Lan Ghost Festival of the Chiu Chow community: Rituals and Chinese opera in bamboo theatres during the 7th lunar month; on the national intangible heritage list.

  9. September

    • Sep 14–16 Festival Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance: Over three Mid-Autumn nights, a dragon studded with incense sticks winds through Tai Hang.
    • Sep 16 Public holiday Day after Mid-Autumn Festival: Big lantern displays in Victoria Park and elsewhere on Mid-Autumn night; the next day is a holiday.

  10. October

    • Oct 1 Public holiday National Day: Usually fireworks over Victoria Harbour on the evening of 1 October.
    • Oct 8 Public holiday Chung Yeung Festival: Public holiday; people climb hills and visit ancestral graves.
    • Oct 21–24 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Wine & Dine Festival: A big food and wine event on the Central harbourfront.

  11. December

    • Dec 12–Jan 4 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Brands and Products Expo: Lunar New Year goods and local brands at Victoria Park.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas holidays: Christmas lights are best in Tsim Sha Tsui and Central; book restaurants ahead.
    • Dec 31 Festival Victoria Harbour New Year's Eve fireworks: A huge countdown fireworks show on both sides of Victoria Harbour.
