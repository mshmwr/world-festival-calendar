Hungary events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–9 (approx.) Festival Busójárás, Mohács: Scary wooden masks chase away winter before Lent; on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  3. March

    • Mar 15 Public holiday 1848 Revolution Day: Public holiday on 15 March with commemorations in Budapest.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 1–25 (approx.) Festival Budapest Spring Festival: Budapest's spring arts festival.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 14–16 (approx.) Festival Gourmet Festival, Budapest: A food festival of Michelin restaurants and Hungarian wineries in a Budapest park.
    • May 16–17 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 30–Aug 1 Sports F1 Hungarian Grand Prix: Formula 1 at the Hungaroring near Budapest; easy to combine with the city.

  7. August

    • Aug 10–15 (approx.) Festival Sziget Festival: One of Europe's big music festivals, on an island in the Danube.
    • Aug 20 Public holiday St Stephen's Day (national day): A big fireworks show over the Danube.

  8. September

    • Sep 9–12 (approx.) Festival Budapest Wine Festival: Wine festival in Buda Castle.

  9. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 13–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Budapest Christmas markets: Light show in front of St Stephen's Basilica.

  10. December

    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December.
