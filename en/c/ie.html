Ireland events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 27–31 (approx.) Festival TradFest Dublin: Irish traditional music in Temple Bar and at Dublin Castle.

  2. February

    • Feb 1 Public holiday St Brigid's Day: First Monday in February (or 1 February if it's a Friday); a holiday added in 2023.

  3. March

    • Mar 15–18 Festival St Patrick's Festival parade, Dublin: Dublin's five-day festival, with the main parade on 17 March and the city turned green.
    • Mar 17 Public holiday St Patrick's Day: Dublin goes green for its parade: Ireland's national day.
    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: Public holiday.

  4. May

    • May 3 Public holiday May Bank Holiday: First Monday in May.

  5. June

    • Jun 7 Public holiday June Bank Holiday: First Monday in June.
    • Jun 16 Festival Bloomsday, Dublin: Celebrates Joyce's Ulysses; people in 1904 dress retrace the book's route.

  6. July

    • Jul 12–25 (approx.) Festival Galway International Arts Festival: The west of Ireland's biggest arts festival, with street performances and parades.
    • Jul 26–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Galway Races: Ireland's liveliest week of racing.

  7. August

    • Aug 2 Public holiday August Bank Holiday: First Monday in August.

  8. September

    • Sep 1–30 (approx.) Festival Lisdoonvarna Matchmaking Festival: Traditional matchmaking festival in a western village, with live music and dancing.

  9. October

    • Oct 24–31 (approx.) Festival Halloween where it began (Derry & Meath): Halloween comes from the Celtic Samhain; Derry and County Meath hold big Halloween festivals.
    • Oct 25 Public holiday October Bank Holiday: Last Monday in October.

  10. November

    • Nov 25–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Dublin Christmas markets & lights: Lights on Grafton Street and Christmas markets.

  11. December

    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas & St Stephen's Day: On 25 December public transport almost stops and shops close.
    • Dec 29–31 (approx.) Festival New Year's Festival Dublin: New Year's Eve concerts and light projections.
