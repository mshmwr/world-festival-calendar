Norway events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 21 Festival Sun Day (Tromsø): The day the sun returns after the polar night, celebrated across northern Norway.
    • Jan 22–31 (approx.) Festival Northern Lights Festival, Tromsø: A music festival in the Arctic city, during aurora season.

  2. February

    • Feb 1–Apr 30 Festival Lofoten cod season: Cod-fishing season in Lofoten, with drying racks full of stockfish.

  3. March

    • Mar 5–7 (approx.) Festival Holmenkollen Ski Festival: Classic ski-jumping event in Oslo.
    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday; Norwegians head to the mountains to ski and city shops mostly close.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 16–17 Public holiday Pentecost: Public holiday.
    • May 17 Public holiday National Day: The whole country wears traditional dress for children's parades.
    • May 20–Jul 22 Season Midnight sun: 24-hour daylight north of the Arctic Circle.
    • May 26–Jun 9 (approx.) Festival Bergen International Festival: One of the largest performing arts festivals in the Nordics.

  5. June

    • Jun 23 Festival Midsummer bonfires (Sankthans): Bonfires by the sea for midsummer.

  6. August

    • Aug 10–14 (approx.) Festival Øya Festival, Oslo: A big music festival in an Oslo park.

  7. September

    • Sep 3–5 (approx.) Festival Bergen Food Festival: Food festival on Bergen's waterfront.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  8. November

    • Nov 15–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Pepperkakebyen (gingerbread town), Bergen: The world's largest gingerbread town.

  9. December

    • Dec 10 Festival Nobel Peace Prize ceremony, Oslo: Prize ceremony at Oslo City Hall, with a torchlight procession and concerts.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December; 25 and 26 December are holidays.
