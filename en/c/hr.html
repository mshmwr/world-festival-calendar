Croatia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday.
    • Jan 17–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Rijeka Carnival: Croatia's biggest carnival.

  2. February

    • Feb 3 Festival Feast of St Blaise, Dubrovnik: The city's patron saint's day, with a procession in the old town; on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  3. March

    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  4. May

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 27 Public holiday Corpus Christi: Public holiday.
    • May 30 Public holiday National Day: Public holiday on 30 May.

  5. June

    • Jun 22 Public holiday Anti-Fascist Struggle Day: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 9–11 (approx.) Festival Ultra Europe, Split: A big electronic music festival in Split, followed by a week of boat parties.
    • Jul 10–Aug 25 Festival Dubrovnik Summer Festival: Theatre and music in the old town.
    • Jul 14–Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Split Summer Festival: Opera and theatre in Diocletian's Palace.

  7. August

    • Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Sinjska Alka: A 300-year-old knights' ring-tilting contest, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • Aug 5 Public holiday Victory and Homeland Thanksgiving Day: Public holiday on 5 August.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday at the very peak of summer season.

  8. September

    • Sep 15–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Truffle season (Istria): White truffle season in Istria, with a truffle festival in Motovun.

  9. November

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 Public holiday Vukovar Remembrance Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 28–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Advent in Zagreb: Repeatedly voted Europe's best Christmas market.

  10. December

    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.
