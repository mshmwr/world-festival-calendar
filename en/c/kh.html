Cambodia events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 7 Public holiday Victory over Genocide Day: Marks the fall of the Khmer Rouge regime in 1979.

  2. March

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 20–21 Festival Equinox sunrise at Angkor Wat (March): The sun rises right over the central tower; it happens again around the 22 September equinox.

  3. April

    • Apr 14–16 Public holiday Khmer New Year: New Year celebrations around Angkor, with water-splashing and traditional games.

  4. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 14 Public holiday King's Birthday: Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Visakha Bucha (Buddha's Birthday): Ceremonies at temples.
    • May 24 Public holiday Royal Ploughing Ceremony: The royal ploughing ceremony that forecasts the year's harvest.

  5. June

    • Jun 18 Public holiday Queen Mother's Birthday: Public holiday.

  6. September

    • Sep 21–22 Festival Equinox sunrise at Angkor Wat (September): The twice-yearly sight of the sun crowning the tower.
    • Sep 24 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.
    • Sep 29–Oct 1 (approx.) Public holiday Pchum Ben: A festival for the ancestors, with temple offerings; many people go home.

  7. October

    • Oct 15 Public holiday King Norodom Sihanouk Commemoration Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 29 Public holiday King's Coronation Day: Public holiday.

  8. November

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.
    • Nov 9 Public holiday Independence Day: Celebrations at the Independence Monument in Phnom Penh.
    • Nov 12–14 (approx.) Festival Water Festival (Bon Om Touk): Dragon-boat races on the Tonle Sap River in Phnom Penh: Cambodia's biggest festival.

  9. December

    • Dec 5 (approx.) Sports Angkor Wat International Half Marathon: A run among the temples of Angkor, held on the first Sunday of December.
    • Dec 29 Public holiday Peace Day: A public holiday added in 2020.
