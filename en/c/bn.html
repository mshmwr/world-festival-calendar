Brunei events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1–2 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  2. February

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday.
    • Feb 8 (approx.) Public holiday First day of Ramadan: Most restaurants are closed by day during Ramadan.
    • Feb 23 Public holiday National Day: Parade in Bandar Seri Begawan.
    • Feb 24 (approx.) Public holiday Nuzul Al-Quran: Public holiday.

  3. March

    • Mar 10–13 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • Mar 12–14 (approx.) Festival Istana Nurul Iman open house (Hari Raya): Istana Nurul Iman opens for three days and visitors can shake hands with the Sultan.

  4. May

    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • May 31 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.

  5. June

    • Jun 6–7 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  6. July

    • Jul 15 Public holiday Sultan's Birthday: Celebrations nationwide with parades and lights.

  7. August

    • Aug 15–16 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  8. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
    • Dec 26–27 (approx.) Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Public holiday.
