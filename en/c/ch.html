Switzerland events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 15–17 (approx.) Sports Lauberhorn ski races, Wengen: The longest downhill on the Ski World Cup, a winter highlight in the Jungfrau region.

  2. February

    • Feb 15–17 Festival Basel Fasnacht: The lights go out at 4 a.m. for the Morgestraich, opening three days of carnival.

  3. April

    • Apr 19 (approx.) Festival Sechseläuten, Zurich: A snowman effigy is burned; how fast its head explodes forecasts the summer.

  4. May

    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: National holiday.

  5. June

    • Jun 15–Sep 30 Season Alpine hiking season: High-altitude trails and lifts are fully open.
    • Jun 17–20 (approx.) Festival Art Basel: The world's most important modern and contemporary art fair.

  6. July

    • Jul 2–17 (approx.) Festival Montreux Jazz Festival: The legendary jazz festival on the shore of Lake Geneva.

  7. August

    • Aug 1 Public holiday National Day: Bonfires and fireworks everywhere; the display at the Rhine Falls is the most spectacular.
    • Aug 4–14 (approx.) Festival Locarno Film Festival: Open-air screenings on the Piazza Grande, one of the largest outdoor cinemas.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Zurich Street Parade: A huge techno parade along Lake Zurich.

  8. September

    • Sep 15–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Alpine cattle descent (Alpabzug): Cows wearing flower crowns and bells come down from the high pastures, with village markets.

  9. November

    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Zurich Christmas markets: The Swarovski Christmas tree and market inside Zurich main station.
    • Nov 22 (approx.) Festival Zibelemärit (onion market), Bern: Onion market on the fourth Monday of November; Bern's old town fills with braided onions.

  10. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; Easter, Boxing Day and other holidays are set by each canton, and most observe them.
