Singapore events by month (2027)

  1. January

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day public holiday.
    • Jan 22 Festival Thaipusam: Hindu devotees carry kavadi in procession from Little India to Tank Road.

  2. February

    • Feb 4–14 (approx.) Festival River Hongbao: Lunar New Year lantern displays and market at Marina Bay.
    • Feb 6–7 Public holiday Chinese New Year & Chingay Parade: Chinatown light-up and the Chingay parade.

  3. March

    • Mar 10 Public holiday Hari Raya Puasa (Eid al-Fitr): Ramadan bazaar and lights around Geylang Serai.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: Public holiday.

  4. April

    • Apr 10–11 (approx.) Sports Singapore Sevens: A World Rugby Sevens stop at the National Stadium.

  5. May

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 15–31 (approx.) Festival Singapore International Festival of Arts: Singapore's largest performing arts festival, with theatre and outdoor shows.
    • May 17 Public holiday Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha): Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Vesak Day: Lanterns and animal releases at temples; Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery is the busiest.

  6. June

    • Jun 1–Jul 31 (approx.) Festival Great Singapore Sale: Island-wide mall sales.

  7. July

    • Jul 15–31 (approx.) Festival Singapore Food Festival: Themed events at restaurants and markets across the island.

  8. August

    • Aug 9 Public holiday National Day: National Day Parade at Marina Bay with flypasts and fireworks.
    • Aug 21–29 (approx.) Festival Singapore Night Festival: Night Festival light projections in Bugis and the museum district.
    • Aug 22–Sep 20 (approx.) Festival Chinatown Mid-Autumn lights: The whole street is hung with themed lanterns, with mooncake markets.

  9. September

    • Sep 25–Oct 31 (approx.) Festival Deepavali lights, Little India: For over a month before Deepavali, Serangoon Road in Little India is strung with lights.

  10. October

    • Oct 8–10 Sports F1 Singapore Grand Prix (Marina Bay night race): Formula 1's Marina Bay night race; roads close downtown, hotel prices double and there are big concerts.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Deepavali: Little India is strung with lights for the Hindu festival of lights.

  11. November

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Christmas on Orchard Road: Christmas on A Great Street: 3 km of lights.

  12. December

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: The final peak of the Orchard Road lights; public holiday.
