Privacy policy (Chinese)

Dates marked "approx." are estimates based on past years or on the lunar or Islamic calendar; Islamic holidays can shift by a day depending on the moon sighting, and local festivals follow each organiser's announcement.

Regions follow the UN geoscheme: Central Europe covers Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary and Poland; Eastern Europe covers Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Romania, Bulgaria and the Baltic states; the Balkans are in Southern Europe.

World Heritage sites follow the UNESCO World Heritage List (as of the 47th session, 2025); large countries show selected sites only. The official list prevails.

Sources include official government holiday calendars, national tourism boards and festival organisers, the UNESCO World Heritage List and more. Translated from the Chinese edition.
