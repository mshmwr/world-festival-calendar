  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Sep 19–Oct 4 (2026) Sports Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games: The 20th Asian Games, centred on Nagoya; Japan's third time hosting. The next edition is in Doha, Qatar, in 2030.
    • Oct 11 Public holiday Sports Day: Second Monday of October.
    • Oct 18–24 (2026) Sports Aichi-Nagoya Asian Para Games: The Asian Para Games, held after the Asian Games.
    • Oct 20–Nov 30 Season Autumn leaves season: Starts in Hokkaido in early October; Kyoto is most colourful in mid-to-late November.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Sep 25–Oct 4 (approx.) Festival Andong International Mask Dance Festival: Traditional Hahoe mask dances plus mask performances from around the world.
    • Oct 1–15 (approx.) Festival Jinju Namgang Lantern Festival: Tens of thousands of lanterns float on the Namgang River beneath Jinju Fortress.
    • Oct 1–10 (approx.) Festival Busan International Film Festival: One of Asia's most important film festivals, centred on the Busan Cinema Center in Haeundae.
    • Oct 3 (approx.) Festival Seoul International Fireworks Festival: A million people watch from Yeouido Hangang Park.
    • Oct 3–4 Public holiday National Foundation Day: Celebrates the founding of Korea by Dangun.
    • Oct 9–11 Public holiday Hangul Day: Celebrates King Sejong's creation of Hangul, with events at Gwanghwamun.
    • Oct 10–Nov 15 Season Autumn leaves season: Seoraksan turns first in mid-October; Naejangsan peaks in early November.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Oct 1–7 Public holiday National Day Golden Week: Peak national travel week; popular sights cap visitor numbers, so avoid it if you can.
    • Oct 1–Nov 30 Season Yangcheng Lake hairy crab season: Hairy crabs are at their best; restaurants in Shanghai and Suzhou serve crab banquets.
    • Oct 15–Nov 10 (approx.) Festival Fragrant Hills Red Leaves Festival, Beijing: The smoke trees of the Fragrant Hills turn red, best late October to early November; crowded at weekends.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Oct 1–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Sun Moon Lake Fireworks Music Festival: Weekend fireworks with concerts on the shore of Sun Moon Lake.
    • Oct 10–11 Public holiday National Day: National Day long weekend, with a ceremony at the Presidential Office and fireworks in the evening.
    • Oct 25 Public holiday Taiwan Retrocession & Guningtou Victory Memorial Day: Restored as a public holiday from 2025.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Oct 1 Public holiday National Day: Usually fireworks over Victoria Harbour on the evening of 1 October.
    • Oct 8 Public holiday Chung Yeung Festival: Public holiday; people climb hills and visit ancestral graves.
    • Oct 21–24 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Wine & Dine Festival: A big food and wine event on the Central harbourfront.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Oct 3–4 (approx.) Festival Golden Eagle Festival: Kazakh eagle-hunting contest in Bayan-Ölgii in western Mongolia; popular with photo tours.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • Sep 30–Oct 8 (approx.) Festival Phuket Vegetarian Festival: From the 1st to the 9th of the 9th lunar month, devotees parade with face-piercing rituals.
    • Oct 13 Public holiday King Bhumibol Memorial Day: Commemorates the passing of the late King; public holiday.
    • Oct 23–25 Public holiday Chulalongkorn Day: Public holiday, with flowers laid at King Chulalongkorn's equestrian statue in Bangkok.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Sep 1–Oct 10 Season Golden rice season, Sapa & Mu Cang Chai: The terraces turn golden before the harvest.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Sep 25–Oct 31 (approx.) Festival Deepavali lights, Little India: For over a month before Deepavali, Serangoon Road in Little India is strung with lights.
    • Oct 8–10 Sports F1 Singapore Grand Prix (Marina Bay night race): Formula 1's Marina Bay night race; roads close downtown, hotel prices double and there are big concerts.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Deepavali: Little India is strung with lights for the Hindu festival of lights.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Sep 30–Oct 8 (approx.) Festival Nine Emperor Gods Festival: From the 1st to the 9th of the 9th lunar month, Nine Emperor Gods temples in Penang and Kuala Lumpur hold vegetarian fasts, processions and fire-walking.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Deepavali: The Indian community's festival of lights, with decorations in Kuala Lumpur's Little India.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • Oct 27–31 (approx.) Festival Ubud Writers & Readers Festival: International literary festival in Ubud, Bali, with talks, music and markets.

  14. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Oct 1–25 (approx.) Festival MassKara Festival, Bacolod: MassKara: street dancing in smiling masks.

  15. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Sep 29–Oct 1 (approx.) Public holiday Pchum Ben: A festival for the ancestors, with temple offerings; many people go home.
    • Oct 15 Public holiday King Norodom Sihanouk Commemoration Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 29 Public holiday King's Coronation Day: Public holiday.

  16. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Oct 15–16 (approx.) Festival Boun Ok Phansa & boat racing: The end of Buddhist Lent, with floating lanterns on the Mekong and boat races in Vientiane.

  17. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.
    • Oct 14–16 (approx.) Festival Thadingyut festival of lights: Celebrates the Buddha's return from heaven; temples and homes are lit up.
    • Oct 30 Public holiday Diwali: Public holiday.

  18. South Asia

  19. 🇮🇳 India

    • Oct 2 Public holiday Gandhi Jayanti: National holiday and a nationwide dry day.
    • Oct 4–8 (approx.) Festival Durga Puja, Kolkata: Kolkata's biggest festival: the city fills with artistic pandals, and idols are immersed in the river on the last day.
    • Oct 9 Public holiday Dussehra: Good triumphs over evil as demon effigies are burned; Mysuru's celebrations are the grandest.
    • Oct 15–19 (approx.) Festival Rajasthan International Folk Festival, Jodhpur: Folk music festival at Mehrangarh Fort in Jodhpur, around the full moon.
    • Oct 29 Public holiday Diwali: India's biggest festival, with lamps and fireworks nationwide.

  20. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Sep 30–Oct 14 (approx.) Festival Dashain: Nepal's biggest festival, lasting 15 days.
    • Oct 1–Nov 30 Season Trekking peak season: Clear skies after the monsoon give the best Himalayan views.
    • Oct 6–11 Public holiday Dashain public holidays: The main days of Dashain are national holidays; many shops close and long-distance bus tickets are hard to get.
    • Oct 27–31 (approx.) Festival Tihar (festival of lights): Five days honouring crows, dogs and cows, with every house lit up.
    • Oct 29–31 Public holiday Tihar public holidays: The later days of Tihar are national holidays, and every home is lit with lamps.

  21. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.

  22. Central Asia

  23. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Oct 1 Public holiday Teachers' Day: Public holiday.

  24. Middle East

  25. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Sep 1–Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Göreme grape harvest season: Grape harvest in Cappadocia; wineries open for tastings.
    • Sep 11–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Biennial: Contemporary art biennial held in odd years.
    • Oct 1–3 Sports F1 Turkish Grand Prix (Istanbul): Formula 1 returns in 2027 to Istanbul Park, famous for its multi-apex Turn 8.
    • Oct 29 Public holiday Republic Day: National Day on 29 October, with parades and fireworks.

  26. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.

  27. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Autumn in Jordan: Cool autumn weather; the Red Sea at Aqaba is still warm enough to swim.

  28. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Oct 1–31 (approx.) Festival Bahrain International Music Festival: Autumn classical music festival.

  29. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Oct 20–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Riyadh Season: Riyadh's annual entertainment season, packed with concerts, boxing and themed zones, running into the new year.

  30. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.
    • Oct 15–25 (approx.) Festival Baku Jazz Festival: Baku's international jazz festival in a city with a jazz pedigree.
    • Oct 25–27 (approx.) Festival Pomegranate Festival, Goychay: Pomegranate harvest festival in Goychay, with markets and tastings.

  31. Western Europe

  32. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Oct 13–17 (approx.) Festival Frieze London: International contemporary art fair in Regent's Park, with gallery shows across the city.

  33. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Oct 24–31 (approx.) Festival Halloween where it began (Derry & Meath): Halloween comes from the Celtic Samhain; Derry and County Meath hold big Halloween festivals.
    • Oct 25 Public holiday October Bank Holiday: Last Monday in October.

  34. 🇫🇷 France

    • Sep 10–Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Bordeaux grape harvest: Harvest time at the châteaux; some offer hands-on visits.
    • Oct 2 (approx.) Festival Nuit Blanche, Paris: An all-night contemporary art event in Paris, with museums and public spaces open free.

  35. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Oct 13–17 (approx.) Festival Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE): The world's biggest electronic music conference and party week.

  36. Central Europe

  37. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Sep 18–Oct 3 Festival Oktoberfest, Munich: The world's biggest beer festival; book tent seats early.
    • Oct 1–10 (approx.) Festival Festival of Lights, Berlin: Light projections on Berlin landmarks.
    • Oct 3 Public holiday German Unity Day: National Day on 3 October; shops close. Often near the last day of Oktoberfest.

  38. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Sep 15–Oct 31 (approx.) Festival Wachau wine festivals: Grape harvest season in the Wachau section of the Danube valley.
    • Oct 2 (approx.) Festival Long Night of Museums, Vienna: One ticket for dozens of museums open late into the night.
    • Oct 26 Public holiday National Day: Military display on Heldenplatz on 26 October; some museums are free.

  39. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Sep 15–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Alpine cattle descent (Alpabzug): Cows wearing flower crowns and bells come down from the high pastures, with village markets.

  40. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Oct 2–3 (approx.) Festival Pilsner Fest: Beer festival in the birthplace of pilsner.
    • Oct 14–17 (approx.) Festival Signal Festival, Prague: Light art festival across Prague.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 28 October.

  41. Eastern Europe

  42. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Oct 31 Festival Halloween at Bran Castle: Halloween party at Dracula's castle.

  43. Southern Europe

  44. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Oct 4 Public holiday St Francis Day: A public holiday restored in 2026.
    • Oct 9–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Alba White Truffle Fair: White truffle fair in Alba, Piedmont, a highlight of autumn food travel.

  45. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Oct 8–16 (approx.) Festival Fiestas del Pilar, Zaragoza: A big festival around National Day, with flower offerings to the Virgin of the Pillar.
    • Oct 12 Public holiday National Day (Fiesta Nacional): Military parade in Madrid on 12 October.

  46. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Sep 10–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Port wine harvest (Douro Valley): Grape harvest in the Douro Valley, with grape-treading at the quintas.
    • Oct 5 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Lisbon Marathon: Runs along the coast from Cascais into Lisbon.

  47. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.
    • Oct 28 Public holiday Ohi Day: National Day on 28 October, with student parades.

  48. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • Sep 15–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Truffle season (Istria): White truffle season in Istria, with a truffle festival in Motovun.

  49. Northern Europe

  50. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  51. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.

  52. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.
    • Oct 3–9 (approx.) Festival Helsinki Baltic Herring Market: A harbour herring market held since 1743.

  53. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Culture Night: Hundreds of museums and churches open late into the night.

  54. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.

  55. North America

  56. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Oct 2–10 Festival Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta: The world's largest balloon festival, with hundreds launching at once.
    • Oct 10 (approx.) Sports Chicago Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors, on a flat, fast course.
    • Oct 11 Public holiday Columbus Day (Indigenous Peoples' Day): Second Monday of October; not observed in some states.
    • Oct 22–24 Sports F1 United States Grand Prix (Austin): Formula 1 at the Circuit of the Americas in Texas, with big concerts over the weekend.
    • Oct 31 Festival Halloween: New York's Village Halloween Parade in Greenwich Village.

  57. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Sep 20–Oct 20 Season Autumn leaves season: The Maple Leaf Route from Québec to Niagara is at its reddest.
    • Oct 15–Nov 20 Season Churchill polar bear season: Polar bears gather on the shore at Churchill, Manitoba, waiting for the sea to freeze.
    • Oct 30–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Banff Mountain Film Festival: International mountain and adventure film festival in Banff.

  58. Latin America

  59. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Oct 8–24 (approx.) Festival Festival Internacional Cervantino, Guanajuato: One of Latin America's most important arts festivals.
    • Oct 29–31 Sports F1 Mexico City Grand Prix: Formula 1 at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, often the same week as the Day of the Dead.
    • Oct 31–Nov 2 Festival Day of the Dead: Candles and marigolds in the cemeteries, and a big parade in Mexico City.

  60. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Pantanal dry season: In the dry season animals gather at the water; the best chance to see jaguars.
    • Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026) (approx.) Festival São Paulo Biennial: International contemporary art biennial in even years (next in 2028).
    • Oct 12 Public holiday Our Lady of Aparecida: 12 October honours Brazil's patron saint; it's also Children's Day.
    • Oct 24–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Natal Luz, Gramado: A three-month Christmas festival in a German-heritage town in the south.

  61. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.
    • Oct 8 Public holiday Battle of Angamos Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 18–28 (approx.) Festival Señor de los Milagros procession, Lima: A huge purple-clad religious procession in Lima in October.

  62. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Jun 15–Dec 10 (approx.) Season Península Valdés whale watching season: Southern right whales come to Península Valdés to breed. Boat trips run from Puerto Pirámides, Argentina's only authorised whale-watching port, from mid-June to early December. Sources: puertopiramides.gov.ar, argentina.travel
    • Oct 1–11 (approx.) Festival Oktoberfest, Villa General Belgrano: Oktoberfest in a German-heritage town.
    • Oct 11 Public holiday Day of Respect for Cultural Diversity: A public holiday in October.

  63. Africa

  64. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Oct 7 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: 6 October marks the 1973 war.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.
    • Oct 22 Festival Abu Simbel Sun Festival: A solar phenomenon that happens twice a year.

  65. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.
    • Oct 28–31 (approx.) Festival Date Festival, Erfoud: Date harvest festival in Erfoud, gateway to the Sahara.
    • Oct 31 Public holiday Unity Day: A public holiday added in 2025.

  66. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Oct 1–Nov 15 Season Jacaranda season (Pretoria): Tens of thousands of jacarandas bloom and turn the capital purple.
    • Oct 2–Nov 21 (approx.) Sports Cricket World Cup: The one-day Cricket World Cup co-hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia, with the final in Johannesburg.

  67. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Great Migration: Wildebeest cross the Mara River, most spectacularly in August.
    • Oct 10–11 Public holiday Mazingira Day: Public holiday on 10 October.
    • Oct 20 Public holiday Mashujaa Day: Public holiday on 20 October.

  68. Oceania

  69. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Jul 15–Oct 31 (approx.) Season Hervey Bay whale watching season: Humpback whales rest in Hervey Bay, Queensland, on their southern migration. July to October is the main season, with most whales in the bay from August to October. Sources: qld.gov.au, statements.qld.gov.au
    • Sep 11–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Floriade, Canberra: Canberra's spring flower show, one of the largest in the southern hemisphere.
    • Oct 1–Nov 13 Sports Rugby World Cup: The first 24-team men's Rugby World Cup, opening in Perth with the final in Sydney.
    • Oct 22–Nov 8 (approx.) Festival Sculpture by the Sea, Bondi: A huge sculpture exhibition on the coastal walk from Bondi to Tamarama.

  70. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.
    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Spring in New Zealand (lambs & blossoms): Lambs are born on the farms and Christchurch's gardens bloom.
    • Sep 23–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival World of WearableArt, Wellington: An international wearable-art design competition and stage show.
    • Oct 25 Public holiday Labour Day: Fourth Monday of October.

October travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in October?

Highlights in October 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); Göreme grape harvest season (Türkiye, Sep 1–Oct 15); São Paulo Biennial (Brazil, Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026)); Bordeaux grape harvest (France, Sep 10–Oct 15); Port wine harvest (Douro Valley) (Portugal, Sep 10–Oct 10); Istanbul Biennial (Türkiye, Sep 11–Nov 7); Floriade, Canberra (Australia, Sep 11–Oct 10); Wachau wine festivals (Austria, Sep 15–Oct 31); Alpine cattle descent (Alpabzug) (Switzerland, Sep 15–Oct 10); Truffle season (Istria) (Croatia, Sep 15–Nov 30), among 72 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in October 2027?

There are 40 public holidays or long weekends, including Pchum Ben in Cambodia (Sep 29–Oct 1); National Day Golden Week in China (Oct 1–7); National Day in Hong Kong (Oct 1); Teachers' Day in Uzbekistan (Oct 1); Gandhi Jayanti in India (Oct 2); German Unity Day in Germany (Oct 3); National Foundation Day in South Korea (Oct 3–4); St Francis Day in Italy (Oct 4); Republic Day in Portugal (Oct 5); Dashain public holidays in Nepal (Oct 6–11); Armed Forces Day in Egypt (Oct 7); Chung Yeung Festival in Hong Kong (Oct 8) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.

Where is in season in October?

How accurate are the dates?
