  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Mar 20–Apr 15 Season Cherry blossom season: The bloom moves north from Kyushu; Tokyo and Kyoto peak around late March to early April.
    • Apr 9–11 Sports F1 Japanese Grand Prix (Suzuka): Formula 1 at Suzuka Circuit in Mie, often during cherry blossom season; book Kintetsu trains from Nagoya and hotels early.
    • Apr 29–May 5 Public holiday Golden Week: Peak domestic travel; flights and hotels cost more, so it's best avoided.
    • Apr 29 Public holiday Showa Day: The first day of Golden Week.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Mar 27–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival: Korea's biggest cherry blossom festival; Yeojwacheon's Romance Bridge is the classic photo spot.
    • Apr 3–9 (approx.) Festival Yeouido Cherry Blossom Festival: 1,800 cherry trees line Gukhoe-daero in Seoul.
    • Apr 4–12 (approx.) Festival Jeju Canola Flower Festival: Canola and cherry blossoms bloom together along Noksan-ro in Gasiri.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Mar 15–Apr 10 (approx.) Festival Shanghai Peach Blossom Festival: Peach orchards bloom around Nanhui in Pudong; a favourite spring outing.
    • Apr 1–May 5 (approx.) Festival Luoyang Peony Festival: Peonies bloom at Wangcheng Park and the National Peony Garden, best in mid-April; pair it with the Longmen Grottoes.
    • Apr 5 Public holiday Qingming Festival: Short Qingming holiday; crowds heading home to sweep tombs and out on spring outings.
    • Apr 9–11 (approx.) Festival Guangxi Sanyuesan Song Festival: Zhuang song festival with antiphonal singing and firecracker-catching in Nanning and Wuming; a two-day holiday in Guangxi.
    • Apr 13–15 Festival Xishuangbanna Water-Splashing Festival: Dai New Year in Jinghong, with dragon-boat races, rocket launches and city-wide water fights.
    • Apr 16–18 Sports F1 Chinese Grand Prix (Shanghai): Formula 1 at the Shanghai International Circuit, reached by Metro Line 11.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Apr 4–6 Public holiday Children's Day & Tomb-Sweeping long weekend: A roughly four-day weekend; peak season for flights and domestic transport.
    • Apr 7–16 (approx.) Festival Dajia Mazu Pilgrimage: A nine-day, eight-night walking pilgrimage, Taiwan's largest religious event; dates are set by divination blocks.
    • Apr 15–May 31 (approx.) Festival Hakka Tung Blossom Festival: Tung blossoms cover the hills of Miaoli, Hsinchu and Taichung; best in late April and early May.
    • Apr 30–May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: A day off for workers; from 2026 civil servants also get the day off.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Apr 5 Public holiday Qingming Festival: Public holiday; traffic controls near cemeteries.
    • Apr 29 (approx.) Festival Tin Hau Festival: On the 23rd of the 3rd lunar month, with flower-cannon processions in Yuen Long and at Joss House Bay.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Apr 6 Public holiday Chakri Day: Public holiday.
    • Apr 12–16 Festival Songkran on Chiang Mai's moat: The area around Chiang Mai's moat has Thailand's liveliest water fights.
    • Apr 13–15 Public holiday Songkran (water festival): Thai New Year with nationwide water fights; liveliest in Chiang Mai and Bangkok.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Feb 11–Apr 20 (approx.) Festival Perfume Pagoda Festival: The pilgrimage season at the Perfume Pagoda outside Hanoi, reached by small boats.
    • Apr 16 (approx.) Public holiday Hung Kings' Commemoration: On the 10th of the 3rd lunar month, honouring the nation's founding kings; a public holiday.
    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Reunification Day & Labour Day holidays: A 30 April–1 May long weekend, one of the busiest domestic travel periods; beach towns raise prices.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Apr 10–11 (approx.) Sports Singapore Sevens: A World Rugby Sevens stop at the National Stadium.

  11. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.

  12. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Apr 9 Public holiday Day of Valor: Commemorates the Battle of Bataan.
    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.

  13. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Apr 14–16 Public holiday Khmer New Year: New Year celebrations around Angkor, with water-splashing and traditional games.

  14. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Apr 14–16 Public holiday Lao New Year (Pi Mai): Luang Prabang's water festival is the most traditional, with a beauty pageant and sand stupas.

  15. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Apr 13–21 Public holiday Thingyan water festival: Myanmar's New Year, with water-throwing nationwide.

  16. South Asia

  17. 🇮🇳 India

    • Apr 13–14 (approx.) Festival Baisakhi: Punjab's New Year harvest festival; liveliest at the Golden Temple in Amritsar.
    • Apr 14 Public holiday Ambedkar Jayanti: National holiday.
    • Apr 15 Public holiday Ram Navami: Hindu festival.
    • Apr 18 Public holiday Mahavir Jayanti: Jain festival.

  18. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Apr 10–18 (approx.) Festival Bisket Jatra, Bhaktapur: Bhaktapur's big festival around Nepali New Year, with chariot-pulling and a giant pole raised.
    • Apr 14 Public holiday Nepali New Year: Bikram Sambat New Year; Bhaktapur holds Bisket Jatra.
    • Apr 15 Public holiday Ram Navami: Hindu festival.

  19. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • Apr 13–14 Public holiday Sinhala & Tamil New Year: Traditional New Year nationwide.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Southwest coast high season: Dry season in Galle and Mirissa, and whale-watching season.

  20. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.

  21. Middle East

  22. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–30 Festival Istanbul Tulip Festival: Millions of tulips in Emirgan Park and elsewhere.
    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Apr 9–20 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Film Festival: Türkiye's most important film festival, with screenings around the city.
    • Apr 23 Public holiday National Sovereignty and Children's Day: Public holiday with children's parades.

  23. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Apr 24–May 10 (approx.) Festival Dubai Food Festival: Restaurant deals and beach food events across Dubai.
    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.

  24. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Best time to visit: Mild spring weather, good for hiking in Petra.
    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Spring in Jordan: The most comfortable season, ideal for Wadi Rum and hiking in Petra.
    • Apr 9 (approx.) Festival Dead Sea Ultra Marathon: Runs from Amman down to the Dead Sea, finishing at the lowest point on Earth.

  25. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Mar 1–Apr 30 (approx.) Festival Spring of Culture, Bahrain: Bahrain's largest cultural festival, with international music and arts.

  26. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Apr 1–30 (approx.) Festival Taif Rose Festival: Rose harvest in the Taif highlands, with rose-oil workshops and markets.

  27. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.

  28. Western Europe

  29. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Apr 24–25 (approx.) Sports London Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors; from 2027 it runs over Saturday and Sunday.

  30. 🇫🇷 France

    • Apr 4 (approx.) Festival Paris Marathon: A city marathon down the Champs-Élysées and past the Arc de Triomphe.

  31. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Mar 19–May 10 (approx.) Season Keukenhof open season: The world's largest tulip garden, open only for about eight weeks in spring.
    • Apr 15–Sep 30 Season Windmill & Giethoorn high season: Good weather in spring and summer; the windmills and canal villages are at their busiest.
    • Apr 24 (approx.) Festival Bloemencorso flower parade: Flower-float parade through the bulb fields from Noordwijk to Haarlem.
    • Apr 27 Public holiday King's Day: The whole country dresses in orange, with canal parties and flea markets in Amsterdam.

  32. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Mar 1–Apr 15 (approx.) Festival Printemps des Arts de Monte-Carlo: Monte-Carlo's spring arts festival at the Opera and other venues.
    • Apr 4–11 (approx.) Sports Rolex Monte-Carlo Masters: The ATP Masters that opens the clay season, with the most scenic sea views on tour.

  33. Central Europe

  34. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Apr 30 Festival Walpurgis Night (Harz): Witches' night in the Harz mountains, with bonfires and costume parties.

  35. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Apr 19 (approx.) Festival Sechseläuten, Zurich: A snowman effigy is burned; how fast its head explodes forecasts the summer.

  36. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Mar 13–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival Prague Easter markets: Easter markets on Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square.

  37. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Apr 1–25 (approx.) Festival Budapest Spring Festival: Budapest's spring arts festival.

  38. Eastern Europe

  39. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Apr 25–May 9 (approx.) Festival Moscow Easter festival: Easter egg installations and fairs around Orthodox Easter (2 May in 2027).

  40. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: Romania's most important holiday, with a long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  41. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  42. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Apr 7–11 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Music Week: A Baltic music showcase festival.

  43. Southern Europe

  44. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • Apr 13–18 (approx.) Festival Milan Design Week: The international furniture fair plus design shows across the city; hotel prices soar.
    • Apr 25 Public holiday Liberation Day: Public holiday on 25 April.

  45. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Apr 11–17 (approx.) Festival Seville April Fair: Flamenco dresses, casetas and horse-drawn parades.
    • Apr 23 Festival Sant Jordi (books & roses), Barcelona: Barcelona's day of love: La Rambla fills with book stalls and roses.

  46. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Apr 16–May 9 (approx.) Festival Madeira Flower Festival: Spring flower festival in Funchal, Madeira, with flower parades and carpets.
    • Apr 25 Public holiday Freedom Day (Carnation Revolution): Public holiday on 25 April.

  47. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Orthodox Easter: Greece's most important holiday, with a candlelit midnight service.
    • Apr 30–May 3 (approx.) Festival Orthodox Easter in Corfu: On Holy Saturday morning, clay pots are thrown from balconies into the streets: Greece's most distinctive Easter.

  48. Northern Europe

  49. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Feb 1–Apr 30 Festival Lofoten cod season: Cod-fishing season in Lofoten, with drying racks full of stockfish.

  50. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Apr 30 Festival Walpurgis Night: Bonfires welcome spring nationwide; the student celebrations in Uppsala are the liveliest.
    • Dec 10–Apr 10 (approx.) Season Icehotel open season: The Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is carved anew every year.

  51. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jan 20–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival SnowCastle of Kemi: The SnowCastle of Kemi and ice sculptures, rebuilt every year.
    • Apr 30–May 1 Public holiday Vappu (May Day): Students wear white caps and the whole country picnics and parties.

  52. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Apr 22 Public holiday First Day of Summer: The third Thursday of April, a traditional Icelandic holiday.
    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.

  53. North America

  54. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Mar 20–Apr 12 (approx.) Festival National Cherry Blossom Festival, Washington DC: Cherry trees bloom around the Tidal Basin on the National Mall, with a Japanese culture parade.
    • Apr 9–18 (approx.) Festival Coachella: A huge music and arts festival in the California desert over two weekends.
    • Apr 19 Sports Boston Marathon: The world's oldest city marathon, on the third Monday of April (Patriots' Day); qualifying times required.
    • Apr 23–May 2 (approx.) Festival New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival: Jazz and heritage music festival at the New Orleans Fair Grounds.
    • Apr 30–May 2 Sports F1 Miami Grand Prix: Formula 1 on a circuit around Hard Rock Stadium, with a big party atmosphere.

  55. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Mar 1–Apr 15 Festival Québec sugar shack season: Maple brunches and maple taffy on snow at a cabane à sucre.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.

  56. Latin America

  57. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Apr 21 Public holiday Tiradentes Day: 21 April honours the independence hero Tiradentes.

  58. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Mar 1–May 31 Season Best time for Iguazú Falls: Plenty of water in autumn, cooler weather and fewer crowds.
    • Apr 2 Public holiday Malvinas Veterans' Day: Public holiday on 2 April.
    • Apr 22–May 10 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires International Book Fair: One of the largest book fairs in the Spanish-speaking world.

  59. Africa

  60. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Mar 15–Jun 15 Season Red Sea diving season: Water temperatures rise and visibility is good at Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh.
    • Apr 29 Public holiday Sinai Liberation Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.

  61. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.

  62. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Mar 26–Apr 3 (approx.) Festival KKNK arts festival, Oudtshoorn: An Afrikaans arts festival in Oudtshoorn, the ostrich capital.
    • Apr 27 Public holiday Freedom Day: 27 April marks the first democratic election in 1994.

  63. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Apr 1–May 31 Season Long rains low season: The long rains: some camps close, roads are muddy and prices are at their lowest.

  64. Oceania

  65. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Mar 31–Apr 25 (approx.) Festival Melbourne International Comedy Festival: One of the world's three biggest comedy festivals.
    • Apr 2–4 Sports F1 Australian Grand Prix (Melbourne): Formula 1 on the Albert Park street circuit in Melbourne; in 2027 it moves from early March to early April, and accommodation prices jump on race weekend.
    • Apr 25 Public holiday Anzac Day: Dawn services everywhere on 25 April; most shops are closed in the morning.

  66. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Apr 25–26 Public holiday Anzac Day: Dawn services everywhere on the morning of 25 April.
