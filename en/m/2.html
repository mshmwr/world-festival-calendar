  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Feb 3 Festival Setsubun: Beans are thrown to drive out demons ("Oni wa soto, fuku wa uchi"), and people eat ehomaki rolls.
    • Feb 11 Public holiday National Foundation Day: Public holiday.
    • Feb 23 Public holiday Emperor's Birthday: The Emperor greets the public at the Imperial Palace.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Jan 9–Feb 1 (approx.) Festival Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival: Ice fishing and catching trout by hand, Gangwon's signature winter event.
    • Feb 6–9 Public holiday Seollal (Lunar New Year): Lunar New Year holidays; most shops close and transport is crowded.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Jan 5–Feb 28 Festival Harbin Ice and Snow Festival: Giant ice sculptures lit up at night at Ice and Snow World; bring full gear for -20°C.
    • Feb 5–11 Public holiday Spring Festival (Lunar New Year): The Spring Festival travel rush; sights are crowded and some shops close.
    • Dec 18–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Harbin Ice and Snow World: A giant ice-sculpture park by the Songhua River, opening around late December; most spectacular lit up at night.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Feb 4–10 Public holiday Lunar New Year: Family reunions; temples are packed on New Year's morning and festive markets fill the streets.
    • Feb 20 Festival Lantern Festival: Pingxi sky lanterns & Yanshui beehive fireworks: Sky lanterns in Pingxi, beehive fireworks in Yanshui and Handan bombing in Taitung.
    • Feb 20–Mar 7 (approx.) Festival Taiwan Lantern Festival: Taiwan's national lantern festival from Lantern Festival day, held in a different city each year, with a projection show on the main lantern at night.
    • Feb 28–Mar 1 Public holiday Peace Memorial Day: The 2/28 long weekend; domestic trips and flights abroad cost more.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Feb 6–7 Festival Lunar New Year Night Parade & fireworks: Night parade of floats in Tsim Sha Tsui, and fireworks over the harbour on the second day.
    • Feb 6–9 Public holiday Lunar New Year holidays: Days 1–3 of the new year are holidays; many small shops reopen only after day 4, but big malls stay open.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Feb 7–9 (approx.) Public holiday Tsagaan Sar (Mongolian New Year): Mongolian lunar new year; families gather and some shops close.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Feb 5–7 Festival Chiang Mai Flower Festival: Flower-float parade on the first weekend of February.
    • Feb 6–7 (approx.) Festival Chinese New Year on Yaowarat Road, Bangkok: Yaowarat Road in Bangkok's Chinatown closes to traffic for lanterns and lion dances.
    • Feb 21–22 Public holiday Makha Bucha: Buddhist holiday with a nationwide alcohol ban and candlelit processions at temples in the evening.
    • Nov 1–Feb 28 Season Peak season (dry season): Cool and dry, the best time to travel, and the most expensive.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Jan 30–Feb 8 (approx.) Festival Nguyen Hue Tết flower street, Ho Chi Minh City: Before Tết, Nguyen Hue walking street becomes a huge flower street.
    • Feb 4–10 Public holiday Tết (Lunar New Year): Vietnam's biggest holiday; many shops close for a week or more.
    • Feb 11–Apr 20 (approx.) Festival Perfume Pagoda Festival: The pilgrimage season at the Perfume Pagoda outside Hanoi, reached by small boats.
    • Feb 20 (approx.) Festival Hoi An Lantern Festival (first full moon): On the 14th of each lunar month Hoi An turns off its lights for lanterns and river candles; biggest at the first full moon.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Feb 4–14 (approx.) Festival River Hongbao: Lunar New Year lantern displays and market at Marina Bay.
    • Feb 6–7 Public holiday Chinese New Year & Chingay Parade: Chinatown light-up and the Chingay parade.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Feb 6–8 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Two days of Chinese New Year; Chinese-run shops close longer, with celebrations in Penang and Kuala Lumpur.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan bazaars: Ramadan bazaars open every evening; Kampung Baru in Kuala Lumpur is the liveliest.
    • Feb 14–20 (approx.) Festival Chinese New Year in George Town, Penang: New Year temple fairs and lion dances in George Town's heritage zone.
    • Feb 20 (approx.) Festival Chap Goh Mei orange throwing, Penang: At Penang's Esplanade, single women toss oranges into the sea hoping for a good match.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday, celebrated in Chinese communities.

  14. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Feb 1–28 Festival Panagbenga Flower Festival, Baguio: Panagbenga flower-float parades.
    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Special holiday; Manila's Chinatown is the liveliest.
    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.

  15. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.

  16. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Feb 19–21 (approx.) Festival Wat Phou Festival: A Buddhist festival at Wat Phou in Champasak (World Heritage), with full-moon lights and a traditional fair.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.

  17. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday; Yangon's Chinatown is lively.
    • Feb 12 Public holiday Union Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.

  18. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Chinese New Year: Public holiday.
    • Feb 8 (approx.) Public holiday First day of Ramadan: Most restaurants are closed by day during Ramadan.
    • Feb 23 Public holiday National Day: Parade in Bandar Seri Begawan.
    • Feb 24 (approx.) Public holiday Nuzul Al-Quran: Public holiday.

  19. South Asia

  20. 🇮🇳 India

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Goa Carnival: Goa's street carnival before Lent, with floats and music.
    • Feb 18–20 (approx.) Festival Desert Festival, Jaisalmer: Camel races, pageants and folk performances in a Rajasthan desert city.

  21. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Feb 7 Public holiday Lhosar (Sonam & Gyalpo Lhosar): Liveliest around Boudhanath Stupa.
    • Feb 19 Public holiday Democracy Day: Public holiday.

  22. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • Feb 4 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 4 February, with a parade in Colombo.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Southwest coast high season: Dry season in Galle and Mirissa, and whale-watching season.

  23. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Feb 8 (approx.) Public holiday First day of Ramadan: Resorts operate as normal during Ramadan; most restaurants in Malé close during the day.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.

  24. Middle East

  25. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: No eating or drinking in public by day; restaurants keep shorter hours and iftar markets open at night.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan nights & iftars: Hotels host iftar and suhoor during Ramadan; avoid eating in public by day.
    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.

  26. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.

  27. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C; from June to September temperatures often top 40°C.

  28. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Feb 22 Public holiday Founding Day: 22 February marks the founding of the first Saudi state in 1727, with traditional shows in Diriyah.
    • Feb 26–27 (approx.) Festival Saudi Cup (horse racing): One of the world's richest races, at King Abdulaziz Racecourse in Riyadh.
    • Nov 1–Mar 15 Season Best time to visit: Winter days in Riyadh and AlUla are in the 20s°C and desert nights near 10°C; inland summers often exceed 45°C.
    • Dec 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival AlUla winter (Winter at Tantora): Concerts, balloon flights and night tours of the ruins during AlUla's winter, with big shows at the Maraya.

  29. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Feb 1–10 (approx.) Festival Qatar International Food Festival: International food festival in a Doha park.
    • Feb 8–Mar 12 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid events at Katara: Ramadan night market and Eid fireworks at Katara Cultural Village.
    • Feb 9 Public holiday National Sport Day: Second Tuesday of February, with runs and sports events nationwide.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.

  30. Western Europe

  31. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Feb 7 (approx.) Festival Chinese New Year in London: A big Lunar New Year parade in Chinatown and Trafalgar Square, one of the largest outside Asia.

  32. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Feb 1 Public holiday St Brigid's Day: First Monday in February (or 1 February if it's a Friday); a holiday added in 2023.

  33. 🇫🇷 France

    • Jan 13–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Winter sales (Soldes): France's official winter sales, with big discounts in department stores and boutiques.
    • Feb 13–28 (approx.) Festival Nice Carnival & Menton Lemon Festival: Flower battles on floats and giant citrus sculptures.
    • Feb 23–Mar 3 (approx.) Festival Paris Fashion Week (autumn/winter): Autumn/winter womenswear shows; lots of fashion events and pricier hotels.

  34. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Maastricht Carnival: The liveliest carnival in the southern Netherlands; the whole city dresses up.

  35. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Feb 7–9 Festival Carnival of Binche: UNESCO-listed: the Gilles clowns throw oranges into the crowd.
    • Feb 19–21 (approx.) Festival Salon du Chocolat Brussels: Chocolatier showcase with tastings.

  36. Central Europe

  37. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Feb 4–9 Festival Cologne Carnival: The big Rose Monday parade; the whole city dresses up.
    • Feb 11–21 (approx.) Festival Berlinale: One of the world's top three film festivals; some screenings are open to the public.

  38. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Jan 7–Feb 28 Festival Vienna ball season: Hundreds of balls, the Opera Ball being the most famous.

  39. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Feb 15–17 Festival Basel Fasnacht: The lights go out at 4 a.m. for the Morgestraich, opening three days of carnival.

  40. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Masopust carnival: Traditional costumed processions before Lent, most authentic in the countryside.

  41. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Feb 4–9 (approx.) Festival Busójárás, Mohács: Scary wooden masks chase away winter before Lent; on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  42. Eastern Europe

  43. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Feb 23 Public holiday Defender of the Fatherland Day: Public holiday.

  44. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Feb 14 Festival Trifon Zarezan (vine-pruning day): A traditional day of pruning the vines and praying for a good harvest.

  45. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Feb 24 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 24 February, with a parade in Tallinn.

  46. Southern Europe

  47. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • Jan 23–Feb 9 Festival Venice Carnival: Elaborate masks and costumes; St Mark's Square is the heart of it.
    • Feb 6–22 (2026) Sports Milano Cortina Winter Olympics: The 25th Winter Olympics, spread across Milan, Cortina d'Ampezzo and other venues; the next will be in the French Alps in 2030.

  48. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Feb 4–14 (approx.) Festival Cádiz Carnival: Spain's liveliest carnival, famous for its satirical songs.

  49. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Feb 5–10 (approx.) Festival Torres Vedras Carnival: Portugal's most distinctive carnival, with men parading in drag.

  50. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Jan 17–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Patras Carnival: Greece's biggest carnival, climaxing on the weekend before Lent.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.

  51. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • Jan 17–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Rijeka Carnival: Croatia's biggest carnival.
    • Feb 3 Festival Feast of St Blaise, Dubrovnik: The city's patron saint's day, with a procession in the old town; on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  52. Northern Europe

  53. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Feb 1–Apr 30 Festival Lofoten cod season: Cod-fishing season in Lofoten, with drying racks full of stockfish.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  54. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Feb 4–6 (approx.) Festival Jokkmokk Winter Market: A Sámi winter market in Lapland with over 400 years of history.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.
    • Dec 10–Apr 10 (approx.) Season Icehotel open season: The Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is carved anew every year.

  55. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jan 20–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival SnowCastle of Kemi: The SnowCastle of Kemi and ice sculptures, rebuilt every year.
    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.

  56. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Feb 5–28 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Light Festival: Winter light art festival.

  57. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Feb 4–7 (approx.) Festival Winter Lights Festival, Reykjavík: Reykjavík's winter light festival and Pool Night.
    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.

  58. North America

  59. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Feb 9 Festival Mardi Gras, New Orleans: Parades and bead-throwing in the French Quarter.
    • Feb 14 Sports Super Bowl: The NFL championship game, in the San Francisco Bay Area in 2026 and at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles in 2027; the halftime show is America's event of the year.
    • Feb 15 Public holiday Presidents' Day: Third Monday of February, with mall sales and busy ski resorts.

  60. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Jan 29–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Québec Winter Carnival: Ice palace, snow sculptures and an ice canoe race.
    • Jan 29–Feb 15 (approx.) Festival Winterlude, Ottawa: The Rideau Canal becomes the world's largest naturally frozen skating rink, with ice sculptures.
    • Nov 14–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Winter Festival of Lights, Niagara Falls: The falls are lit up at night with light displays.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.

  61. Latin America

  62. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Feb 1 Public holiday Constitution Day: First Monday of February.
    • Feb 4–9 (approx.) Festival Mazatlán Carnival: One of Mexico's biggest carnivals.
    • Nov 15–Mar 15 Festival Monarch butterfly season (Michoacán): Hundreds of millions of monarch butterflies winter in the forest, most in February; a World Heritage reserve.

  63. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Feb 4–10 (approx.) Festival Salvador Carnival: Bahia's street carnival with music trucks, the largest by participants in the world.
    • Feb 5–10 Festival Rio Carnival: Samba school parades in the Sambadrome; tickets go on sale months ahead.

  64. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • Feb 1–14 (approx.) Festival Virgen de la Candelaria, Puno: A huge religious dance festival in Puno on Lake Titicaca, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.

  65. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Feb 6–9 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires Carnival (murgas): Murga music and dance shows in neighbourhood streets.
    • Feb 8–9 Public holiday Carnival holidays: Carnival parades everywhere on the Monday and Tuesday before Lent.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.

  66. Africa

  67. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Jan 27–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Cairo International Book Fair: One of the largest book fairs in the Middle East.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan lanterns & night markets, Cairo: During Ramadan Cairo's streets are hung with fanous lanterns and the old-town night markets run late.
    • Feb 22 Festival Abu Simbel Sun Festival: At dawn the sun lights the statue of Ramesses II deep inside the temple; it happens again on 22 October.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.

  68. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Jan 25–Feb 28 Season Almond blossom season (Tafraoute): Almond trees blossom in the Anti-Atlas.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: Most restaurants close by day and sights keep shorter hours.
    • Feb 8–Mar 10 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid (Fes, Marrakech): Many restaurants close by day during Ramadan; markets come alive after the evening iftar.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.

  69. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Feb 1–Mar 31 Festival Wine harvest (Stellenbosch): Grape harvest in the Cape Winelands, with harvest activities at the estates.

  70. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jan 1–Mar 15 Season Dry-season safari: The dry season in January–February concentrates animals near water; it's also calving season.

  71. Oceania

  72. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Feb 19–Mar 6 (approx.) Festival Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras: The big parade along Oxford Street in Sydney.
    • Feb 19–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Adelaide Fringe: The world's second-largest fringe festival.

  73. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Feb 6 Public holiday Waitangi Day: New Zealand's national day, with Māori cultural events at Waitangi.
    • Feb 25–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Aotearoa New Zealand Festival of the Arts, Wellington: New Zealand's largest international arts festival, with theatre, dance and music in the capital.
    • Feb 26–28 (approx.) Festival Auckland Lantern Festival: Auckland's Lunar New Year lantern festival.
    • Dec 15–Feb 28 Season Peak summer season: Peak season for South Island hikes and lakes; book accommodation early.
