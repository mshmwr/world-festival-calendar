Where to go in July

  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Jul 7 Festival Tanabata: Wishes are written on strips of paper and hung on bamboo; Sendai and some other places celebrate in August.
    • Jul 19 Public holiday Marine Day: Third Monday of July, the start of beach season.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Jul 1–5 (approx.) Festival Daegu Chimac Festival: Fried chicken and beer festival at Duryu Park in Daegu, Korea's summer favourite.
    • Jul 17–26 (approx.) Festival Boryeong Mud Festival: Mud fights on Daecheon Beach; the liveliest outdoor event of summer.
    • Jul 17–19 Public holiday Constitution Day: Restored as a public holiday from 2026.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Jul 17–Aug 10 (approx.) Festival Qingdao International Beer Festival: Asia's biggest beer festival, running for weeks at the Golden Beach beer city.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Taiwan International Balloon Festival, Taitung: Hot-air balloons rise over Luye Highland every morning, with light-show concerts at night.
    • Jul 3–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival Yilan International Children's Folklore & Folkgame Festival: Summer water play and international folk performances at Dongshan River Water Park.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Jul 1 Public holiday HKSAR Establishment Day: Fireworks or celebrations over Victoria Harbour on 1 July.
    • Jul 14–20 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Book Fair: A huge book fair at the Wan Chai convention centre; crowded during school holidays.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Jul 10–15 Public holiday National Day (Naadam holidays): Holidays during Naadam; Ulaanbaatar hotels are at their priciest and hardest to book.
    • Jul 11–15 Festival Naadam Festival: Wrestling, horse racing and archery: Mongolia's biggest festival.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • Jul 18–19 (approx.) Festival Ubon Candle Festival: A parade of giant wax sculptures for Buddhist Lent.
    • Jul 18–20 Public holiday Asahna Bucha & Buddhist Lent Day: Two consecutive Buddhist holidays with a nationwide alcohol ban; the Ubon Candle Festival takes place at the same time.
    • Jul 28 Public holiday King's Birthday: Public holiday, with portraits of the King and yellow flags everywhere.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Jun 1–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Da Nang International Fireworks Festival: International fireworks teams compete over the Han River on Saturday nights.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Jun 1–Jul 31 (approx.) Festival Great Singapore Sale: Island-wide mall sales.
    • Jul 15–31 (approx.) Festival Singapore Food Festival: Themed events at restaurants and markets across the island.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Jul 1–10 (approx.) Festival Royal Floria Putrajaya: International flower festival in Putrajaya with floats and floral displays.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • Jun 13–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Bali Arts Festival: A month of dance and music at the Denpasar Arts Centre.
    • Jul 1–Sep 30 (approx.) Season Toraja funeral season: The Toraja of Sulawesi hold their great funerals mostly in the dry season; visits can be arranged through local guides.
    • Jul 15–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival Bali Kite Festival: Giant traditional kite contests on Sanur beach.

  14. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Jul 18 (approx.) Festival Khao Phansa: Buddhist Lent begins, with candles and offerings to monks at the temples.

  15. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Jul 18 Public holiday Full Moon of Waso (start of Buddhist Lent): Buddhist Lent begins.
    • Jul 19 Public holiday Martyrs' Day: Remembers the assassination of General Aung San.

  16. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Jul 15 Public holiday Sultan's Birthday: Celebrations nationwide with parades and lights.

  17. South Asia

  18. 🇮🇳 India

    • Jul 5 (approx.) Festival Rath Yatra, Puri: Huge chariot procession in Puri, Odisha, pulled by hundreds of thousands of devotees.

  19. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season East coast high season: While the southwest has its rains, Trincomalee and Arugam Bay on the east coast are dry; peak surf season.
    • Jul 1–15 (approx.) Festival Kataragama Festival: Pilgrimage festival at the sacred site of Kataragama, shared by Buddhists and Hindus.

  20. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Jul 26 Public holiday Independence Day: Parades and fireworks in Malé.

  21. Middle East

  22. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Jul 1–20 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Jazz Festival: Istanbul's summer jazz festival.
    • Jul 2–4 (approx.) Festival Kırkpınar Oil Wrestling, Edirne: One of the world's oldest wrestling events, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • Jul 15 Public holiday Democracy and National Unity Day: Commemorates the defeat of the 2016 coup attempt.

  23. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Dubai Summer Surprises: Summer sales season with family activities in the malls.

  24. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Jul 22–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Jerash Festival of Culture and Arts: Music and folk performances in Jerash's Roman city; Jordan's biggest cultural festival.

  25. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Bahrain Summer Festival: Summer indoor family activities and shopping season.

  26. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Jun 1–Jul 31 (approx.) Festival Jeddah Season: Jeddah's summer entertainment season, with waterfront events and concerts.

  27. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Summer in Qatar: Summer is mostly indoors, with family activities and deals at malls and hotels.

  28. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.
    • Jul 20–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Gabala International Music Festival: Classical music festival in the Gabala mountains in the north.

  29. Western Europe

  30. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Jun 28–Jul 11 Sports Wimbledon: The grass-court Grand Slam; queuing for same-day tickets is a tradition.
    • Jul 2–4 Sports F1 British Grand Prix (Silverstone): Formula 1 at Silverstone, the birthplace of F1; often the same time as Wimbledon, so London hotels are harder to book.
    • Jul 23–Aug 2 (2026) Sports Commonwealth Games, Glasgow: Multi-sport games for 74 Commonwealth nations and territories, held in Glasgow, Scotland.

  31. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Jul 12–25 (approx.) Festival Galway International Arts Festival: The west of Ireland's biggest arts festival, with street performances and parades.
    • Jul 26–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Galway Races: Ireland's liveliest week of racing.

  32. 🇫🇷 France

    • Jun 20–Jul 25 Season Provence lavender season: The fields of the Valensole plateau peak in early July and vanish after harvest.
    • Jul 4–26 (approx.) Sports Tour de France: Finishes on the Champs-Élysées in Paris.
    • Jul 5–26 (approx.) Festival Festival d'Avignon: One of the world's biggest theatre festivals, in the Palais des Papes and theatres across Avignon.
    • Jul 8–Aug 20 (approx.) Festival Paris Plages: The banks of the Seine become artificial beaches and leisure areas.
    • Jul 14 Public holiday Bastille Day: Military parade on the Champs-Élysées and fireworks at the Eiffel Tower.

  33. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Apr 15–Sep 30 Season Windmill & Giethoorn high season: Good weather in spring and summer; the windmills and canal villages are at their busiest.
    • Jul 9–11 (approx.) Festival North Sea Jazz Festival: Big jazz festival in Rotterdam.

  34. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Jul 16–25 (approx.) Festival Ghent Festival (Gentse Feesten): Ten days of music and street festivities in Ghent's old town, one of Europe's biggest city festivals.
    • Jul 16–25 (approx.) Festival Tomorrowland: World-famous electronic music festival in Boom; tickets sell out globally in seconds.
    • Jul 21 Public holiday National Day: Military parade and fireworks in Brussels on 21 July.
    • Jul 23–25 Sports F1 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa): Formula 1 at Spa-Francorchamps in the Ardennes forest, where the weather is unpredictable.

  35. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Monte-Carlo Summer Festival: Concerts by international stars at the Monte-Carlo Sporting Club.
    • Jul 15–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival International Fireworks Festival, Monte-Carlo: International fireworks competition over Port Hercule on several summer nights.

  36. Central Europe

  37. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Jul 24 (approx.) Festival Berlin Pride (CSD): One of Europe's largest Pride parades.

  38. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Film Festival at Vienna City Hall: Free concert films on a big screen outside City Hall on summer nights.
    • Jul 9–11 Sports F1 Austrian Grand Prix (Red Bull Ring): Formula 1 on a circuit in the Styrian mountains, where many fans camp.
    • Jul 18–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Salzburg Festival: Top-class classical music in Mozart's hometown.
    • Jul 21–Aug 22 (approx.) Festival Bregenz Festival (lake stage): Opera on a floating stage on Lake Constance.

  39. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Jun 15–Sep 30 Season Alpine hiking season: High-altitude trails and lifts are fully open.
    • Jul 2–17 (approx.) Festival Montreux Jazz Festival: The legendary jazz festival on the shore of Lake Geneva.

  40. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Jul 2–10 (approx.) Festival Karlovy Vary International Film Festival: International film festival in the Czech spa town.
    • Jul 5–6 Public holiday Saints Cyril & Methodius Day & Jan Hus Day: Two public holidays in a row.

  41. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Jul 30–Aug 1 Sports F1 Hungarian Grand Prix: Formula 1 at the Hungaroring near Budapest; easy to combine with the city.

  42. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • May 15–Sep 30 Festival Chopin concerts, Warsaw: Free open-air Chopin recitals by the Chopin Monument in Łazienki Park every summer Sunday.
    • Jun 25–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Jewish Culture Festival, Kraków: Jewish culture festival in Kazimierz, one of the largest in Europe.
    • Jul 24–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival St Dominic's Fair, Gdańsk: A summer fair dating back to the 13th century; the old town fills with stalls.

  43. Eastern Europe

  44. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Jul 24–26 (approx.) Festival Sighișoara Medieval Festival: Medieval carnival in Dracula's hometown citadel.

  45. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Jun 1–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season: Summer evening performances in the Roman theatre.
    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Varna Summer International Music Festival: Summer classical music festival in the Black Sea city of Varna.

  46. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Jul 9–11 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Maritime Days: A maritime festival in Tallinn harbour, with sailing ships and markets.
    • Jul 15–25 (approx.) Festival Pärnu Music Festival: Summer music festival in the seaside town of Pärnu.

  47. Southern Europe

  48. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Jun 12–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Verona Opera Festival: Opera under the stars in Verona's Roman arena.
    • Jul 2 Festival Palio di Siena: Bareback horse race on the Piazza del Campo, run again on 16 August.
    • Jul 17–18 (approx.) Festival Festa del Redentore, Venice: Fireworks over St Mark's Basin on the third weekend of July, with locals picnicking in boats.

  49. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Jul 6–14 Festival San Fermín (Running of the Bulls), Pamplona: San Fermín, with bull runs at 8 a.m. each morning.

  50. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Jul 15–31 (approx.) Festival Óbidos Medieval Market: Medieval market and jousting in the walled town.

  51. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.
    • Jun 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Athens Epidaurus Festival: Summer performances at the Odeon of Herodes Atticus and the ancient theatre of Epidaurus.

  52. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • Jul 9–11 (approx.) Festival Ultra Europe, Split: A big electronic music festival in Split, followed by a week of boat parties.
    • Jul 10–Aug 25 Festival Dubrovnik Summer Festival: Theatre and music in the old town.
    • Jul 14–Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Split Summer Festival: Opera and theatre in Diocletian's Palace.

  53. Northern Europe

  54. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • May 20–Jul 22 Season Midnight sun: 24-hour daylight north of the Arctic Circle.

  55. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jul 2–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Savonlinna Opera Festival: Opera festival inside the medieval Olavinlinna Castle.

  56. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Jun 27–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Roskilde Festival: The biggest music festival in the Nordics.
    • Jul 2–11 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Jazz Festival: Over a hundred jazz gigs across the city.

  57. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • May 1–Aug 15 Season Puffin season: Puffins can be seen in the Westfjords and the Westman Islands.
    • May 25–Jul 20 Season Midnight sun: Almost no darkness in summer; perfect for a Ring Road trip.
    • Jul 30–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Þjóðhátíð, Westman Islands: A huge bonfire and music festival in the Westman Islands.

  58. North America

  59. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Jun 11–Jul 19 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (USA, Canada & Mexico): The first 48-team World Cup, hosted by 16 cities: 11 in the US plus Canada and Mexico; the final is in New York/New Jersey.
    • Jul 4 Public holiday Independence Day: Fireworks nationwide; Macy's show in New York is the biggest.
    • Jul 22–25 (approx.) Festival San Diego Comic-Con: The world's biggest comics and pop culture convention.

  60. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Jun 12–Jul 7 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (Toronto & Vancouver): Canada hosts 13 matches in Toronto and Vancouver.
    • Jun 25–Jul 3 (approx.) Festival Festival International de Jazz de Montréal: One of the world's biggest jazz festivals, with lots of free outdoor shows.
    • Jul 1 Public holiday National Day: Celebrations on Parliament Hill in Ottawa.
    • Jul 9–18 Festival Calgary Stampede: The world's biggest rodeo and parade.

  61. Latin America

  62. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Jun 11–Jul 5 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (Mexico): The opening match is at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City, with Guadalajara and Monterrey also hosting.
    • Jul 20–27 (approx.) Festival Guelaguetza, Oaxaca: Indigenous dance festival on the last two Mondays of July.

  63. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jun 24–Jul 25 Sports FIFA Women's World Cup: The first Women's World Cup in South America, hosted by eight cities including Rio and São Paulo.
    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Pantanal dry season: In the dry season animals gather at the water; the best chance to see jaguars.
    • Jul 28–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Paraty International Literary Festival (FLIP): International literary festival in the colonial town of Paraty.

  64. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season Machu Picchu dry season: The Inca Trail closes for maintenance every February.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.
    • Jul 23–Aug 8 Sports Pan American Games, Lima: The Americas' biggest multi-sport games, Lima's second time hosting.
    • Jul 28–29 Public holiday Fiestas Patrias (national holidays): National Day on 28–29 July, peak travel time for Peruvians; book Machu Picchu tickets even earlier.

  65. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Jul 9 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 9 July.

  66. Africa

  67. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Jul 1 Public holiday June 30 Revolution Day: Public holiday.
    • Jul 23 Public holiday July 23 Revolution Day: National Day.

  68. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Jul 30 Public holiday Throne Day: 30 July marks the King's accession.

  69. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Jun 24–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival National Arts Festival, Makhanda: South Africa's National Arts Festival in the Eastern Cape, one of Africa's largest.
    • Jul 2–11 (approx.) Festival Knysna Oyster Festival: An oyster and sports festival in Knysna on the Garden Route.

  70. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Great Migration: Wildebeest cross the Mara River, most spectacularly in August.

  71. Oceania

  72. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.

July travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in July?

Highlights in July 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); Chopin concerts, Warsaw (Poland, May 15–Sep 30); Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season (Bulgaria, Jun 1–Sep 15); Athens Epidaurus Festival (Greece, Jun 1–Aug 31); Da Nang International Fireworks Festival (Vietnam, Jun 1–Jul 11); Great Singapore Sale (Singapore, Jun 1–Jul 31); Jeddah Season (Saudi Arabia, Jun 1–Jul 31); FIFA World Cup (USA, Canada & Mexico) (United States, Jun 11–Jul 19 (2026)); FIFA World Cup (Mexico) (Mexico, Jun 11–Jul 5 (2026)); Verona Opera Festival (Italy, Jun 12–Sep 5), among 84 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in July 2027?

There are 21 public holidays or long weekends, including HKSAR Establishment Day in Hong Kong (Jul 1); National Day in Canada (Jul 1); June 30 Revolution Day in Egypt (Jul 1); Independence Day in United States (Jul 4); Saints Cyril & Methodius Day & Jan Hus Day in Czechia (Jul 5–6); Independence Day in Argentina (Jul 9); National Day (Naadam holidays) in Mongolia (Jul 10–15); Bastille Day in France (Jul 14); Democracy and National Unity Day in Türkiye (Jul 15); Sultan's Birthday in Brunei (Jul 15); Constitution Day in South Korea (Jul 17–19); Asahna Bucha & Buddhist Lent Day in Thailand (Jul 18–20) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.

Where is in season in July?
