  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Apr 29–May 5 Public holiday Golden Week: Peak domestic travel; flights and hotels cost more, so it's best avoided.
    • May 3 Public holiday Constitution Memorial Day: Golden Week holidays.
    • May 4 Public holiday Greenery Day: Golden Week holidays.
    • May 5 Public holiday Children's Day: Carp streamers fly to wish boys good health.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Labour Day: A public holiday from 2026; businesses close.
    • May 5 Public holiday Children's Day: The busiest day at theme parks, zoos and family attractions.
    • May 13 Festival Lotus Lantern Festival (Buddha's Birthday): Lantern parade along Jongno in Seoul; Jogyesa Temple is hung with lotus lanterns.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Apr 1–May 5 (approx.) Festival Luoyang Peony Festival: Peonies bloom at Wangcheng Park and the National Peony Garden, best in mid-April; pair it with the Longmen Grottoes.
    • May 1–4 Public holiday May Day holiday: May Day Golden Week, one of the busiest domestic travel periods; sights cap visitors and prices rise.
    • May 15 (approx.) Sports Great Wall Marathon: A marathon on the Huangyaguan section of the Great Wall in Tianjin, famous for its steps.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Apr 15–May 31 (approx.) Festival Hakka Tung Blossom Festival: Tung blossoms cover the hills of Miaoli, Hsinchu and Taichung; best in late April and early May.
    • Apr 30–May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: A day off for workers; from 2026 civil servants also get the day off.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 13 Festival Cheung Chau Bun Festival: Piu Sik parade on Cheung Chau and the midnight bun-scrambling race.
    • May 13 Public holiday Buddha's Birthday: Same day as the Cheung Chau Bun Festival; expect long queues for the Cheung Chau ferry.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • May 20 Public holiday Buddha's Birthday: Ceremonies at monasteries; public holiday.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Labour Day: A holiday for private companies.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • May 4 Public holiday Coronation Day: Public holiday.
    • May 8–10 (approx.) Festival Rocket Festival (Bun Bang Fai): Home-made rockets are launched to bring the rains in Yasothon, on the second weekend of May.
    • May 20 Public holiday Visakha Bucha (Buddha's Birthday): The most important Buddhist holiday, with a nationwide alcohol ban.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Apr 30–May 3 Public holiday Reunification Day & Labour Day holidays: A 30 April–1 May long weekend, one of the busiest domestic travel periods; beach towns raise prices.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 15–31 (approx.) Festival Singapore International Festival of Arts: Singapore's largest performing arts festival, with theatre and outdoor shows.
    • May 17 Public holiday Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha): Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Vesak Day: Lanterns and animal releases at temples; Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery is the busiest.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: National holiday.
    • May 17 Public holiday Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha): National holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Vesak Day: Buddhist holiday with float processions at temples.
    • May 30–31 Festival Kaamatan harvest festival, Sabah: The Kadazandusun thank the rice spirit and crown a harvest queen.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 6 Public holiday Ascension Day: Public holiday.
    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday, often with extra collective leave days around it; domestic transport is crowded.
    • May 20 (approx.) Festival Vesak sky lanterns at Borobudur: Monks walk from Mendut Temple to Borobudur, and sky lanterns are released at night.

  14. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 15 Festival Pahiyas Festival, Lucban: Houses are decorated with colourful rice wafers, fruit and vegetables.
    • May 16 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.

  15. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 14 Public holiday King's Birthday: Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Visakha Bucha (Buddha's Birthday): Ceremonies at temples.
    • May 24 Public holiday Royal Ploughing Ceremony: The royal ploughing ceremony that forecasts the year's harvest.

  16. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • May 1–3 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 8–10 (approx.) Festival Boun Bang Fai rocket festival: A pre-monsoon rain-making festival where villages launch home-made bamboo rockets.

  17. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.
    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • May 20 Public holiday Full Moon of Kason (Buddha Day): Devotees water the bodhi trees at the pagodas.

  18. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • May 17 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Adha: Public holiday.
    • May 31 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.

  19. South Asia

  20. 🇮🇳 India

  22. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • May 19–20 (approx.) Public holiday Vesak: The most important Buddhist festival: Colombo is filled with lanterns and illuminated pandals, and alcohol sales are banned.
    • May 19–25 (approx.) Festival Vesak lantern displays: Illuminated pandals and lantern displays in Colombo during Vesak.

  23. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1 Public holiday Labour Day: Public holiday.

  26. Middle East
