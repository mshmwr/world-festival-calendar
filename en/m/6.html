  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Jun 3 (2026) Public holiday Local election day: A holiday for the national election; some shops stay open.
    • Jun 5–12 (approx.) Festival Gangneung Danoje Festival: On UNESCO's intangible heritage list: shamanic rites, mask plays, swinging and wrestling by the Namdaecheon in Gangneung.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Memorial Day: Honours those who died for the nation; sirens sound for a minute's silence at 10 a.m.

  3. 🇨🇳 China

    • Jun 9 Public holiday Dragon Boat Festival: Short Dragon Boat holiday with boat races in many places.
    • Jun 11–20 (approx.) Festival Shanghai International Film Festival: China's only "A-category" international film festival, with many public screenings.

  4. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Jun 7–9 (approx.) Festival Lukang Dragon Boat Festival & races: Dragon-boat races around the festival; Lukang holds its traditional Dragon King rites and performances.
    • Jun 9 Public holiday Dragon Boat Festival: Dragon-boat races around the island; Lukang and Erlong Village in Yilan are the most distinctive.

  5. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Jun 9 Public holiday Dragon Boat Festival: Dragon-boat races in many districts; Stanley and Tai O are the liveliest.
    • Jun 9 (approx.) Sports Stanley International Dragon Boat Championships: Dragon-boat racing at Stanley Main Beach on Dragon Boat Festival, with a lively atmosphere.

  6. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Children's Day: Public holiday with family events in Ulaanbaatar.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • Jun 3 Public holiday Queen Suthida's Birthday: Public holiday.
    • Jun 4–6 (approx.) Festival Hua Hin Jazz Festival: Jazz festival on the beach at Hua Hin, a seaside town with royal roots.
    • Jun 26–28 (approx.) Festival Phi Ta Khon (ghost festival): Parade of painted ghost masks in Dan Sai, Loei.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Jun 1–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Da Nang International Fireworks Festival: International fireworks teams compete over the Han River on Saturday nights.
    • Jun 1–6 (approx.) Festival Hue Festival: A big festival in even years; odd years host the smaller Hue Traditional Craft Festival.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Jun 1–Jul 31 (approx.) Festival Great Singapore Sale: Island-wide mall sales.

  11. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Jun 1–2 Festival Gawai Dayak, Sarawak: The Dayak harvest festival, with longhouses open to visitors.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Islamic New Year: National holiday.
    • Jun 7 Public holiday Agong's Birthday: National holiday.
    • Jun 26–28 (approx.) Festival Rainforest World Music Festival, Kuching: World music festival at the Sarawak Cultural Village.

  12. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • Jun 1 Public holiday Pancasila Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 6 Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jun 13–Jul 11 (approx.) Festival Bali Arts Festival: A month of dance and music at the Denpasar Arts Centre.

  13. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Jun 12 Public holiday Independence Day: Marks the declaration of independence in 1898.
    • Jun 24 Festival San Juan water-splashing festival: San Juan City in Manila's water festival, with people drenching each other in the streets.

  14. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Jun 18 Public holiday Queen Mother's Birthday: Public holiday.

  15. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Jun 6–7 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  16. South Asia

  17. 🇮🇳 India

    • Jun 16 (approx.) Public holiday Ashura: Islamic holiday.

  18. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season East coast high season: While the southwest has its rains, Trincomalee and Arugam Bay on the east coast are dry; peak surf season.
    • Jun 18 (approx.) Public holiday Poson: Marks the arrival of Buddhism; pilgrims flock to Anuradhapura and Mihintale.

  19. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  20. Middle East

  21. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Jun 1–30 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Music Festival: Classical music festival, with some concerts in historic venues such as Hagia Irene.

  22. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  23. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  24. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jun 14–15 (approx.) Public holiday Ashura: An important Shia day of mourning, with processions in old Manama and some roads closed.

  25. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Jun 1–Jul 31 (approx.) Festival Jeddah Season: Jeddah's summer entertainment season, with waterfront events and concerts.

  26. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.
    • Jun 15 Public holiday National Salvation Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 26–28 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.

  27. Western Europe

  28. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Jun 15–19 (approx.) Festival Royal Ascot: A royal race meeting where the hats steal the show.
    • Jun 23–27 (approx.) Festival Glastonbury Festival: Britain's biggest outdoor music festival; tickets sell out fast, and it takes a fallow year every few years.
    • Jun 28–Jul 11 Sports Wimbledon: The grass-court Grand Slam; queuing for same-day tickets is a tradition.

  29. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Jun 7 Public holiday June Bank Holiday: First Monday in June.
    • Jun 16 Festival Bloomsday, Dublin: Celebrates Joyce's Ulysses; people in 1904 dress retrace the book's route.

  30. 🇫🇷 France

    • May 23–Jun 6 (approx.) Sports French Open (Roland-Garros): The clay-court Grand Slam at Roland-Garros by the Bois de Boulogne.
    • Jun 20–Jul 25 Season Provence lavender season: The fields of the Valensole plateau peak in early July and vanish after harvest.
    • Jun 21 Festival Fête de la Musique: Free street music on the summer solstice all over France; every corner of Paris has a gig.

  31. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Apr 15–Sep 30 Season Windmill & Giethoorn high season: Good weather in spring and summer; the windmills and canal villages are at their busiest.
    • Jun 1–30 (approx.) Festival Holland Festival: Amsterdam's international performing arts festival.

  32. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Jun 4–6 Sports F1 Monaco Grand Prix: Formula 1's most iconic race, run on the city streets; in 2027 it hosts its first Sprint. Grandstand seats and hotels often sell out a year ahead.

  33. Central Europe

  34. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Jun 5 (approx.) Festival Heidelberg Castle illuminations: The castle is lit up with fireworks three times each summer (June, July, September).
    • Jun 19–27 (approx.) Festival Kiel Week: The world's biggest sailing event, with parades and music in Kiel harbour.

  35. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • May 15–Jun 20 (approx.) Festival Wiener Festwochen: Vienna's festival of theatre, music and dance.
    • Jun 25–27 (approx.) Festival Donauinselfest: Europe's biggest free open-air music festival.

  36. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Jun 15–Sep 30 Season Alpine hiking season: High-altitude trails and lifts are fully open.
    • Jun 17–20 (approx.) Festival Art Basel: The world's most important modern and contemporary art fair.

  37. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • May 12–Jun 3 Festival Prague Spring International Music Festival: Opens on 12 May, the anniversary of Smetana's death, with Má vlast.
    • Jun 19–21 (approx.) Festival Festival of the Five-Petalled Rose, Český Krumlov: The town dresses in medieval costume for jousting and markets.

  38. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • May 15–Sep 30 Festival Chopin concerts, Warsaw: Free open-air Chopin recitals by the Chopin Monument in Łazienki Park every summer Sunday.
    • Jun 25–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Jewish Culture Festival, Kraków: Jewish culture festival in Kazimierz, one of the largest in Europe.

  39. Eastern Europe

  40. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Jun 12–14 Public holiday Russia Day: National Day.
    • Jun 19 (approx.) Festival Sabantuy, Kazan: Tatar summer farming festival with wrestling, pole-climbing and other traditional contests.
    • Jun 27 (approx.) Festival White Nights & Scarlet Sails, St Petersburg: Scarlet-sailed ship and fireworks on the Neva during the White Nights.

  41. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Children's Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 4–13 (approx.) Festival Transilvania International Film Festival: International film festival in Cluj.
    • Jun 18–27 (approx.) Festival Sibiu International Theatre Festival: One of the world's three biggest performing arts festivals, with lots of street shows in Sibiu's old town.
    • Jun 20–21 Public holiday Orthodox Pentecost: Public holiday.

  42. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • May 15–Jun 10 Festival Rose Valley harvest: Roses are picked at dawn in the Kazanlak valley; the Rose Festival is in early June.
    • May 30–Jun 7 (approx.) Festival Rose Festival: Rose picking and parades in the Kazanlak Rose Valley on the first weekend of June.
    • Jun 1–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season: Summer evening performances in the Roman theatre.

  43. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Jun 4–7 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Old Town Days: Medieval market and performances.
    • Jun 23–24 Public holiday Midsummer (Jaanipäev): Bonfires and folk celebrations.

  44. Southern Europe

  45. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Jun 2 Public holiday Republic Day: Military parade and air display in Rome on 2 June.
    • Jun 12–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Verona Opera Festival: Opera under the stars in Verona's Roman arena.

  46. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Jun 12–14 (2026) Sports F1 Spanish Grand Prix (Barcelona): Formula 1 at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya; in 2027 the Spanish round moves to Madrid.
    • Jun 23 Festival Night of San Juan: Solstice bonfires with all-night fireworks on the beaches of Barcelona and Alicante.
    • Jun 29 Festival Haro Wine Battle: A red-wine battle in the Rioja town of Haro.

  47. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Jun 10 Public holiday Portugal Day: National Day on 10 June, the same week as Lisbon's Santo António festival.
    • Jun 12–13 Festival Santo António festival, Lisbon: Street parties with grilled sardines in Alfama.
    • Jun 18–20 Sports F1 Portuguese Grand Prix (Portimão): Formula 1 returns in 2027 to the rollercoaster Algarve circuit; pair it with the southern beaches.
    • Jun 23–24 Festival São João festival, Porto: All-night revelry where people tap each other on the head with plastic hammers for luck.

  48. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.
    • Jun 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Athens Epidaurus Festival: Summer performances at the Odeon of Herodes Atticus and the ancient theatre of Epidaurus.
    • Jun 21 Public holiday Whit Monday: Public holiday.

  49. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • Jun 22 Public holiday Anti-Fascist Struggle Day: Public holiday.

  50. Northern Europe

  51. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • May 20–Jul 22 Season Midnight sun: 24-hour daylight north of the Arctic Circle.
    • May 26–Jun 9 (approx.) Festival Bergen International Festival: One of the largest performing arts festivals in the Nordics.
    • Jun 23 Festival Midsummer bonfires (Sankthans): Bonfires by the sea for midsummer.

  52. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Jun 6 Public holiday National Day: Royal celebration at Skansen open-air museum in Stockholm on 6 June.
    • Jun 25–26 Public holiday Midsummer: Dancing round the maypole, flower crowns and pickled herring.

  53. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jun 25–26 Public holiday Midsummer: Lakeside bonfires; cities empty as everyone heads to their cottages.

  54. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Jun 23 Festival Midsummer bonfires (Sankthans): Midsummer bonfires by the sea where a witch effigy is burned.
    • Jun 27–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival Roskilde Festival: The biggest music festival in the Nordics.

  55. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • May 1–Aug 15 Season Puffin season: Puffins can be seen in the Westfjords and the Westman Islands.
    • May 25–Jul 20 Season Midnight sun: Almost no darkness in summer; perfect for a Ring Road trip.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Festival Seamen's Day: Harbour festivities on the first Sunday of June.
    • Jun 17 Public holiday National Day: Parades and concerts in Reykjavík.

  56. North America

  57. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Jun 11–Jul 19 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (USA, Canada & Mexico): The first 48-team World Cup, hosted by 16 cities: 11 in the US plus Canada and Mexico; the final is in New York/New Jersey.
    • Jun 18–19 Public holiday Juneteenth: Federal holiday marking the end of slavery.
    • Jun 27 (approx.) Festival NYC Pride March: The big march on the last Sunday of June, with rainbow flags all over the city.

  58. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Jun 12–Jul 7 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (Toronto & Vancouver): Canada hosts 13 matches in Toronto and Vancouver.
    • Jun 25–Jul 3 (approx.) Festival Festival International de Jazz de Montréal: One of the world's biggest jazz festivals, with lots of free outdoor shows.

  59. Latin America

  60. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Jun 11–Jul 5 (2026) Sports FIFA World Cup (Mexico): The opening match is at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City, with Guadalajara and Monterrey also hosting.

  61. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jun 1–30 (approx.) Festival Festa Junina: Country-style festivals all through June; biggest in Campina Grande in the northeast.
    • Jun 24–Jul 25 Sports FIFA Women's World Cup: The first Women's World Cup in South America, hosted by eight cities including Rio and São Paulo.
    • Jun 26–28 (approx.) Festival Parintins Folklore Festival: A giant ox-puppet contest in a town on the Amazon.

  62. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season Machu Picchu dry season: The Inca Trail closes for maintenance every February.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.
    • Jun 7 Public holiday Flag Day: Public holiday.
    • Jun 24 Festival Inti Raymi: A re-enactment of the Inca sun ceremony at Sacsayhuamán, Cusco.
    • Jun 29 Public holiday Saints Peter and Paul Day: Public holiday.

  63. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Jun 15–Dec 10 (approx.) Season Península Valdés whale watching season: Southern right whales come to Península Valdés to breed. Boat trips run from Puerto Pirámides, Argentina's only authorised whale-watching port, from mid-June to early December. Sources: puertopiramides.gov.ar, argentina.travel
    • Jun 20–21 Public holiday Flag Day & General Güemes Day: Two memorial holidays in June, often making a long weekend.

  64. Africa

  65. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Mar 15–Jun 15 Season Red Sea diving season: Water temperatures rise and visibility is good at Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh.
    • Jun 10 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.

  66. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Jun 4–12 (approx.) Festival Fes Festival of World Sacred Music: World sacred music festival in the old city of Fes.
    • Jun 4–6 (approx.) Festival Cherry Festival, Sefrou: Cherry festival in Sefrou in the Middle Atlas, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • Jun 6 (approx.) Public holiday Islamic New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jun 24–26 (approx.) Festival Gnaoua World Music Festival, Essaouira: Gnaoua and world music festival in the Atlantic port of Essaouira.

  67. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Jun 16 Public holiday Youth Day: 16 June commemorates the Soweto uprising.
    • Jun 24–Jul 4 (approx.) Festival National Arts Festival, Makhanda: South Africa's National Arts Festival in the Eastern Cape, one of Africa's largest.

  68. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jun 1 Public holiday Madaraka Day: 1 June marks self-rule in 1963.

  69. Oceania

  70. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • May 21–Jun 12 (approx.) Festival Vivid Sydney: Light projections on the Opera House and light art around the harbour.
    • Jun 11–22 (approx.) Festival Dark Mofo, Hobart: An alternative arts festival around the winter solstice in Tasmania.

  71. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jun 7 Public holiday King's Birthday: First Monday of June, around the start of the ski season.
    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.
    • Jun 25–28 (approx.) Festival Queenstown Winter Festival: Celebrations to open the ski season.
    • Jun 25 Public holiday Matariki (Māori New Year): The rising of the Matariki star cluster, a public holiday since 2022.

June travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in June?

Highlights in June 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); Prague Spring International Music Festival (Czechia, May 12–Jun 3); Wiener Festwochen (Austria, May 15–Jun 20); Chopin concerts, Warsaw (Poland, May 15–Sep 30); Rose Valley harvest (Bulgaria, May 15–Jun 10); Vivid Sydney (Australia, May 21–Jun 12); French Open (Roland-Garros) (France, May 23–Jun 6); Bergen International Festival (Norway, May 26–Jun 9); Rose Festival (Bulgaria, May 30–Jun 7); Istanbul Music Festival (Türkiye, Jun 1–30), among 72 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in June 2027?

There are 46 public holidays or long weekends, including Children's Day in Mongolia (Jun 1); Children's Day in Romania (Jun 1); Madaraka Day in Kenya (Jun 1); Pancasila Day in Indonesia (Jun 1); Republic Day in Italy (Jun 2); Local election day in South Korea (Jun 3 (2026)); Queen Suthida's Birthday in Thailand (Jun 3); Islamic New Year in Maldives (Jun 6); Islamic New Year in UAE (Jun 6); Islamic New Year in Jordan (Jun 6); National Day in Sweden (Jun 6); Islamic New Year in Morocco (Jun 6) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.

Where is in season in June?
