  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Oct 20–Nov 30 Season Autumn leaves season: Starts in Hokkaido in early October; Kyoto is most colourful in mid-to-late November.
    • Nov 3 Public holiday Culture Day: Culture festivals are held across Japan.
    • Nov 15 Festival Shichi-Go-San: Children aged three, five and seven visit shrines in kimono.
    • Nov 23 Public holiday Labour Thanksgiving Day: Public holiday.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Oct 10–Nov 15 Season Autumn leaves season: Seoraksan turns first in mid-October; Naejangsan peaks in early November.
    • Nov 1–17 (approx.) Festival Seoul Lantern Festival: The whole length of Cheonggyecheon is hung with themed lanterns.
    • Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Busan Fireworks Festival: Fireworks over the sea in front of Gwangan Bridge; book viewing seats early.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Oct 1–Nov 30 Season Yangcheng Lake hairy crab season: Hairy crabs are at their best; restaurants in Shanghai and Suzhou serve crab banquets.
    • Oct 15–Nov 10 (approx.) Festival Fragrant Hills Red Leaves Festival, Beijing: The smoke trees of the Fragrant Hills turn red, best late October to early November; crowded at weekends.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Oct 1–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Sun Moon Lake Fireworks Music Festival: Weekend fireworks with concerts on the shore of Sun Moon Lake.
    • Nov 7–Dec 6 (approx.) Festival Xinshe Sea of Flowers: Vast flower fields in Xinshe, Taichung: Taiwan's biggest autumn-winter flower show.

  6. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Nov 26 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 29 Public holiday Genghis Khan's Birthday: A holiday set by the Mongolian calendar; the date changes every year.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Nov 1–Feb 28 Season Peak season (dry season): Cool and dry, the best time to travel, and the most expensive.
    • Nov 13–14 (approx.) Festival Loy Krathong & Yi Peng lantern festival, Chiang Mai: Floating krathong offerings; Chiang Mai holds the Yi Peng lantern festival at the same time.
    • Nov 14–15 (approx.) Festival Surin Elephant Round-up: Hundreds of elephants perform and parade on the third weekend of November.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.
    • Nov 24 Public holiday Vietnam Culture Day: A public holiday added in 2025.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Christmas on Orchard Road: Christmas on A Great Street: 3 km of lights.

  11. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Nov 1–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Malaysia Year-End Sale: Year-end sales season, with lights around Bukit Bintang in Kuala Lumpur.

  12. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.
    • Nov 1–2 Public holiday All Saints' & All Souls' Days: Families visit graves nationwide; cemeteries are busy through the night and traffic is heavy.
    • Nov 30 Public holiday Bonifacio Day: Public holiday.

  13. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.
    • Nov 9 Public holiday Independence Day: Celebrations at the Independence Monument in Phnom Penh.
    • Nov 12–14 (approx.) Festival Water Festival (Bon Om Touk): Dragon-boat races on the Tonle Sap River in Phnom Penh: Cambodia's biggest festival.

  14. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.
    • Nov 11–13 (approx.) Festival That Luang Festival: The most important Buddhist festival, at That Luang in Vientiane.

  15. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.
    • Nov 7–13 (approx.) Festival Taunggyi Balloon Festival: Hot-air balloons loaded with fireworks are launched at night.
    • Nov 13 Public holiday Tazaungdaing festival of lights: Coincides with the Taunggyi Balloon Festival; lights everywhere.
    • Nov 23 Public holiday National Day: Public holiday.

  16. South Asia

  17. 🇮🇳 India

    • Nov 7–14 (approx.) Festival Pushkar Camel Fair: A desert fair in Rajasthan where tens of thousands of camels are traded.
    • Nov 14 Public holiday Guru Nanak Jayanti: The Golden Temple in Amritsar is lit up for the occasion.

  18. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Oct 1–Nov 30 Season Trekking peak season: Clear skies after the monsoon give the best Himalayan views.
    • Nov 4 Public holiday Chhath Parva: The biggest festival of the southern plains, with sun worship at riverbanks.
    • Nov 10–12 (approx.) Festival Mani Rimdu: Masked dances at Tengboche Monastery in the Everest region, in trekking season.

  19. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Nov 3 Public holiday Victory Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.

  20. Middle East

  21. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Sep 11–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Biennial: Contemporary art biennial held in odd years.

  22. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Nov 18–23 (approx.) Festival Abu Dhabi Art: Art fair on Saadiyat Island.

  23. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Autumn in Jordan: Cool autumn weather; the Red Sea at Aqaba is still warm enough to swim.

  24. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C; from June to September temperatures often top 40°C.

  25. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Oct 20–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Riyadh Season: Riyadh's annual entertainment season, packed with concerts, boxing and themed zones, running into the new year.
    • Nov 1–Mar 15 Season Best time to visit: Winter days in Riyadh and AlUla are in the 20s°C and desert nights near 10°C; inland summers often exceed 45°C.

  26. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.

  27. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Nov 8 Public holiday Victory Day: Commemorates the 2020 Karabakh war; public holiday.
    • Nov 9 Public holiday Flag Day: Celebrations at National Flag Square in Baku.

  28. Western Europe

  29. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Nov 5 Festival Guy Fawkes Night: Fireworks and bonfires across Britain.
    • Nov 20–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Hyde Park Winter Wonderland: A Christmas fair in Hyde Park with a Ferris wheel, ice rink and markets.

  30. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Nov 25–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Dublin Christmas markets & lights: Lights on Grafton Street and Christmas markets.

  31. 🇫🇷 France

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday during the school autumn break.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Armistice Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 (approx.) Festival Beaujolais Nouveau Day: The new wine is released on the third Thursday of November, celebrated in bars everywhere.
    • Nov 27–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Strasbourg Christmas Market: France's oldest Christmas market and a must on Alsace Christmas tours.

  32. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Nov 13 (approx.) Festival Arrival of Sinterklaas: The Dutch Saint Nicholas arrives by boat, with parades in many towns.
    • Nov 26–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Amsterdam Light Festival: Light art along the canals, best seen from a night boat tour.

  33. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Armistice Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 27–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Brussels Christmas Market: Winter Wonders Christmas market and ice rink.

  34. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18–19 Festival Monaco National Day fireworks: Harbour fireworks on the eve of National Day and a palace ceremony on the 19th.
    • Nov 19 Public holiday National Day (Prince's Day): A ceremony on Palace Square on 19 November, when the princely family greet the crowds from the balcony.

  35. Central Europe

  36. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Nov 23–Dec 24 Festival Christmas markets: Nuremberg, Dresden and Cologne have the most famous markets.

  37. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 14–Dec 26 (approx.) Festival Vienna Christmas markets: Christmas markets at City Hall Square and Schönbrunn.

  38. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Zurich Christmas markets: The Swarovski Christmas tree and market inside Zurich main station.
    • Nov 22 (approx.) Festival Zibelemärit (onion market), Bern: Onion market on the fourth Monday of November; Bern's old town fills with braided onions.

  39. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Nov 17 Public holiday Struggle for Freedom and Democracy Day: Marks the 1989 Velvet Revolution.
    • Nov 28–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Prague Christmas markets: A giant Christmas tree and market on Old Town Square.

  40. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 13–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Budapest Christmas markets: Light show in front of St Stephen's Basilica.

  41. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Families return home to visit graves, and cemeteries glow with candles.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 11 November, with a parade in Warsaw.
    • Nov 27–Dec 26 (approx.) Festival Kraków Christmas market: Market on the Main Square and a nativity-crib (szopka) contest.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Warsaw Christmas market & lights: Lights along the Royal Route and the Old Town Square in Warsaw.

  42. Eastern Europe

  43. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Nov 4 Public holiday Unity Day: Public holiday.

  44. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Nov 27–Dec 27 (approx.) Festival Bucharest Christmas Market: Christmas market on Constitution Square.
    • Nov 30–Dec 1 Public holiday St Andrew's Day & National Day: Holidays on 30 November and 1 December, with a military parade in Bucharest.

  45. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Nov 12–28 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival: One of the largest film festivals in northern Europe.
    • Nov 21–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Christmas Market: Often rated one of Europe's most beautiful Christmas markets.

  46. Southern Europe

  47. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Oct 9–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Alba White Truffle Fair: White truffle fair in Alba, Piedmont, a highlight of autumn food travel.
    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 7 Public holiday National Unity Day: A public holiday in November.

  48. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Madrid Christmas markets & lights: Christmas market on Plaza Mayor and a parade on the eve of Three Kings.

  49. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 11 Festival St Martin's Day (roast chestnuts): A traditional day of roast chestnuts and new wine.
    • Nov 25–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Lisbon Christmas lights: A giant Christmas tree on Praça do Comércio and lights across the city.

  50. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.
    • Nov 4–14 (approx.) Festival Thessaloniki International Film Festival: Greece's most important film festival.
    • Nov 14 (approx.) Festival Athens Marathon (the Authentic): The original route from the town of Marathon to Athens' Panathenaic Stadium.

  51. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • Sep 15–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Truffle season (Istria): White truffle season in Istria, with a truffle festival in Motovun.
    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 Public holiday Vukovar Remembrance Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 28–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Advent in Zagreb: Repeatedly voted Europe's best Christmas market.

  52. Northern Europe

  53. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.
    • Nov 15–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Pepperkakebyen (gingerbread town), Bergen: The world's largest gingerbread town.

  54. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.
    • Nov 6 Public holiday All Saints' Day: A Saturday in early November when cemeteries glow with candles.
    • Nov 27–Dec 20 (approx.) Festival Skansen Christmas market: Traditional Christmas market at Skansen open-air museum.

  55. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.
    • Nov 6 Public holiday All Saints' Day: A Saturday in early November.
    • Nov 20–Jan 6 Festival Santa Claus Village high season: Rovaniemi, with husky and reindeer sleigh rides.

  56. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Tivoli Christmas season: Christmas lights at the century-old amusement park.

  57. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.
    • Nov 4–6 (approx.) Festival Iceland Airwaves: Reykjavík's music festival, a winter travel highlight.

  58. North America

  59. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Nov 7 (approx.) Sports New York City Marathon: The world's largest marathon, through all five boroughs with huge crowds.
    • Nov 11 Public holiday Veterans Day: Federal holiday; shops stay open.
    • Nov 18–20 Sports F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix: Formula 1 late on Saturday night on the Las Vegas Strip; hotels along the track are at their priciest.
    • Nov 25 Public holiday Thanksgiving & Macy's Parade: Macy's giant-balloon parade in New York; Black Friday follows the next day.
    • Nov 30–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in New York (Rockefeller Center tree): The Rockefeller Center tree lighting, Fifth Avenue windows and skating rinks.

  60. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Oct 15–Nov 20 Season Churchill polar bear season: Polar bears gather on the shore at Churchill, Manitoba, waiting for the sea to freeze.
    • Oct 30–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Banff Mountain Film Festival: International mountain and adventure film festival in Banff.
    • Nov 14–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Winter Festival of Lights, Niagara Falls: The falls are lit up at night with light displays.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.
    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Vancouver Christmas Market: A German-style Christmas market next to the Vancouver Convention Centre.

  61. Latin America

  62. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Oct 31–Nov 2 Festival Day of the Dead: Candles and marigolds in the cemeteries, and a big parade in Mexico City.
    • Nov 15 Public holiday Revolution Day: Third Monday of November, with a parade in Mexico City.
    • Nov 15–Mar 15 Festival Monarch butterfly season (Michoacán): Hundreds of millions of monarch butterflies winter in the forest, most in February; a World Heritage reserve.
    • Nov 27–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Guadalajara International Book Fair: The largest book fair in the Spanish-speaking world.

  63. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026) (approx.) Festival São Paulo Biennial: International contemporary art biennial in even years (next in 2028).
    • Oct 24–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Natal Luz, Gramado: A three-month Christmas festival in a German-heritage town in the south.
    • Nov 2 Public holiday All Souls' Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 5–7 Sports F1 São Paulo Grand Prix: Formula 1 at Interlagos, South America's only F1 round.
    • Nov 15 Public holiday Republic Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 20 Public holiday Black Consciousness Day: A national holiday since 2024.

  64. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • Nov 1 Public holiday All Saints' Day: Public holiday.

  65. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.
    • Nov 6–14 (approx.) Festival Fiesta de la Tradición (gaucho festival), San Antonio de Areco: A celebration of gaucho traditions, with Argentine cowboy shows.
    • Nov 9 (2026) Public holiday Papal visit holiday: A special holiday declared for the visit of Pope Leo XIV.
    • Nov 11–20 (approx.) Festival Mar del Plata International Film Festival: Latin America's only "A-category" international film festival.
    • Nov 20 Public holiday National Sovereignty Day: A public holiday in November.

  66. Africa

  67. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.
    • Nov 12–21 (approx.) Festival Cairo International Film Festival: The oldest international film festival in the Middle East.

  68. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.
    • Nov 6 Public holiday Green March Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 18 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 18 November.
    • Nov 26–Dec 4 (approx.) Festival Marrakech International Film Festival: North Africa's most important film festival.

  69. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Oct 1–Nov 15 Season Jacaranda season (Pretoria): Tens of thousands of jacarandas bloom and turn the capital purple.
    • Oct 2–Nov 21 (approx.) Sports Cricket World Cup: The one-day Cricket World Cup co-hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia, with the final in Johannesburg.
    • Nov 4 (2026) Public holiday Local election day: A holiday for election day.

  70. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Nov 1–Dec 15 Season Short rains season: Brief showers; safaris still run and prices are lower than in peak season.
    • Nov 19–22 (approx.) Festival Lamu Cultural Festival: Traditional Swahili dhow races and donkey races.

  71. Oceania

  72. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Oct 1–Nov 13 Sports Rugby World Cup: The first 24-team men's Rugby World Cup, opening in Perth with the final in Sydney.
    • Oct 22–Nov 8 (approx.) Festival Sculpture by the Sea, Bondi: A huge sculpture exhibition on the coastal walk from Bondi to Tamarama.
    • Nov 2 Sports Melbourne Cup: "The race that stops a nation"; a holiday in Victoria.

  73. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Spring in New Zealand (lambs & blossoms): Lambs are born on the farms and Christchurch's gardens bloom.

November travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in November?

Highlights in November 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); São Paulo Biennial (Brazil, Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026)); Istanbul Biennial (Türkiye, Sep 11–Nov 7); Truffle season (Istria) (Croatia, Sep 15–Nov 30); Sun Moon Lake Fireworks Music Festival (Taiwan, Oct 1–Nov 30); Rugby World Cup (Australia, Oct 1–Nov 13); Cricket World Cup (South Africa, Oct 2–Nov 21); Alba White Truffle Fair (Italy, Oct 9–Dec 5); Fragrant Hills Red Leaves Festival, Beijing (China, Oct 15–Nov 10); Global Village, Dubai (UAE, Oct 20–May 5), among 77 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in November 2027?

There are 49 public holidays or long weekends, including All Saints' Day in France (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Belgium (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Austria (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Hungary (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Poland (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Italy (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Spain (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Portugal (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Croatia (Nov 1); All Saints' Day in Peru (Nov 1); All Saints' & All Souls' Days in Philippines (Nov 1–2); All Saints' Day in Monaco (Nov 1) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.
