Events in September 2027 by country

  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Sep 19–Oct 4 (2026) Sports Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games: The 20th Asian Games, centred on Nagoya; Japan's third time hosting. The next edition is in Doha, Qatar, in 2030.
    • Sep 20 Public holiday Respect for the Aged Day: Third Monday of September.
    • Sep 22 (2026) Public holiday Citizens' Holiday (Silver Week): Falls between Respect for the Aged Day and the Autumnal Equinox, making a five-day weekend in 2026.
    • Sep 23 Public holiday Autumnal Equinox Day: Autumn equinox week, a time for visiting family graves.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Sep 14–16 Public holiday Chuseok: One of Korea's biggest holidays; Gyeongbokgung and other palaces often open free.
    • Sep 25–Oct 4 (approx.) Festival Andong International Mask Dance Festival: Traditional Hahoe mask dances plus mask performances from around the world.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Sep 11–19 Sports World Athletics Championships, Beijing: Held at Beijing's National Stadium (Bird's Nest); Beijing's second time hosting.
    • Sep 15 Public holiday Mid-Autumn Festival: Mid-Autumn holiday, often merged with National Day.
    • Sep 16–20 (approx.) Festival Qiantang River Tidal Bore Festival: The tidal bore peaks around the 18th of the 8th lunar month; watch the "single-line tide" at Yanguan, Haining.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Sep 15 Public holiday Mid-Autumn Festival: Barbecues and moon-viewing all over Taiwan.
    • Sep 19 (approx.) Sports Sun Moon Lake International Swimming Carnival: A mass swim across Sun Moon Lake around Mid-Autumn, with tens of thousands taking part.
    • Sep 28 Public holiday Teachers' Day: Restored as a public holiday from 2025.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Sep 14–16 Festival Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance: Over three Mid-Autumn nights, a dragon studded with incense sticks winds through Tai Hang.
    • Sep 16 Public holiday Day after Mid-Autumn Festival: Big lantern displays in Victoria Park and elsewhere on Mid-Autumn night; the next day is a holiday.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • Sep 30–Oct 8 (approx.) Festival Phuket Vegetarian Festival: From the 1st to the 9th of the 9th lunar month, devotees parade with face-piercing rituals.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Sep 1–Oct 10 Season Golden rice season, Sapa & Mu Cang Chai: The terraces turn golden before the harvest.
    • Sep 2 Public holiday National Day: Celebrations at Ba Dinh Square in Hanoi; busy holiday travel.
    • Sep 15 Festival Hoi An Mid-Autumn Lantern Festival: Lanterns fill the old town and floating lanterns drift on the Thu Bon River.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Aug 22–Sep 20 (approx.) Festival Chinatown Mid-Autumn lights: The whole street is hung with themed lanterns, with mooncake markets.
    • Sep 25–Oct 31 (approx.) Festival Deepavali lights, Little India: For over a month before Deepavali, Serangoon Road in Little India is strung with lights.

  11. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Sep 16 Public holiday Malaysia Day: Marks the formation of Malaysia in 1963.
    • Sep 30–Oct 8 (approx.) Festival Nine Emperor Gods Festival: From the 1st to the 9th of the 9th lunar month, Nine Emperor Gods temples in Penang and Kuala Lumpur hold vegetarian fasts, processions and fire-walking.

  12. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • Jul 1–Sep 30 (approx.) Season Toraja funeral season: The Toraja of Sulawesi hold their great funerals mostly in the dry season; visits can be arranged through local guides.

  13. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Sep 17–25 (approx.) Festival Peñafrancia Festival, Naga: The Virgin's feast in Naga, southern Luzon; a fluvial procession draws hundreds of thousands of pilgrims.

  14. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Sep 21–22 Festival Equinox sunrise at Angkor Wat (September): The twice-yearly sight of the sun crowning the tower.
    • Sep 24 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.
    • Sep 29–Oct 1 (approx.) Public holiday Pchum Ben: A festival for the ancestors, with temple offerings; many people go home.

  15. South Asia

  16. 🇮🇳 India

    • Sep 4–14 (approx.) Festival Ganesh Chaturthi: Biggest in Mumbai, where Ganesh idols are immersed in the sea on the last day.
    • Sep 12 (approx.) Festival Onam, Kerala: Kerala's biggest harvest festival, with flower carpets, feasts and snake-boat races.

  17. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Sep 15–22 (approx.) Festival Indra Jatra: The living goddess Kumari's chariot is pulled through Kathmandu Durbar Square.
    • Sep 19 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.
    • Sep 30–Oct 14 (approx.) Festival Dashain: Nepal's biggest festival, lasting 15 days.

  18. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season East coast high season: While the southwest has its rains, Trincomalee and Arugam Bay on the east coast are dry; peak surf season.

  19. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Sep 2 (approx.) Public holiday Day the Maldives Embraced Islam: A public holiday set by the Islamic calendar.

  20. Central Asia

  21. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Aug 15–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Melon season (Kovun Sayli): Late-summer melons and grapes; markets are at their most abundant.
    • Sep 1 Public holiday Independence Day: Celebrations and fireworks in Tashkent.

  22. Middle East

  23. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Sep 1–Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Göreme grape harvest season: Grape harvest in Cappadocia; wineries open for tastings.
    • Sep 11–Nov 7 (approx.) Festival Istanbul Biennial: Contemporary art biennial held in odd years.

  24. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Autumn in Jordan: Cool autumn weather; the Red Sea at Aqaba is still warm enough to swim.

  25. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Sep 23 Public holiday National Day: Green lights, fireworks and drone shows across the country on 23 September.

  26. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.
    • Sep 24–26 Sports F1 Azerbaijan Grand Prix (Baku): Formula 1 on a street circuit beside Baku's old city, racing on Saturday; it skirts the city walls and has a very long straight.

  27. Western Europe

  28. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Sep 18–26 (approx.) Festival London Design Festival: Design shows and installations across the city.

  29. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Sep 1–30 (approx.) Festival Lisdoonvarna Matchmaking Festival: Traditional matchmaking festival in a western village, with live music and dancing.

  30. 🇫🇷 France

    • Sep 10–Oct 15 (approx.) Festival Bordeaux grape harvest: Harvest time at the châteaux; some offer hands-on visits.

  31. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Apr 15–Sep 30 Season Windmill & Giethoorn high season: Good weather in spring and summer; the windmills and canal villages are at their busiest.

  32. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Sep 3–5 (approx.) Festival Belgian Beer Weekend, Brussels: Belgian beer festival on the Grand-Place.

  33. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Sep 22–25 (approx.) Festival Monaco Yacht Show: The world's top superyacht show, filling Port Hercule with giant yachts.

  34. Central Europe

  35. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Aug 26–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Stuttgart Wine Village: Wine festival on Stuttgart's market square.
    • Sep 18–Oct 3 Festival Oktoberfest, Munich: The world's biggest beer festival; book tent seats early.
    • Sep 26 (approx.) Sports Berlin Marathon: A flat course where the men's world record has been broken many times; finishes through the Brandenburg Gate.

  36. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Sep 15–Oct 31 (approx.) Festival Wachau wine festivals: Grape harvest season in the Wachau section of the Danube valley.

  37. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Jun 15–Sep 30 Season Alpine hiking season: High-altitude trails and lifts are fully open.
    • Sep 15–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Alpine cattle descent (Alpabzug): Cows wearing flower crowns and bells come down from the high pastures, with village markets.

  38. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Sep 28 Public holiday Czech Statehood Day (St Wenceslas): Public holiday.

  39. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Sep 9–12 (approx.) Festival Budapest Wine Festival: Wine festival in Buda Castle.

  40. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • May 15–Sep 30 Festival Chopin concerts, Warsaw: Free open-air Chopin recitals by the Chopin Monument in Łazienki Park every summer Sunday.

  41. Eastern Europe

  42. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Sep 4–5 (approx.) Festival Moscow City Day: Concerts and fireworks across the city on the first weekend of September.

  43. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Jun 1–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season: Summer evening performances in the Roman theatre.
    • Sep 6 Public holiday Unification Day: Public holiday.
    • Sep 22 Public holiday Independence Day: Public holiday.

  44. Southern Europe

  45. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Jun 12–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Verona Opera Festival: Opera under the stars in Verona's Roman arena.
    • Sep 1–11 (approx.) Festival Venice Film Festival: One of the world's top three film festivals, held on the Lido.
    • Sep 3–5 Sports F1 Italian Grand Prix (Monza): Formula 1 at Monza near Milan, Ferrari's home crowd; fans flood the track after the race.
    • Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Regata Storica, Venice: Historic regatta and parade on the Grand Canal on the first Sunday of September.

  46. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Sep 10–12 Sports F1 Madrid Grand Prix: Formula 1 on Madrid's new IFEMA street circuit, from 2026.
    • Sep 20–24 (approx.) Festival La Mercè, Barcelona: Barcelona's biggest festival, with human towers, giants and fire runs.

  47. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Sep 10–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Port wine harvest (Douro Valley): Grape harvest in the Douro Valley, with grape-treading at the quintas.

  48. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.

  49. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • Sep 15–Nov 30 (approx.) Festival Truffle season (Istria): White truffle season in Istria, with a truffle festival in Motovun.

  50. Northern Europe

  51. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Sep 3–5 (approx.) Festival Bergen Food Festival: Food festival on Bergen's waterfront.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  52. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.

  53. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.

  54. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Aug 27–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Aarhus Festival: Denmark's biggest city culture festival.

  55. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.

  56. North America

  57. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Aug 29–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Burning Man: A large arts community gathering in Nevada's Black Rock Desert; tickets are limited.
    • Aug 30–Sep 12 (approx.) Sports US Open (tennis): The final Grand Slam of the year at Flushing Meadows, New York; the night sessions are electric.
    • Sep 1–30 (approx.) Festival Aloha Festivals, Honolulu: Hawaiian culture festival with floral parades and block parties.
    • Sep 6 Public holiday Labour Day: First Monday of September, the last long weekend of summer.

  58. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Sep 6 Public holiday Labour Day: First Monday of September.
    • Sep 9–19 (approx.) Festival Toronto International Film Festival: One of North America's most important film festivals.
    • Sep 20–Oct 20 Season Autumn leaves season: The Maple Leaf Route from Québec to Niagara is at its reddest.

  59. Latin America

  60. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Sep 15–16 Public holiday Independence Day: On the night of the 15th the president gives the Cry of Independence on the Zócalo.

  61. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Pantanal dry season: In the dry season animals gather at the water; the best chance to see jaguars.
    • Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026) (approx.) Festival São Paulo Biennial: International contemporary art biennial in even years (next in 2028).
    • Sep 7 Public holiday Independence Day: Military parade in Brasília on 7 September.

  62. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season Machu Picchu dry season: The Inca Trail closes for maintenance every February.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.

  63. Africa

  64. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Aug 1–Sep 30 Season Namaqualand wildflower season: The desert bursts into wildflowers almost overnight.
    • Sep 3–5 (approx.) Festival FNB Art Joburg: One of Africa's most important contemporary art fairs.
    • Sep 24 Public holiday Heritage Day: 24 September, also known as Braai Day.

  65. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Great Migration: Wildebeest cross the Mara River, most spectacularly in August.

  66. Oceania

  67. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Sep 3–24 (approx.) Festival Brisbane Festival: Arts festival on the Brisbane River, opening with fireworks over the water.
    • Sep 11–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival Floriade, Canberra: Canberra's spring flower show, one of the largest in the southern hemisphere.

  68. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.
    • Sep 15–Nov 15 Season Spring in New Zealand (lambs & blossoms): Lambs are born on the farms and Christchurch's gardens bloom.
    • Sep 23–Oct 10 (approx.) Festival World of WearableArt, Wellington: An international wearable-art design competition and stage show.

September travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in September?

Highlights in September 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); Chopin concerts, Warsaw (Poland, May 15–Sep 30); Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season (Bulgaria, Jun 1–Sep 15); Verona Opera Festival (Italy, Jun 12–Sep 5); Melon season (Kovun Sayli) (Uzbekistan, Aug 15–Sep 15); Chinatown Mid-Autumn lights (Singapore, Aug 22–Sep 20); Stuttgart Wine Village (Germany, Aug 26–Sep 6); Aarhus Festival (Denmark, Aug 27–Sep 5); Burning Man (United States, Aug 29–Sep 6); US Open (tennis) (United States, Aug 30–Sep 12), among 57 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in September 2027?

There are 24 public holidays or long weekends, including Independence Day in Uzbekistan (Sep 1); National Day in Vietnam (Sep 2); Day the Maldives Embraced Islam in Maldives (Sep 2); Unification Day in Bulgaria (Sep 6); Labour Day in United States (Sep 6); Labour Day in Canada (Sep 6); Independence Day in Brazil (Sep 7); Chuseok in South Korea (Sep 14–16); Mid-Autumn Festival in China (Sep 15); Mid-Autumn Festival in Taiwan (Sep 15); Independence Day in Mexico (Sep 15–16); Day after Mid-Autumn Festival in Hong Kong (Sep 16) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.

Where is in season in September?
