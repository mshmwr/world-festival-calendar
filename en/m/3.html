  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Mar 3 Festival Hinamatsuri (Doll Festival): Hina dolls are displayed to wish girls good health.
    • Mar 7 Sports Tokyo Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors; ballot entries open around August. In 2027 it also hosts the Age Group World Championships.
    • Mar 20–Apr 15 Season Cherry blossom season: The bloom moves north from Kyushu; Tokyo and Kyoto peak around late March to early April.
    • Mar 21 Public holiday Vernal Equinox Day: Spring equinox week, a time for visiting family graves.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Mar 1 Public holiday Independence Movement Day: Commemorates the 1919 March First independence movement, with events nationwide.
    • Mar 27–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival: Korea's biggest cherry blossom festival; Yeojwacheon's Romance Bridge is the classic photo spot.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Mar 15–Apr 10 (approx.) Festival Shanghai Peach Blossom Festival: Peach orchards bloom around Nanhui in Pudong; a favourite spring outing.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Feb 20–Mar 7 (approx.) Festival Taiwan Lantern Festival: Taiwan's national lantern festival from Lantern Festival day, held in a different city each year, with a projection show on the main lantern at night.
    • Feb 28–Mar 1 Public holiday Peace Memorial Day: The 2/28 long weekend; domestic trips and flights abroad cost more.
    • Nov 15–Apr 30 (approx.) Season Dahu strawberry season: Pick-your-own strawberry farms in Dahu, Miaoli, open from November to April. Quality peaks from mid-December to Lunar New Year; from February there is more fruit and prices drop. Sources: travel.yam.com, bella.tw

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Mar 25–27 (approx.) Festival Art Basel Hong Kong: Asia's biggest art fair at the Wan Chai convention centre, with gallery events around town.
    • Mar 26–28 (approx.) Sports Hong Kong Sevens: Held at the Kai Tak main stadium, famous for its fancy-dress party in the stands.
    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: The Easter long weekend is peak time for Hongkongers travelling abroad.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Mar 2–3 (approx.) Festival Khövsgöl Ice Festival: Ice sculptures, sledding and reindeer-herder traditions on frozen Lake Khövsgöl.
    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Mar 13–15 (approx.) Festival Pattaya International Music Festival: Big free concerts on Pattaya Beach.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Feb 11–Apr 20 (approx.) Festival Perfume Pagoda Festival: The pilgrimage season at the Perfume Pagoda outside Hanoi, reached by small boats.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Mar 10 Public holiday Hari Raya Puasa (Eid al-Fitr): Ramadan bazaar and lights around Geylang Serai.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: Public holiday.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan bazaars: Ramadan bazaars open every evening; Kampung Baru in Kuala Lumpur is the liveliest.
    • Mar 10–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: End of Ramadan; Muslim families hold open houses and many travel home.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Mar 7 (approx.) Festival Ogoh-ogoh parade: On the eve of Nyepi, villages parade giant ogoh-ogoh effigies and then burn them; the next day the island falls silent.
    • Mar 8 (approx.) Public holiday Nyepi (Bali Day of Silence): The whole island goes silent for 24 hours: the airport closes and visitors must stay in; giant ogoh-ogoh effigies parade the night before.
    • Mar 10–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Nationwide travel home; transport is tight for a week either side.
    • Mar 26–28 Public holiday Easter holidays: Good Friday and Easter.

  14. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 21–28 Public holiday Holy Week: The most important holiday in this Catholic country; many businesses close.
    • Mar 21–28 (approx.) Festival Moriones Festival, Marinduque: During Holy Week, islanders wear Roman soldier masks and re-enact Bible stories.
    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.

  15. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 20–21 Festival Equinox sunrise at Angkor Wat (March): The sun rises right over the central tower; it happens again around the 22 September equinox.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.

  16. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.

  17. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Mar 2 Public holiday Peasants' Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 22 Public holiday Full Moon of Tabaung: The pagoda festival, celebrated at Shwedagon.
    • Mar 27 Public holiday Armed Forces Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.

  18. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Mar 10–13 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • Mar 12–14 (approx.) Festival Istana Nurul Iman open house (Hari Raya): Istana Nurul Iman opens for three days and visitors can shake hands with the Sultan.

  19. South Asia

  20. 🇮🇳 India

    • Mar 6 Public holiday Maha Shivaratri: Hindu festival with all-night pilgrimages to holy sites such as Varanasi.
    • Mar 10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: National holiday; the area around Delhi's Jama Masjid is lively.
    • Mar 22 Festival Holi: Coloured-powder revelry; most traditional in Mathura and Vrindavan.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: National holiday.

  21. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Mar 6 Public holiday Maha Shivaratri: Crowds of sadhus and devotees at Pashupatinath Temple; a national holiday.
    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: A holiday for women.
    • Mar 10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 21–22 Public holiday Holi (Fagu Purnima): The festival of coloured powder; liveliest in Kathmandu Durbar Square.

  22. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Southwest coast high season: Dry season in Galle and Mirissa, and whale-watching season.

  23. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.

  24. Central Asia

  25. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr (Ramazon Hayit): Public holiday.
    • Mar 18–24 (approx.) Festival Navruz fairs: Around Navruz, Samarkand and Bukhara cook the traditional sweet sumalak, with fairs.
    • Mar 21–22 Public holiday Navruz: Spring-equinox new year, with traditional fairs and shows in Samarkand and Bukhara.

  26. Middle East

  27. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan Bayram (Eid al-Fitr): Şeker Bayramı; transport and sights are crowded.

  28. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: No eating or drinking in public by day; restaurants keep shorter hours and iftar markets open at night.
    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan nights & iftars: Hotels host iftar and suhoor during Ramadan; avoid eating in public by day.
    • Mar 1–31 (approx.) Festival Dubai Art Season: Dubai's art season (Art Dubai and more) is concentrated in March.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Holiday at the end of Ramadan, with mall promotions and fireworks.
    • Mar 27 (approx.) Sports Dubai World Cup (horse racing): One of the world's richest race days, at Meydan Racecourse.
    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.

  29. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Best time to visit: Mild spring weather, good for hiking in Petra.
    • Mar 1–May 15 Season Spring in Jordan: The most comfortable season, ideal for Wadi Rum and hiking in Petra.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.

  30. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Mar 1–Apr 30 (approx.) Festival Spring of Culture, Bahrain: Bahrain's largest cultural festival, with international music and arts.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: A three-day public holiday; Saudi visitors drive over the causeway and weekend traffic is heavy.
    • Mar 12–14 Sports F1 Bahrain Grand Prix (Sakhir): Formula 1's 2027 season opener, a night race; the 2026 race was cancelled because of the Middle East conflict.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C; from June to September temperatures often top 40°C.

  31. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Mar 9–14 Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: About a week off after Ramadan; malls and sights are busy and some government offices close.
    • Mar 19–21 Sports F1 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix (Jeddah): Formula 1's night race on the Jeddah Corniche street circuit, one of the fastest of the year; the 2026 race was cancelled because of the Middle East conflict.
    • Nov 1–Mar 15 Season Best time to visit: Winter days in Riyadh and AlUla are in the 20s°C and desert nights near 10°C; inland summers often exceed 45°C.
    • Dec 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival AlUla winter (Winter at Tantora): Concerts, balloon flights and night tours of the ruins during AlUla's winter, with big shows at the Maraya.

  32. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Feb 8–Mar 12 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid events at Katara: Ramadan night market and Eid fireworks at Katara Cultural Village.
    • Mar 9–11 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday; government offices usually close for longer.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.

  33. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Mar 8 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday.
    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 20–26 Public holiday Novruz: Azerbaijan's most important holiday, about a week off, with bonfires, fire-jumping and sweet stalls in Baku's old city.

  34. Western Europe

  35. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A four-day weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; peak time for domestic trips.
    • Mar 27 (approx.) Sports The Boat Race (Oxford v Cambridge): The traditional rowing race on the Thames, watched by crowds along the banks.

  36. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: Public holiday.

  37. 🇫🇷 France

    • Feb 23–Mar 3 (approx.) Festival Paris Fashion Week (autumn/winter): Autumn/winter womenswear shows; lots of fashion events and pricier hotels.
    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: Public holiday.

  38. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

  39. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  40. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Mar 29 Public holiday Easter Monday: Public holiday.

  43. 🇦🇹 Austria

  44. 🇨🇿 Czechia

  46. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday; shops close.

  47. Eastern Europe

  48. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Mar 1–15 (approx.) Festival Lake Baikal ice festival: Lake Baikal's frozen season, with on-ice activities and ice caves.

  49. 🇷🇴 Romania

  50. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

  51. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Mar 26–28 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  52. Southern Europe

  53. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • Mar 28 Public holiday Easter (Vatican): The Pope celebrates Mass in St Peter's Square.

  54. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Mar 15–19 Festival Las Fallas, Valencia: Giant figures are all burned on the final night.
    • Mar 21–28 Festival Semana Santa (Holy Week): The religious processions in Seville and Málaga are the most spectacular.

  55. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Mar 26–28 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  56. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Mar 15 Public holiday Clean Monday: First day of Orthodox Lent: kite-flying and picnics nationwide.
    • Mar 25 Public holiday Independence Day: Military parade in Athens on 25 March.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.

  57. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • Mar 28–29 Public holiday Easter: Public holiday.

  58. Northern Europe

  59. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Feb 1–Apr 30 Festival Lofoten cod season: Cod-fishing season in Lofoten, with drying racks full of stockfish.
    • Mar 5–7 (approx.) Festival Holmenkollen Ski Festival: Classic ski-jumping event in Oslo.
    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday; Norwegians head to the mountains to ski and city shops mostly close.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  60. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.
    • Dec 10–Apr 10 (approx.) Season Icehotel open season: The Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is carved anew every year.

  61. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jan 20–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival SnowCastle of Kemi: The SnowCastle of Kemi and ice sculptures, rebuilt every year.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.
    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.
    • Dec 20–Apr 5 (approx.) Season Sampo icebreaker cruise from Kemi: Cruise on a retired icebreaker from Kemi and float in the icy sea in a survival suit. Runs from December to early April; walking on the ice may not be possible early in the season. Sources: visitfinland.com, experience365.fi

  62. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday; most shops close.

  63. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Mar 1 Festival Beer Day: Marks the end of beer prohibition in 1989, celebrated in bars.
    • Mar 25–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter Monday.
    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.

  64. North America

  65. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Mar 13 (approx.) Festival St Patrick's Day (Chicago River dyed green): Chicago dyes its river green and parades on the Saturday before 17 March; New York's Fifth Avenue parade is on the 17th.
    • Mar 20–Apr 12 (approx.) Festival National Cherry Blossom Festival, Washington DC: Cherry trees bloom around the Tidal Basin on the National Mall, with a Japanese culture parade.

  66. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Mar 1–Apr 15 Festival Québec sugar shack season: Maple brunches and maple taffy on snow at a cabane à sucre.
    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: National holiday; Easter Monday is not a holiday in most provinces.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.

  67. Latin America

  68. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Mar 6–27 (approx.) Festival Cancún spring break: US college spring breakers flood Cancún; hotel prices peak.
    • Mar 15 Public holiday Benito Juárez's Birthday: Third Monday of March.
    • Mar 25–26 (approx.) Festival Passion play of Iztapalapa, Mexico City: A huge Passion play re-enactment in Iztapalapa.
    • Nov 15–Mar 15 Festival Monarch butterfly season (Michoacán): Hundreds of millions of monarch butterflies winter in the forest, most in February; a World Heritage reserve.
    • Nov 15–Mar 31 (approx.) Season Monarch butterfly season: The monarch butterfly sanctuaries of Michoacán and the State of Mexico (El Rosario, Sierra Chincua and others) open from mid to late November until the end of March, when hundreds of millions of butterflies winter in the fir forests. Opening dates depend on when the butterflies arrive. Sources: mexicobusiness.news, mexiconewsdaily.com

  69. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Mar 26 Public holiday Good Friday: Public holiday.

  70. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • Mar 5–14 (approx.) Festival Grape Harvest Festival, Ica: Grape harvest festival in Ica, the home of pisco.
    • Mar 25–28 Public holiday Holy Week: Religious processions in Cusco from Maundy Thursday to Easter; peak domestic travel.

  71. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Mar 1–8 (approx.) Festival Vendimia (grape harvest festival), Mendoza: Parades and a harvest queen in the wine region.
    • Mar 1–May 31 Season Best time for Iguazú Falls: Plenty of water in autumn, cooler weather and fewer crowds.
    • Mar 24 Public holiday Day of Remembrance for Truth and Justice: 24 March remembers the victims of the military dictatorship, often bridged into a long weekend.
    • Mar 25–26 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Maundy Thursday to Easter.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.

  72. Africa

  73. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Festival Ramadan lanterns & night markets, Cairo: During Ramadan Cairo's streets are hung with fanous lanterns and the old-town night markets run late.
    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday lasting several days.
    • Mar 15–Jun 15 Season Red Sea diving season: Water temperatures rise and visibility is good at Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.

  74. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Feb 8–Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Ramadan: Most restaurants close by day and sights keep shorter hours.
    • Feb 8–Mar 10 (approx.) Festival Ramadan & Eid (Fes, Marrakech): Many restaurants close by day during Ramadan; markets come alive after the evening iftar.
    • Mar 9–10 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.

  75. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Feb 1–Mar 31 Festival Wine harvest (Stellenbosch): Grape harvest in the Cape Winelands, with harvest activities at the estates.
    • Mar 21–22 Public holiday Human Rights Day: Public holiday on 21 March.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: Good Friday and Family Day (Easter Monday).
    • Mar 26–27 (approx.) Festival Cape Town International Jazz Festival: Africa's largest jazz festival.
    • Mar 26–Apr 3 (approx.) Festival KKNK arts festival, Oudtshoorn: An Afrikaans arts festival in Oudtshoorn, the ostrich capital.

  76. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jan 1–Mar 15 Season Dry-season safari: The dry season in January–February concentrates animals near water; it's also calving season.
    • Mar 9 (approx.) Public holiday Eid al-Fitr: Public holiday.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday.

  77. Oceania

  78. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Feb 19–Mar 6 (approx.) Festival Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras: The big parade along Oxford Street in Sydney.
    • Feb 19–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Adelaide Fringe: The world's second-largest fringe festival.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A four-day weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; most shops close on Good Friday.
    • Mar 31–Apr 25 (approx.) Festival Melbourne International Comedy Festival: One of the world's three biggest comedy festivals.

  79. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Feb 25–Mar 21 (approx.) Festival Aotearoa New Zealand Festival of the Arts, Wellington: New Zealand's largest international arts festival, with theatre, dance and music in the capital.
    • Mar 13 (approx.) Festival Hokitika Wildfoods Festival: A quirky food festival of "wild foods" like insects.
    • Mar 26–29 Public holiday Easter holidays: A long weekend from Good Friday to Easter Monday; most shops close on Good Friday.
