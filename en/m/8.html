  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Aug 11 Public holiday Mountain Day: Public holiday leading into the Obon holidays.
    • Aug 13–16 Public holiday Obon: Families return home to honour ancestors; bon dances and farewell fires everywhere, with Kyoto's Gozan Okuribi on 8/16.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Aug 6–8 (approx.) Festival Incheon Pentaport Rock Festival: Korea's largest outdoor rock festival, at Songdo Moonlight Festival Park in Incheon.
    • Aug 15–16 Public holiday Liberation Day: Marks liberation from Japanese rule in 1945; flags fly everywhere.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Jul 17–Aug 10 (approx.) Festival Qingdao International Beer Festival: Asia's biggest beer festival, running for weeks at the Golden Beach beer city.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Taiwan International Balloon Festival, Taitung: Hot-air balloons rise over Luye Highland every morning, with light-show concerts at night.
    • Jul 3–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival Yilan International Children's Folklore & Folkgame Festival: Summer water play and international folk performances at Dongshan River Water Park.
    • Aug 2–31 (approx.) Festival Keelung Ghost Festival: Taiwan's best-known Ghost Festival; water lanterns are released at Badouzi on the 14th night of the 7th lunar month.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Aug 2–30 (approx.) Festival Yu Lan Ghost Festival of the Chiu Chow community: Rituals and Chinese opera in bamboo theatres during the 7th lunar month; on the national intangible heritage list.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Phuket rainy low season: Rainy season on the west coast brings big waves; many island trips stop and rooms are cheap.
    • Aug 12 Public holiday Mother's Day (Queen Mother Sirikit's Birthday): Public holiday.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Aug 16 (approx.) Festival Ghost Festival (Vu Lan): On the 15th of the 7th lunar month, with temple ceremonies and floating lanterns.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Aug 9 Public holiday National Day: National Day Parade at Marina Bay with flypasts and fireworks.
    • Aug 21–29 (approx.) Festival Singapore Night Festival: Night Festival light projections in Bugis and the museum district.
    • Aug 22–Sep 20 (approx.) Festival Chinatown Mid-Autumn lights: The whole street is hung with themed lanterns, with mooncake markets.

  11. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Aug 1–31 (approx.) Festival George Town Festival: A whole August of arts, music and street performances in George Town, Penang.
    • Aug 15–16 Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): National holiday with parades.
    • Aug 31 Public holiday National Day: Parade at Merdeka Square, Kuala Lumpur.

  12. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Apr 1–Oct 31 Season Bali dry season: Lots of sunshine and low humidity; July and August are high season.
    • Jul 1–Sep 30 (approx.) Season Toraja funeral season: The Toraja of Sulawesi hold their great funerals mostly in the dry season; visits can be arranged through local guides.
    • Jul 15–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival Bali Kite Festival: Giant traditional kite contests on Sanur beach.
    • Aug 8–15 (approx.) Festival Sekaten, Yogyakarta: A traditional festival at Yogyakarta's palace for the Prophet's Birthday, with gamelan and a night market.
    • Aug 11–21 Festival Galungan, Bali: The second Galungan of 2027, followed ten days later by Kuningan.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 17 Public holiday Independence Day: Flag-raising ceremonies and games around the country.

  13. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Aug 14–16 (approx.) Festival Kadayawan Festival, Davao: Tribal dances and floats celebrating the fruit and flower harvest.
    • Aug 21 Public holiday Ninoy Aquino Day: Special holiday.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday National Heroes Day: Last Monday of August.

  14. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Aug 15–16 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  15. South Asia

  16. 🇮🇳 India

    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Nehru Trophy Snake Boat Race, Kerala: Long snake-boat race on the Alappuzha backwaters, on the second Saturday of August.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Independence Day: The Prime Minister speaks at Delhi's Red Fort; roads nearby are closed.
    • Aug 15 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Islamic holiday.
    • Aug 25 Public holiday Janmashtami: Liveliest in Mathura and Vrindavan; Mumbai has human-pyramid pot-breaking (Dahi Handi).

  17. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Aug 17 Public holiday Janai Purnima: Hindu festival when devotees change their sacred thread.
    • Aug 25 Public holiday Janmashtami: Liveliest at the Krishna Mandir in Patan Durbar Square.

  18. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season East coast high season: While the southwest has its rains, Trincomalee and Arugam Bay on the east coast are dry; peak surf season.
    • Aug 1–25 (approx.) Festival Nallur Festival, Jaffna: A 25-day festival at Jaffna's Nallur temple, the north's biggest Hindu festival.
    • Aug 8–18 (approx.) Festival Kandy Esala Perahera: The Esala Perahera: dozens of decorated elephants parade at night.

  19. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Rainy low season: Southwest-monsoon showers; resorts are cheaper and some areas have bigger swells.
    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Whale shark & manta season (Baa Atoll): Manta rays and whale sharks gather at Hanifaru Bay, Baa Atoll, a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
    • Aug 3 (approx.) Public holiday National Day: Commemorates the expulsion of the Portuguese; set by the Islamic calendar.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  20. Central Asia

  21. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Aug 15–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Melon season (Kovun Sayli): Late-summer melons and grapes; markets are at their most abundant.
    • Aug 25–30 (approx.) Festival Sharq Taronalari music festival: International music festival on Samarkand's Registan, held every two years (odd years).

  22. Middle East

  23. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Apr 1–Nov 30 Festival Cappadocia balloon season: Stable weather in spring and autumn means balloons fly almost daily; winter snow and wind often ground them.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday Victory Day: Public holiday with a military parade.

  24. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Dubai Summer Surprises: Summer sales season with family activities in the malls.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  25. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Jul 22–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Jerash Festival of Culture and Arts: Music and folk performances in Jerash's Roman city; Jordan's biggest cultural festival.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  26. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Bahrain Summer Festival: Summer indoor family activities and shopping season.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.

  27. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Summer in Qatar: Summer is mostly indoors, with family activities and deals at malls and hotels.

  28. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Apr 15–Oct 15 Season Best time to visit: Spring and autumn are the most comfortable; Baku is hot in July and August, so escape to the Gabala mountains.
    • Jul 20–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Gabala International Music Festival: Classical music festival in the Gabala mountains in the north.

  29. Western Europe

  30. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Jul 23–Aug 2 (2026) Sports Commonwealth Games, Glasgow: Multi-sport games for 74 Commonwealth nations and territories, held in Glasgow, Scotland.
    • Aug 6–30 Festival Edinburgh Festival Fringe: The world's largest arts festival, running alongside the Military Tattoo.
    • Aug 6–28 (approx.) Festival Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo: Night-time military music on the esplanade of Edinburgh Castle, during the Fringe.
    • Aug 29–30 Festival Notting Hill Carnival: Europe's biggest street carnival, with Caribbean-style parades.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday Summer Bank Holiday: Last Monday in August, the same day as Notting Hill Carnival.

  31. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Jul 26–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Galway Races: Ireland's liveliest week of racing.
    • Aug 2 Public holiday August Bank Holiday: First Monday in August.

  32. 🇫🇷 France

    • Jul 8–Aug 20 (approx.) Festival Paris Plages: The banks of the Seine become artificial beaches and leisure areas.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: 15 August, peak French holiday time.

  33. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Apr 15–Sep 30 Season Windmill & Giethoorn high season: Good weather in spring and summer; the windmills and canal villages are at their busiest.
    • Aug 13–22 (approx.) Festival Grachtenfestival, Amsterdam: Classical concerts on and along the canals.
    • Aug 21–23 (2026) Sports F1 Dutch Grand Prix (Zandvoort): Formula 1's final race at Zandvoort in 2026, on a circuit among the dunes.

  34. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Aug 14–16 (2026) Festival Brussels Flower Carpet: Held every two years (even years): nearly a million begonias form a carpet.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  35. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Monte-Carlo Summer Festival: Concerts by international stars at the Monte-Carlo Sporting Club.
    • Jul 15–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival International Fireworks Festival, Monte-Carlo: International fireworks competition over Port Hercule on several summer nights.
    • Aug 15–16 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  36. Central Europe

  37. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Aug 26–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Stuttgart Wine Village: Wine festival on Stuttgart's market square.

  38. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Film Festival at Vienna City Hall: Free concert films on a big screen outside City Hall on summer nights.
    • Jul 18–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Salzburg Festival: Top-class classical music in Mozart's hometown.
    • Jul 21–Aug 22 (approx.) Festival Bregenz Festival (lake stage): Opera on a floating stage on Lake Constance.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  39. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Jun 15–Sep 30 Season Alpine hiking season: High-altitude trails and lifts are fully open.
    • Aug 1 Public holiday National Day: Bonfires and fireworks everywhere; the display at the Rhine Falls is the most spectacular.
    • Aug 4–14 (approx.) Festival Locarno Film Festival: Open-air screenings on the Piazza Grande, one of the largest outdoor cinemas.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Zurich Street Parade: A huge techno parade along Lake Zurich.

  40. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Jul 30–Aug 1 Sports F1 Hungarian Grand Prix: Formula 1 at the Hungaroring near Budapest; easy to combine with the city.
    • Aug 10–15 (approx.) Festival Sziget Festival: One of Europe's big music festivals, on an island in the Danube.
    • Aug 20 Public holiday St Stephen's Day (national day): A big fireworks show over the Danube.

  41. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • May 15–Sep 30 Festival Chopin concerts, Warsaw: Free open-air Chopin recitals by the Chopin Monument in Łazienki Park every summer Sunday.
    • Jul 24–Aug 15 (approx.) Festival St Dominic's Fair, Gdańsk: A summer fair dating back to the 13th century; the old town fills with stalls.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  42. Eastern Europe

  43. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Aug 5–8 (approx.) Festival Untold Festival, Cluj: Romania's biggest music festival.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Dormition of the Mother of God: Public holiday.

  44. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Jun 1–Sep 15 (approx.) Festival Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season: Summer evening performances in the Roman theatre.
    • Jul 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Varna Summer International Music Festival: Summer classical music festival in the Black Sea city of Varna.

  45. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Aug 20 Public holiday Restoration of Independence Day: Public holiday.

  46. Southern Europe

  47. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • May 8–Nov 21 (approx.) Festival Venice Biennale: Odd years bring the Art Biennale, at the Arsenale and the Giardini.
    • Jun 12–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Verona Opera Festival: Opera under the stars in Verona's Roman arena.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Ferragosto (Assumption Day): Peak Italian holiday time: city shops often close and the beaches are packed.
    • Aug 16 Festival Palio di Siena: Riders from 17 city districts race bareback in just 90 seconds.

  48. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday; when it falls on a Sunday some regions move it to Monday.
    • Aug 25 Festival La Tomatina, Buñol: Last Wednesday of August; tickets required.

  49. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday.

  50. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • May 15–Oct 15 Season Aegean island-hopping season: Frequent ferries; July and August are the hottest and priciest.
    • Jun 1–Aug 31 (approx.) Festival Athens Epidaurus Festival: Summer performances at the Odeon of Herodes Atticus and the ancient theatre of Epidaurus.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Dormition of the Mother of God: 15 August is the peak of Greek holidays; island ferries and rooms are hardest to book.
    • Aug 15 Festival Feast of the Virgin, Tinos: Big pilgrimages and festivities on Tinos and Paros.

  51. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Best time for Plitvice: Most water in spring, red leaves in autumn.
    • Jul 10–Aug 25 Festival Dubrovnik Summer Festival: Theatre and music in the old town.
    • Jul 14–Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Split Summer Festival: Opera and theatre in Diocletian's Palace.
    • Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Sinjska Alka: A 300-year-old knights' ring-tilting contest, on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
    • Aug 5 Public holiday Victory and Homeland Thanksgiving Day: Public holiday on 5 August.
    • Aug 15 Public holiday Assumption Day: Public holiday at the very peak of summer season.

  52. Northern Europe

  53. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Aug 10–14 (approx.) Festival Øya Festival, Oslo: A big music festival in an Oslo park.

  54. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Aug 1–31 Festival Crayfish parties: August crayfish parties with paper hats and drinking songs.
    • Aug 1–8 (approx.) Festival Medieval Week, Gotland: Medieval festival in the walled town of Visby, with jousting and markets.
    • Aug 17–21 (approx.) Festival Stockholm Culture Festival: Free culture festival in central Stockholm.

  55. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jul 2–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Savonlinna Opera Festival: Opera festival inside the medieval Olavinlinna Castle.
    • Aug 13–29 (approx.) Festival Helsinki Festival: Finland's biggest arts festival.
    • Aug 25–28 (approx.) Festival Air Guitar World Championships, Oulu: The Air Guitar World Championships in Oulu.
    • Aug 26 (approx.) Festival Night of the Arts, Helsinki: The Night of the Arts during the Helsinki Festival, with events all night.

  56. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Aug 20–29 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Cooking festival: A Nordic food festival.
    • Aug 27–Sep 5 (approx.) Festival Aarhus Festival: Denmark's biggest city culture festival.

  57. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • May 1–Aug 15 Season Puffin season: Puffins can be seen in the Westfjords and the Westman Islands.
    • Jul 30–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Þjóðhátíð, Westman Islands: A huge bonfire and music festival in the Westman Islands.
    • Aug 2 Public holiday Commerce Day: First Monday in August; music festivals around the country over the long weekend.
    • Aug 21 (approx.) Festival Reykjavík Culture Night: A culture festival on the day of the Reykjavík Marathon, with fireworks at night.

  58. North America

  59. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Aug 29–Sep 6 (approx.) Festival Burning Man: A large arts community gathering in Nevada's Black Rock Desert; tickets are limited.
    • Aug 30–Sep 12 (approx.) Sports US Open (tennis): The final Grand Slam of the year at Flushing Meadows, New York; the night sessions are electric.

  60. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Aug 5–8 (approx.) Festival Fêtes de la Nouvelle-France, Québec: Old Québec recreates colonial life of the 17th and 18th centuries.

  61. Latin America

  62. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Pantanal dry season: In the dry season animals gather at the water; the best chance to see jaguars.
    • Jul 28–Aug 1 (approx.) Festival Paraty International Literary Festival (FLIP): International literary festival in the colonial town of Paraty.

  63. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • May 1–Sep 30 Season Machu Picchu dry season: The Inca Trail closes for maintenance every February.
    • May 1–Oct 31 Season Amazon dry season: Dry season in Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado makes the trails easier.
    • Jul 23–Aug 8 Sports Pan American Games, Lima: The Americas' biggest multi-sport games, Lima's second time hosting.
    • Aug 6 Public holiday Battle of Junín Day: Public holiday.
    • Aug 30 Public holiday Santa Rosa de Lima Day: Public holiday.

  64. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Aug 13–26 (approx.) Festival Buenos Aires Tango Festival: The Tango World Championship, with lots of free shows.
    • Aug 16 Public holiday General San Martín Day: A public holiday in August.

  65. Africa

  66. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Aug 7–22 Festival Coptic Feast of the Virgin: The Coptic fast of the Virgin, with pilgrimages to monasteries.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 14 (approx.) Festival Mawlid at Al-Hussein Mosque, Cairo: Festivities around Cairo's Al-Hussein Mosque for the Prophet's Birthday, with traditional sweets.

  67. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Aug 14 Public holiday Oued Ed-Dahab Day: Public holiday.
    • Aug 14–15 (approx.) Public holiday Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid): Public holiday.
    • Aug 20–21 Public holiday Revolution of the King and the People & Youth Day: Two holidays in a row on 20 and 21 August.

  68. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jun 1–Nov 30 Season Hermanus whale season: Southern right whales come close to shore, most in September and October.
    • Aug 1–Sep 30 Season Namaqualand wildflower season: The desert bursts into wildflowers almost overnight.
    • Aug 9 Public holiday Women's Day: Public holiday on 9 August.

  69. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jul 1–Oct 31 Season Great Migration: Wildebeest cross the Mara River, most spectacularly in August.

  70. Oceania

  71. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Aug 29 (approx.) Sports Sydney Marathon: One of the World Marathon Majors, crossing the Harbour Bridge to finish at the Opera House.

  72. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jun 15–Oct 10 Season Ski season: Ski fields around Queenstown and Wānaka.

August travel FAQ

What are the big festivals and events in August?

Highlights in August 2027 include Petra by Night (Jordan, Jan 1–Dec 31); Cappadocia balloon season (Türkiye, Apr 1–Nov 30); Venice Biennale (Italy, May 8–Nov 21); Chopin concerts, Warsaw (Poland, May 15–Sep 30); Plovdiv Roman theatre summer season (Bulgaria, Jun 1–Sep 15); Athens Epidaurus Festival (Greece, Jun 1–Aug 31); Verona Opera Festival (Italy, Jun 12–Sep 5); Taiwan International Balloon Festival, Taitung (Taiwan, Jul 1–Aug 31); Dubai Summer Surprises (UAE, Jul 1–Aug 31); Film Festival at Vienna City Hall (Austria, Jul 1–Aug 31); Varna Summer International Music Festival (Bulgaria, Jul 1–Aug 31); Bahrain Summer Festival (Bahrain, Jul 1–Aug 31), among 80 events listed on this page.

Which countries have public holidays in August 2027?

There are 49 public holidays or long weekends, including National Day in Switzerland (Aug 1); August Bank Holiday in Ireland (Aug 2); Commerce Day in Iceland (Aug 2); National Day in Maldives (Aug 3); Victory and Homeland Thanksgiving Day in Croatia (Aug 5); Battle of Junín Day in Peru (Aug 6); National Day in Singapore (Aug 9); Women's Day in South Africa (Aug 9); Mountain Day in Japan (Aug 11); Mother's Day (Queen Mother Sirikit's Birthday) in Thailand (Aug 12); Obon in Japan (Aug 13–16); Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid) in Maldives (Aug 14) and more. Sights and transport get busier and some shops close, so check before you book.
