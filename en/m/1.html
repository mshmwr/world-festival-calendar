  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇯🇵 Japan

    • Jan 1–3 Public holiday New Year & Hatsumode: Japan's most important holiday: shrine visits for the new year, and lucky bags in department stores.
    • Jan 11 Public holiday Coming of Age Day: Second Monday of January; new adults attend ceremonies in furisode kimono.

  3. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: A one-day New Year holiday; most sights stay open.
    • Jan 9–Feb 1 (approx.) Festival Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival: Ice fishing and catching trout by hand, Gangwon's signature winter event.

  4. 🇨🇳 China

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year's Day: New Year's holiday; popular sights and train tickets get tight.
    • Jan 5–Feb 28 Festival Harbin Ice and Snow Festival: Giant ice sculptures lit up at night at Ice and Snow World; bring full gear for -20°C.
    • Dec 18–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Harbin Ice and Snow World: A giant ice-sculpture park by the Songhua River, opening around late December; most spectacular lit up at night.

  5. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Jan 1 Public holiday Founding Day of the Republic of China (New Year's Day): New Year long weekend; big crowds after the Taipei 101 fireworks.

  6. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day public holiday.
    • Dec 12–Jan 4 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Brands and Products Expo: Lunar New Year goods and local brands at Victoria Park.

  7. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  8. Southeast Asia

  9. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year holidays: New Year holidays; crowds at Bangkok's countdowns and on the islands.
    • Jan 9 Public holiday Children's Day: Second Saturday of January; government offices open to visitors, but it isn't a day off.
    • Jan 15–17 (approx.) Festival Bo Sang Umbrella Festival: Annual festival in Bo Sang's handmade paper-umbrella village near Chiang Mai, with umbrella parades and painting.
    • Nov 1–Feb 28 Season Peak season (dry season): Cool and dry, the best time to travel, and the most expensive.

  10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: A one-day New Year holiday.
    • Jan 30–Feb 8 (approx.) Festival Nguyen Hue Tết flower street, Ho Chi Minh City: Before Tết, Nguyen Hue walking street becomes a huge flower street.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.

  11. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day public holiday.
    • Jan 22 Festival Thaipusam: Hindu devotees carry kavadi in procession from Little India to Tank Road.

  12. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Jan 22 Festival Thaipusam at Batu Caves: The world's largest Thaipusam, with a million devotees climbing 272 steps.

  13. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 5 Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Islamic holiday; public holiday.
    • Jan 13–23 Festival Galungan, Bali: A major Balinese festival every 210 days; every house raises a bamboo penjor, followed ten days later by Kuningan.

  14. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Eve and New Year's Day holidays, with fireworks across Manila.
    • Jan 17 Festival Sinulog Festival, Cebu: A huge street-dance parade on the third Sunday of January.
    • Jan 17 Festival Ati-Atihan Festival, Kalibo: The "mother of Philippine festivals", with street dancing in blackface paint.
    • Jan 24 Festival Dinagyang Festival, Iloilo: Tribal-costume dance competition on the fourth Sunday of January.
    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.

  15. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 7 Public holiday Victory over Genocide Day: Marks the fall of the Khmer Rouge regime in 1979.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.

  16. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.

  17. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 4 Public holiday Independence Day: Marks independence from Britain in 1948.
    • Jan 8 Public holiday Karen New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jan 14–16 (approx.) Festival Naga New Year: Naga New Year in northern Sagaing, with warrior-costume dances; travel permits required.
    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.

  18. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Jan 1–2 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  19. South Asia

  20. 🇮🇳 India

    • Jan 8–14 (approx.) Festival International Kite Festival, Gujarat: International kite festival on the riverfront in Ahmedabad; the whole city flies kites on 14 January.
    • Jan 14–15 (approx.) Festival Harvest festivals (Pongal, Makar Sankranti): South India's harvest festival; Gujarat holds its International Kite Festival at the same time.
    • Jan 21–25 (approx.) Festival Jaipur Literature Festival: The world's largest free literary festival, held in Jaipur, often during Golden Triangle tours.
    • Jan 26 Public holiday Republic Day: Military parade along Kartavya Path, New Delhi.

  21. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Jan 11 Public holiday Prithvi Jayanti: Honours the king who unified Nepal.
    • Jan 15 Public holiday Maghe Sankranti: A post-solstice festival with ritual bathing at rivers.
    • Jan 29 Public holiday Martyrs' Day: Public holiday.
    • Dec 28–Jan 1 (approx.) Festival Pokhara Street Festival: Pokhara's Lakeside becomes a car-free food and performance street, liveliest at New Year.

  22. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • Jan 15 Public holiday Thai Pongal: Tamil harvest festival; liveliest around Jaffna in the north.
    • Jan 17–22 (approx.) Festival Duruthu Perahera, Kelaniya: Full-moon procession at Kelaniya Temple near Colombo, with elephants and dancers.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Southwest coast high season: Dry season in Galle and Mirissa, and whale-watching season.

  23. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.

  24. Central Asia

  25. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year holidays.

  26. Middle East

  27. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  28. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday after the Burj Khalifa fireworks.
    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Dec 5–Jan 11 (approx.) Festival Dubai Shopping Festival: City-wide sales, fireworks and drone shows.

  29. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Jan 5 (approx.) Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Public holiday.

  30. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C; from June to September temperatures often top 40°C.

  31. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Nov 1–Mar 15 Season Best time to visit: Winter days in Riyadh and AlUla are in the 20s°C and desert nights near 10°C; inland summers often exceed 45°C.
    • Dec 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival AlUla winter (Winter at Tantora): Concerts, balloon flights and night tours of the ruins during AlUla's winter, with big shows at the Maraya.

  32. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.

  33. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Jan 1–4 Public holiday New Year: New Year holidays.
    • Jan 20 Public holiday Black January (Martyrs' Day): Commemorates the 1990 crackdown by Soviet troops; people lay flowers at the Alley of Martyrs.
    • Dec 15–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Baku New Year lights: New Year lights and markets along Baku Boulevard and Fountain Square.

  34. Western Europe

  35. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year bank holiday; most shops keep shorter hours.
    • Jan 25 Festival Burns Night: Celebrates the Scottish poet Robert Burns, with haggis and poetry.
    • Nov 20–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Hyde Park Winter Wonderland: A Christmas fair in Hyde Park with a Ferris wheel, ice rink and markets.

  36. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 27–31 (approx.) Festival TradFest Dublin: Irish traditional music in Temple Bar and at Dublin Castle.

  37. 🇫🇷 France

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; most shops and some museums close.
    • Jan 13–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Winter sales (Soldes): France's official winter sales, with big discounts in department stores and boutiques.

  38. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 26–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Amsterdam Light Festival: Light art along the canals, best seen from a night boat tour.

  39. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 27–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Brussels Christmas Market: Winter Wonders Christmas market and ice rink.

  40. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 15–24 (approx.) Festival International Circus Festival of Monte-Carlo: The world's most important circus competition, held in the big top at Fontvieille.
    • Jan 21–24 (approx.) Sports Rallye Monte-Carlo: The opening round of the World Rally Championship, with stages through the Alps and a finish at the port of Monaco.
    • Jan 27 Public holiday Saint Devota's Day: Feast of Monaco's patron saint, with a boat burned in the harbour on the evening of 26 January.
    • Dec 1–Jan 2 (approx.) Festival Monaco Christmas Village: A Christmas village at Port Hercule with an ice rink and Ferris wheel.

  41. Central Europe

  42. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; shops close.

  43. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Jan 1 Festival Vienna New Year's Concert: Broadcast worldwide from the Golden Hall; tickets are allocated by ballot.
    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday, the day of the Vienna New Year's Concert.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday; shops close.
    • Jan 7–Feb 28 Festival Vienna ball season: Hundreds of balls, the Opera Ball being the most famous.

  44. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 15–17 (approx.) Sports Lauberhorn ski races, Wengen: The longest downhill on the Ski World Cup, a winter highlight in the Jungfrau region.

  45. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year & Restoration Day of the Czech State: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 28–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Prague Christmas markets: A giant Christmas tree and market on Old Town Square.

  46. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  47. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday with Three Kings parades.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Warsaw Christmas market & lights: Lights along the Royal Route and the Old Town Square in Warsaw.

  48. Eastern Europe

  49. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Jan 1–8 Public holiday New Year holidays: Russia's longest holiday, usually running to 8 January.
    • Jan 7 Public holiday Orthodox Christmas: Midnight services in the churches.
    • Dec 1–Jan 15 (approx.) Festival Moscow New Year lights: Big light displays and New Year fairs around Red Square and Tverskaya Street.

  50. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Jan 1–2 Public holiday New Year: Holidays on 1 and 2 January.
    • Jan 6–7 Public holiday Epiphany & St John's Day: Two public holidays in a row.
    • Jan 24 Public holiday Union of the Romanian Principalities Day: Public holiday.

  51. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 29–31 (approx.) Festival Kukeri festival: Mask festival in Pernik, where huge fur-covered masks drive away evil spirits.

  52. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 21–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Christmas Market: Often rated one of Europe's most beautiful Christmas markets.

  53. Southern Europe

  54. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday with parades of the witch Befana.
    • Jan 23–Feb 9 Festival Venice Carnival: Elaborate masks and costumes; St Mark's Square is the heart of it.
    • Dec 8–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in Rome (Vatican midnight Mass): Christmas market on Piazza Navona, with the tree and nativity in St Peter's Square.

  55. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Three Kings' Day: Three Kings parades on the evening of 5 January; 6 January is a holiday.
    • Jan 20 Festival Tamborrada, San Sebastián: The city drums for 24 hours to honour its patron saint.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Madrid Christmas markets & lights: Christmas market on Plaza Mayor and a parade on the eve of Three Kings.

  56. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 25–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Lisbon Christmas lights: A giant Christmas tree on Praça do Comércio and lights across the city.

  57. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Crosses are thrown into the sea at harbours across the country.
    • Jan 17–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Patras Carnival: Greece's biggest carnival, climaxing on the weekend before Lent.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.
    • Dec 1–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas & New Year (Syntagma Square, Athens): Christmas tree on Syntagma Square and city lights; on 6 January (Epiphany) crosses are thrown into the harbours.

  58. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday.
    • Jan 17–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Rijeka Carnival: Croatia's biggest carnival.
    • Nov 28–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Advent in Zagreb: Repeatedly voted Europe's best Christmas market.

  59. Northern Europe

  60. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 21 Festival Sun Day (Tromsø): The day the sun returns after the polar night, celebrated across northern Norway.
    • Jan 22–31 (approx.) Festival Northern Lights Festival, Tromsø: A music festival in the Arctic city, during aurora season.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.

  61. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday.
    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.
    • Dec 10–Apr 10 (approx.) Season Icehotel open season: The Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is carved anew every year.

  62. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 6 Public holiday Epiphany: Public holiday.
    • Jan 20–Apr 5 (approx.) Festival SnowCastle of Kemi: The SnowCastle of Kemi and ice sculptures, rebuilt every year.
    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.
    • Nov 20–Jan 6 Festival Santa Claus Village high season: Rovaniemi, with husky and reindeer sleigh rides.

  63. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 26–29 (approx.) Festival Copenhagen Fashion Week: Nordic fashion week.

  64. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday after Reykjavík's city-wide New Year's Eve fireworks.
    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.

  65. North America

  66. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Jan 1 Festival Rose Parade: A century-old tradition in Pasadena, California.
    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: Federal holiday; many sights close or shorten hours on New Year's Day.
    • Jan 18 Public holiday Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Third Monday of January; federal holiday.
    • Nov 30–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in New York (Rockefeller Center tree): The Rockefeller Center tree lighting, Fifth Avenue windows and skating rinks.

  67. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: National holiday.
    • Jan 29–Feb 14 (approx.) Festival Québec Winter Carnival: Ice palace, snow sculptures and an ice canoe race.
    • Jan 29–Feb 15 (approx.) Festival Winterlude, Ottawa: The Rideau Canal becomes the world's largest naturally frozen skating rink, with ice sculptures.
    • Nov 14–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Winter Festival of Lights, Niagara Falls: The falls are lit up at night with light displays.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.

  68. Latin America

  69. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: Public holiday.
    • Jan 6 Festival Three Kings' Day: People share rosca de reyes; whoever finds the little figure buys tamales in February.
    • Nov 15–Mar 15 Festival Monarch butterfly season (Michoacán): Hundreds of millions of monarch butterflies winter in the forest, most in February; a World Heritage reserve.

  70. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; big crowds after New Year's Eve on Copacabana.
    • Oct 24–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Natal Luz, Gramado: A three-month Christmas festival in a German-heritage town in the south.

  71. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.

  72. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.

  73. Africa

  74. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Jan 7 Public holiday Coptic Christmas: 7 January, Christmas for Egypt's Coptic Christians.
    • Jan 27–Feb 9 (approx.) Festival Cairo International Book Fair: One of the largest book fairs in the Middle East.
    • Jan 28 Public holiday January 25 Revolution & Police Day: Public holiday.
    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.

  75. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 11 Public holiday Proclamation of Independence Day: Public holiday.
    • Jan 14 Public holiday Amazigh New Year: A public holiday since 2024.
    • Jan 25–Feb 28 Season Almond blossom season (Tafraoute): Almond trees blossom in the Anti-Atlas.
    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.

  76. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; Cape Town's beaches are packed.
    • Jan 2 Festival Kaapse Klopse (Cape Town Minstrel Carnival): The Cape Town minstrel parade, a New Year tradition.
    • Dec 1–Jan 5 (approx.) Festival Cape Town festive lights (Adderley Street): Christmas lights in central Cape Town and events at the V&A Waterfront.

  77. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday.
    • Jan 1–Mar 15 Season Dry-season safari: The dry season in January–February concentrates animals near water; it's also calving season.

  78. Oceania

  79. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Jan 1 Public holiday New Year: New Year's Day holiday; big crowds after Sydney's fireworks.
    • Jan 8–26 (approx.) Festival Sydney Festival: Sydney's summer arts festival with outdoor music and theatre.
    • Jan 17–31 (approx.) Sports Australian Open: Melbourne's Grand Slam, the first of the year.
    • Jan 26 Public holiday Australia Day: A regatta and fireworks on Sydney Harbour.
    • Dec 15–Jan 31 (approx.) Festival Tasmanian lavender season: Peak bloom at Bridestowe Lavender Estate.

  80. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Jan 1–4 Public holiday New Year: Holidays on 1 and 2 January.
    • Dec 15–Feb 28 Season Peak summer season: Peak season for South Island hikes and lakes; book accommodation early.
