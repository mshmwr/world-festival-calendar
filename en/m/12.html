  1. Northeast Asia

  2. 🇰🇷 South Korea

    • Dec 12–31 (approx.) Festival Seoul Christmas Market, Gwanghwamun: A winter market and lights on Gwanghwamun Square, best after dark.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: Christmas lights are best in Myeongdong and the department stores; book restaurants ahead.

  3. 🇨🇳 China

    • Dec 18–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Harbin Ice and Snow World: A giant ice-sculpture park by the Songhua River, opening around late December; most spectacular lit up at night.

  4. 🇹🇼 Taiwan

    • Nov 7–Dec 6 (approx.) Festival Xinshe Sea of Flowers: Vast flower fields in Xinshe, Taichung: Taiwan's biggest autumn-winter flower show.
    • Dec 24–25 Public holiday Constitution Day: Restored as a public holiday from 2025; falls on Christmas Day.
    • Dec 31 Festival Taipei 101 New Year's Eve fireworks: New Year's Eve countdown fireworks on Taipei 101; roads around Xinyi are closed.

  5. 🇭🇰 Hong Kong

    • Dec 12–Jan 4 (approx.) Festival Hong Kong Brands and Products Expo: Lunar New Year goods and local brands at Victoria Park.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas holidays: Christmas lights are best in Tsim Sha Tsui and Central; book restaurants ahead.
    • Dec 31 Festival Victoria Harbour New Year's Eve fireworks: A huge countdown fireworks show on both sides of Victoria Harbour.

  6. 🇲🇳 Mongolia

    • Dec 29 Public holiday Independence Day: Marks the 1911 declaration of independence.

  7. Southeast Asia

  8. 🇹🇭 Thailand

    • Nov 1–Feb 28 Season Peak season (dry season): Cool and dry, the best time to travel, and the most expensive.
    • Dec 1–31 (approx.) Festival Bangkok Christmas & New Year lights: Big light displays around Siam and ICONSIAM, with fireworks on the Chao Phraya at New Year.
    • Dec 5–6 Public holiday Father's Day (National Day): The late King Bhumibol's birthday; public holiday.
    • Dec 10 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.

  9. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best season for northern Vietnam: Cool and dry, with good visibility on Ha Long Bay.
    • Nov 1–Apr 30 Season Phu Quoc high season: Calm dry-season seas, ideal for island-hopping and diving.
    • Dec 20–25 (approx.) Festival Da Lat Flower Festival: Da Lat's flower festival is held about every two years in December, with flower floats and displays.

  10. 🇸🇬 Singapore

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Christmas on Orchard Road: Christmas on A Great Street: 3 km of lights.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: The final peak of the Orchard Road lights; public holiday.

  11. 🇲🇾 Malaysia

    • Nov 1–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Malaysia Year-End Sale: Year-end sales season, with lights around Bukit Bintang in Kuala Lumpur.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Kuala Lumpur's malls go big on Christmas decorations.

  12. 🇮🇩 Indonesia

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday, often with extra collective leave days around it.

  13. 🇵🇭 Philippines

    • Nov 1–May 31 Season Boracay peak season: Calmer seas in the northeast-monsoon months; the beaches are at their best.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Special holiday.
    • Dec 16–24 Festival Simbang Gabi (dawn Masses): Dawn Masses on the nine days before Christmas, with rice-cake stalls outside the churches.
    • Dec 18 (approx.) Festival Giant Lantern Festival, San Fernando: A giant-lantern contest in San Fernando, Pampanga, with patterns that change to music.
    • Dec 24–25 Public holiday Christmas: The Philippine Christmas season starts in September; Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are national holidays.
    • Dec 30 Public holiday Rizal Day: Honours the national hero José Rizal.

  14. 🇰🇭 Cambodia

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time for Angkor: Cool dry season, ideal for sunrise at the temples.
    • Dec 5 (approx.) Sports Angkor Wat International Half Marathon: A run among the temples of Angkor, held on the first Sunday of December.
    • Dec 29 Public holiday Peace Day: A public holiday added in 2020.

  15. 🇱🇦 Laos

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: The cool season, when Kuang Si Falls is at its bluest.
    • Dec 2 Public holiday National Day: Public holiday on 2 December.

  16. 🇲🇲 Myanmar

    • Oct 1–Mar 31 Season Bagan balloon season: Balloons drift over the temple plain at sunrise.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  17. 🇧🇳 Brunei

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
    • Dec 26–27 (approx.) Public holiday Isra and Mi'raj: Public holiday.

  18. South Asia

  19. 🇮🇳 India

    • Dec 20–31 (approx.) Festival Christmas & New Year in Goa: Christmas Mass in Goa's churches and New Year beach parties; the priciest time to stay.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; liveliest in Goa.

  20. 🇳🇵 Nepal

    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
    • Dec 28–Jan 1 (approx.) Festival Pokhara Street Festival: Pokhara's Lakeside becomes a car-free food and performance street, liveliest at New Year.
    • Dec 30 Public holiday Tamu Lhosar: Gurung New Year, with parades in Pokhara.

  21. 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka

    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Southwest coast high season: Dry season in Galle and Mirissa, and whale-watching season.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday; every full-moon (Poya) day is also a holiday in Sri Lanka, when alcohol sales are banned.

  22. 🇲🇻 Maldives

    • Dec 1–Apr 30 Season Dry-season peak: Calm seas and good visibility: the best diving.

  23. Central Asia

  24. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Constitution Day: Public holiday.

  25. Middle East

  26. 🇹🇷 Türkiye

    • Dec 1–31 (approx.) Festival Istanbul New Year lights: Christmas and New Year lights on İstiklal Avenue and in Nişantaşı.
    • Dec 7–17 Festival Mevlana Whirling Dervish Festival, Konya: Sufi whirling ceremonies commemorating Rumi.

  27. 🇦🇪 UAE

    • Oct 20–May 5 (approx.) Festival Global Village, Dubai: A winter-only themed market with country pavilions and food, open in the evenings.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Peak season: Around 20–30°C, ideal for desert trips.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival Sheikh Zayed Festival, Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi's big heritage festival, with country pavilions, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Dec 2 Public holiday National Day: Fireworks and parades in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
    • Dec 5–Jan 11 (approx.) Festival Dubai Shopping Festival: City-wide sales, fireworks and drone shows.
    • Dec 10–12 Sports F1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix: The Formula 1 season finale at Yas Marina, starting at dusk; combine it with the Yas Island theme parks.

  28. 🇯🇴 Jordan

    • Jan 1–Dec 31 Festival Petra by Night: Candles line the Siq to the Treasury on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday nights; separate ticket required.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  29. 🇧🇭 Bahrain

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C; from June to September temperatures often top 40°C.
    • Dec 10–20 (approx.) Festival Bahrain National Day lights: Lights and fireworks in Manama around National Day.
    • Dec 16–17 Public holiday National Day: Holidays on 16 and 17 December, with lights and fireworks in the city.

  30. 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

    • Oct 20–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Riyadh Season: Riyadh's annual entertainment season, packed with concerts, boxing and themed zones, running into the new year.
    • Nov 1–Mar 15 Season Best time to visit: Winter days in Riyadh and AlUla are in the 20s°C and desert nights near 10°C; inland summers often exceed 45°C.
    • Dec 1–Mar 31 (approx.) Festival AlUla winter (Winter at Tantora): Concerts, balloon flights and night tours of the ruins during AlUla's winter, with big shows at the Maraya.

  31. 🇶🇦 Qatar

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Best time to visit: Winter days are around 20–25°C, perfect for souqs and desert safaris; summers are hot and humid.
    • Dec 3–5 Sports F1 Qatar Grand Prix (Lusail): Formula 1's night race at Lusail International Circuit.
    • Dec 10–18 (approx.) Festival Qatar National Day week & Darb Al Saai: In the week before National Day, the heritage village at Umm Salal opens with camels and falcons.
    • Dec 18 Public holiday National Day: Parade and fireworks on the Corniche on 18 December, with flags all over the city.

  32. 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan

    • Dec 15–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Baku New Year lights: New Year lights and markets along Baku Boulevard and Fountain Square.
    • Dec 31 Public holiday Solidarity Day of World Azerbaijanis: 31 December runs into the New Year holidays for a long break.

  33. Western Europe

  34. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

    • Nov 20–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Hyde Park Winter Wonderland: A Christmas fair in Hyde Park with a Ferris wheel, ice rink and markets.
    • Dec 25–28 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: On 25 December public transport almost stops and shops close; the winter sales start on the 26th.
    • Dec 31 Festival London New Year's Eve fireworks: Fireworks around the London Eye on the Thames; tickets required.

  35. 🇮🇪 Ireland

    • Nov 25–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Dublin Christmas markets & lights: Lights on Grafton Street and Christmas markets.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas & St Stephen's Day: On 25 December public transport almost stops and shops close.
    • Dec 29–31 (approx.) Festival New Year's Festival Dublin: New Year's Eve concerts and light projections.

  36. 🇫🇷 France

    • Nov 27–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Strasbourg Christmas Market: France's oldest Christmas market and a must on Alsace Christmas tours.
    • Dec 5–8 Festival Fête des Lumières, Lyon: Light projections across the city's buildings, free over four nights.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Most shops and restaurants close.

  37. 🇳🇱 Netherlands

    • Nov 26–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Amsterdam Light Festival: Light art along the canals, best seen from a night boat tour.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Public holidays on 25 and 26 December.

  38. 🇧🇪 Belgium

    • Nov 27–Jan 3 (approx.) Festival Brussels Christmas Market: Winter Wonders Christmas market and ice rink.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  39. 🇲🇨 Monaco

    • Dec 1–Jan 2 (approx.) Festival Monaco Christmas Village: A Christmas village at Port Hercule with an ice rink and Ferris wheel.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  40. Central Europe

  41. 🇩🇪 Germany

    • Nov 23–Dec 24 Festival Christmas markets: Nuremberg, Dresden and Cologne have the most famous markets.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December; most Christmas markets end by the 23rd.

  42. 🇦🇹 Austria

    • Nov 14–Dec 26 (approx.) Festival Vienna Christmas markets: Christmas markets at City Hall Square and Schönbrunn.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday, but shops may open: a big Christmas shopping day.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December; shops close.

  43. 🇨🇭 Switzerland

    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Zurich Christmas markets: The Swarovski Christmas tree and market inside Zurich main station.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; Easter, Boxing Day and other holidays are set by each canton, and most observe them.

  44. 🇨🇿 Czechia

    • Nov 28–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Prague Christmas markets: A giant Christmas tree and market on Old Town Square.
    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of the 24th; most Christmas markets stay open into early January.

  45. 🇭🇺 Hungary

    • Nov 13–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Budapest Christmas markets: Light show in front of St Stephen's Basilica.
    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December.

  46. 🇵🇱 Poland

    • Nov 27–Dec 26 (approx.) Festival Kraków Christmas market: Market on the Main Square and a nativity-crib (szopka) contest.
    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Warsaw Christmas market & lights: Lights along the Royal Route and the Old Town Square in Warsaw.
    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Since 2025, 24 December is also a holiday; shops close.

  47. Eastern Europe

  48. 🇷🇺 Russia

    • Dec 1–Jan 15 (approx.) Festival Moscow New Year lights: Big light displays and New Year fairs around Red Square and Tverskaya Street.

  49. 🇷🇴 Romania

    • Nov 27–Dec 27 (approx.) Festival Bucharest Christmas Market: Christmas market on Constitution Square.
    • Nov 30–Dec 1 Public holiday St Andrew's Day & National Day: Holidays on 30 November and 1 December, with a military parade in Bucharest.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.

  50. 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

    • Dec 1–31 (approx.) Festival Sofia Christmas Market: Christmas market in central Sofia.
    • Dec 24–28 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays from 24 to 26 December.

  51. 🇪🇪 Estonia

    • Nov 21–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Tallinn Christmas Market: Often rated one of Europe's most beautiful Christmas markets.
    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays from 24 to 26 December.

  52. Southern Europe

  53. 🇮🇹 Italy

    • Oct 9–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Alba White Truffle Fair: White truffle fair in Alba, Piedmont, a highlight of autumn food travel.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday that officially opens the Christmas season.
    • Dec 8–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in Rome (Vatican midnight Mass): Christmas market on Piazza Navona, with the tree and nativity in St Peter's Square.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.

  54. 🇪🇸 Spain

    • Nov 27–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Madrid Christmas markets & lights: Christmas market on Plaza Mayor and a parade on the eve of Three Kings.
    • Dec 6–8 Public holiday December long weekend (Constitution Day & Immaculate Conception): Holidays on 6 and 8 December, often bridged into a long weekend (puente).
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  55. 🇵🇹 Portugal

    • Nov 25–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Lisbon Christmas lights: A giant Christmas tree on Praça do Comércio and lights across the city.
    • Dec 1 Public holiday Restoration of Independence Day: Public holiday.
    • Dec 1–31 (approx.) Festival Óbidos Christmas Village: The walled town turns into a Christmas village.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  56. 🇬🇷 Greece

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Santorini low season: Many hotels and restaurants close; few visitors and low prices.
    • Dec 1–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas & New Year (Syntagma Square, Athens): Christmas tree on Syntagma Square and city lights; on 6 January (Epiphany) crosses are thrown into the harbours.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.

  57. 🇭🇷 Croatia

    • Nov 28–Jan 7 (approx.) Festival Advent in Zagreb: Repeatedly voted Europe's best Christmas market.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December.

  58. Northern Europe

  59. 🇳🇴 Norway

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Northern lights season: Tromsø and Lofoten; the nights are longest around the winter solstice.
    • Nov 15–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Pepperkakebyen (gingerbread town), Bergen: The world's largest gingerbread town.
    • Dec 10 Festival Nobel Peace Prize ceremony, Oslo: Prize ceremony at Oslo City Hall, with a torchlight procession and concerts.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December; 25 and 26 December are holidays.

  60. 🇸🇪 Sweden

    • Sep 15–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Abisko has one of the highest chances of seeing the aurora anywhere.
    • Nov 27–Dec 20 (approx.) Festival Skansen Christmas market: Traditional Christmas market at Skansen open-air museum.
    • Dec 5–10 Festival Nobel Week, Stockholm: Nobel Prize ceremony and banquet, with light art around the city.
    • Dec 10–Apr 10 (approx.) Season Icehotel open season: The Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is carved anew every year.
    • Dec 13 Festival St Lucia's Day: Girls in white with crowns of candles walk in procession.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Christmas Eve (24 December) is the main celebration; shops close early.

  61. 🇫🇮 Finland

    • Sep 1–Mar 31 Season Lapland northern lights season: Clear nights in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä often bring the aurora; glass igloos are the favourite.
    • Nov 20–Jan 6 Festival Santa Claus Village high season: Rovaniemi, with husky and reindeer sleigh rides.
    • Dec 1–22 (approx.) Festival Helsinki Christmas Market: Christmas market on Senate Square.
    • Dec 6 Public holiday Independence Day: National Day on 6 December, when two candles are lit in every window.
    • Dec 24–26 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays from 24 to 26 December; most shops and restaurants close.

  62. 🇩🇰 Denmark

    • Nov 14–Dec 31 (approx.) Festival Tivoli Christmas season: Christmas lights at the century-old amusement park.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December; 25 and 26 December are holidays.

  63. 🇮🇸 Iceland

    • Sep 1–Apr 15 Season Northern lights season: Best outside town, away from light pollution.
    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Ice cave season: The blue ice caves of Vatnajökull open only in winter.
    • Dec 12–24 Festival Icelandic Christmas: the 13 Yule Lads: A Yule Lad arrives each of the 13 nights before Christmas, with events around the country.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas: Shops close from the afternoon of 24 December.
    • Dec 31 Festival Reykjavík New Year's Eve fireworks: Residents set off their own fireworks: the whole city lights up on New Year's Eve.

  64. North America

  65. 🇺🇸 United States

    • Nov 30–Jan 6 (approx.) Festival Christmas in New York (Rockefeller Center tree): The Rockefeller Center tree lighting, Fifth Avenue windows and skating rinks.
    • Dec 24–25 Public holiday Christmas: Most shops and sights close on 25 December; the Rockefeller Center tree in New York draws the biggest crowds.
    • Dec 31 Festival Times Square New Year's Eve: The ball-drop countdown; stake out a spot hours in advance.

  66. 🇨🇦 Canada

    • Nov 14–Feb 28 (approx.) Festival Winter Festival of Lights, Niagara Falls: The falls are lit up at night with light displays.
    • Nov 15–Apr 10 Season Yellowknife aurora season: The best aurora viewing in North America.
    • Nov 20–Dec 24 (approx.) Festival Vancouver Christmas Market: A German-style Christmas market next to the Vancouver Convention Centre.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: National holiday; Boxing Day on the 26th is a big sales day.

  67. Latin America

  68. 🇲🇽 Mexico

    • Nov 15–Mar 15 Festival Monarch butterfly season (Michoacán): Hundreds of millions of monarch butterflies winter in the forest, most in February; a World Heritage reserve.
    • Nov 27–Dec 5 (approx.) Festival Guadalajara International Book Fair: The largest book fair in the Spanish-speaking world.
    • Dec 12 Festival Day of the Virgin of Guadalupe: Mexico's most important religious holiday, when millions make the pilgrimage to the Basilica of Guadalupe in Mexico City.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday; posadas are held everywhere from 16 to 24 December.

  69. 🇧🇷 Brazil

    • Sep 6–Dec 6 (2026) (approx.) Festival São Paulo Biennial: International contemporary art biennial in even years (next in 2028).
    • Oct 24–Jan 17 (approx.) Festival Natal Luz, Gramado: A three-month Christmas festival in a German-heritage town in the south.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.
    • Dec 31 Festival New Year's Eve on Copacabana: Two million people dressed in white watch fireworks on the beach.

  70. 🇵🇪 Peru

    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 9 Public holiday Battle of Ayacucho Day: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  71. 🇦🇷 Argentina

    • Nov 1–Mar 31 Season Patagonia high season: Southern-hemisphere summer, the best time for glacier trekking.
    • Dec 8 Public holiday Immaculate Conception: Public holiday.
    • Dec 25 Public holiday Christmas: Public holiday.

  72. Africa

  73. 🇪🇬 Egypt

    • Oct 15–Apr 15 Season Best time to visit: Avoids the summer heat; ideal for a Nile cruise.

  74. 🇲🇦 Morocco

    • Oct 1–Apr 30 Season Best time for the Sahara: Desert camps at Merzouga and Zagora: pleasant days, cold nights.
    • Nov 26–Dec 4 (approx.) Festival Marrakech International Film Festival: North Africa's most important film festival.

  75. 🇿🇦 South Africa

    • Dec 1–Jan 5 (approx.) Festival Cape Town festive lights (Adderley Street): Christmas lights in central Cape Town and events at the V&A Waterfront.
    • Dec 16 Public holiday Day of Reconciliation: Public holiday on 16 December, the start of the summer holiday season.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: Midsummer in South Africa; seaside resorts are at their priciest.

  76. 🇰🇪 Kenya

    • Nov 1–Dec 15 Season Short rains season: Brief showers; safaris still run and prices are lower than in peak season.
    • Dec 12–13 Public holiday Jamhuri Day: National Day on 12 December.
    • Dec 25–27 Public holiday Christmas: Holidays on 25 and 26 December; safari lodges enter their year-end peak.

  77. Oceania

  78. 🇦🇺 Australia

    • Dec 15–Jan 31 (approx.) Festival Tasmanian lavender season: Peak bloom at Bridestowe Lavender Estate.
    • Dec 25–26 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: Midsummer; sales begin on the 26th, and the Sydney to Hobart race sets off that day.
    • Dec 26 Sports Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race: The classic yacht race that leaves Sydney Harbour on Boxing Day.
    • Dec 31 Festival Sydney New Year's Eve fireworks: One of the world's first big New Year's Eve fireworks shows.

  79. 🇳🇿 New Zealand

    • Dec 15–Feb 28 Season Peak summer season: Peak season for South Island hikes and lakes; book accommodation early.
    • Dec 25–28 Public holiday Christmas & Boxing Day: Midsummer, with sales from 26 December.
    • Dec 31 Festival Auckland New Year's Eve (Sky Tower fireworks): New Year's Eve fireworks from the Sky Tower.
