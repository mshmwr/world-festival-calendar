Contact

Found a mistake or want something added? Let me know.

How to reach us

  • GitHub: open an issue on GitHub Issues to report a bug, suggest a feature or a country or event to add. Progress is public.
  • Ko-fi: leave a message on Ko-fi; partnership and press enquiries are welcome there too.

Common requests

  • A date is wrong or an event was cancelled: please include a link to the official announcement.
  • Add a country, festival or experience: send the name, place, approximate dates and official website.
  • Tour operators and tourism boards: listings are free; accurate information and official sources are always welcome.
